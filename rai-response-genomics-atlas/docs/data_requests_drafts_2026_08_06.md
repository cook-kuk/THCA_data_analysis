# Data request drafts — 2026-08-06

Four requests, ordered by expected yield per unit of effort. **Nothing here has been sent.**
Each is deliberately narrow: a specific dataset, a specific question, and a stated
willingness to co-author, because vague "please share your data" requests get ignored.

Common facts to keep consistent across all four (evidence-lock):
- We tested an 8-gene thyroid-differentiation panel (SLC5A5, TPO, TG, TSHR, PAX8, NKX2-1,
  FOXE1, DIO1) against radioiodine outcome in TCGA-THCA (n = 235 RAI-treated).
- Null for the response to the first RAI course (Cohen's d = 0.00, 95% CI -0.45 to +0.46, P = 0.91).
- Associated with a new tumour event after initial treatment (Firth-penalised OR 0.22,
  95% CI 0.06-0.78, P = 0.019; 9 events, so reported with rare-event caveats).
- Null under all five index-course rules, so the null is not an artefact of how repeated
  radioiodine courses were aggregated.
- Cannot separate predictive from prognostic: only 2 events among non-RAI-treated patients.
- The limiting factor is that TCGA is a low-risk cohort in which 86% achieve complete
  response. We need an advanced/metastatic cohort.

## 1. ICGC DACO — EGAS00001001788 (KFSHRC, 158 PTC: 66 RAI-refractory / 92 RAI-avid)

Route: formal application at `daco.icgc-argo.org`, plus a courtesy note to
`daco@icgc-argo.org`. This is the **highest-priority action** because it is a governed
process with a published ~10-business-day review rather than a personal favour.

Requirements to assemble first:
- institutional representative (supervising PI) named and willing to co-sign;
- scientific abstract of intended use (draft below);
- three qualifying publications by the applicant;
- SNU IRB approval letter or a formal exemption determination.

**Scientific abstract draft (≈200 words):**

> Radioiodine is the only systemic therapy given to the majority of patients with
> differentiated thyroid carcinoma, yet no molecular test predicts which tumours will stop
> concentrating iodine. We have shown in TCGA-THCA (n = 235 radioiodine-treated patients)
> that an eight-gene thyroid-differentiation panel is unassociated with the documented best
> response to the first radioiodine course (Cohen's d = 0.00, 95% CI -0.45 to +0.46,
> P = 0.91) but is associated with a new tumour event after initial treatment
> (Firth-penalised OR 0.22, 95% CI 0.06-0.78, P = 0.019). That cohort is limited: 86% of patients
> achieve complete response, leaving little room to discriminate. We request the whole-exome
> and clinical data of EGAD00001003358 to test, in a cohort with a 42% radioiodine-refractory
> fraction, whether differentiation-programme loss distinguishes refractory from avid
> disease, and whether it does so independently of BRAF V600E, TERT promoter status and
> APOBEC SBS13. Analyses will be restricted to the stated hypothesis, tumour cellularity
> will be modelled as a covariate throughout, and no attempt at re-identification will be
> made. We would welcome co-authorship discussion with the KFSHRC investigators.

## 5. Domestic option worth pursuing in parallel — KTCSG

The Korean Thyroid Cancer Study Group (multicentre; Kim Won Bae, Shong Young Kee, Kim Won
Gu and colleagues, Asan Medical Center network) has published multicentre RAI-refractory DTC
cohorts. This is a collaboration approach between Korean peers rather than a data-access
application, and it is the only route on this list where the cohort would be domestic and
the relationship durable. Worth raising with the supervising PI before cold-contacting.

The Korea Biobank Network holds thyroid biospecimens with linked clinical data through the
standard National Biobank of Korea application; whether radioiodine response is a coded
variable has not been confirmed and should be checked before investing in an application.

## Revision note, 2026-08-06 (post-audit)

All effect sizes in these drafts were updated after the statistical audit. The
structural-outcome odds ratios are now Firth-penalised (0.22 and 0.22 rather than the
first-pass maximum-likelihood 0.19 and 0.20), because 9 and 13 events with several
covariates bias ordinary logistic estimates away from the null. The initial-response
figure is now the first-course rule (d = 0.00, P = 0.91) rather than the best-response
rule. Do not send an earlier copy of these drafts.
