# Data request — Boucai et al. Clin Cancer Res 2023

## Target

Boucai L, Saqcena M, Kuo F, et al. **Genomic and Transcriptomic Characteristics of Metastatic Thyroid Cancers with Exceptional Responses to Radioactive Iodine Therapy.** Clin Cancer Res 2023. PMID 36780190 · PMC10106408 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10106408/

## Why we need this data

This is the only public-facing dataset that combines (a) metastatic differentiated thyroid cancer, (b) RECIST v1.1 RAI response endpoint with an "exceptional responder" definition, and (c) tumor expression + WES. It is the only Tier-1 RAI-response anchor we can plausibly access without a prospective trial.

## Verified facts (PMC fetch 2026-05-21)

- **Data availability**: "Data were generated by the authors and available on request" — **no GEO / SRA / dbGaP accession**. Raw data access requires direct author contact.
- **Cohort**: n=8 exceptional responders (ER) vs n=16 non-responders (NR). ER definition: structural tumor volume reduction per RECIST v1.1 after RAI. Matched 1:2 by histology and stage.
- **Signatures used**: 16-gene canonical TDS + 64-gene **enhanced TDS (eTDS)** spanning iodide uptake / organification / thyroid hormone biosynthesis. BRAF-RAS Score (BRS, 71-gene). MAPK output via ERK transcriptional scores + Hallmark GSEA.
- **Supplementary tables present**: S1 (clinical characteristics), S2 (somatic variants by WES + MSK-IMPACT), S4 (**64-gene eTDS components** — directly usable for cross-checking our 8-gene panel against the broader iodide axis).
