3단계 — Spatial niche identification (오늘, S_F5)

28 samples × 3 cohorts spatial:

  • PT (normal): Moran's I ≈ 0 — scattered/absent ✓
  • PTC-LPTC-ATC: Moran's I = 0.27-0.58 — niche-organized ★
  • HT/GD (autoimmune-only): I ≈ 0 — tumor-specific niche ✓
  • → TROP2는 scattered tumor population이 아니라 tumor-side spatial niche
정정된 framing: "TROP2 enrichment in advanced disease" (bulk-level, FAIL) → "TROP2 forms tumor-specific spatial niches in PTC/LPTC/ATC, absent in normal/autoimmune controls" (spatial-level, demonstrated). Bulk가 못 본 이유는 sample-level mean이 niche structure를 평균화시켰기 때문.
Critical caveat: 이 spatial niche finding도 signature-level exploratory이며 (1) niche의 functional vulnerability 입증 아님, (2) clinical risk prediction 아님, (3) ADC target 적합성 결론 아님. Niche가 있다는 사실 자체만 honest report.

📋 Advisor / professor 1-page brief

Q1 — TROP2가 왜 갑자기 튀어나왔는가?

갑자기 나온 게 아닙니다. (i) GSE76039 anchor에서 ATC vs PDTC bulk d = +1.13 신호로 supplement에 등재 (2026-04). (ii) GPL570 4-cohort bulk validation에서 5 contrast 중 1만 expected direction → bulk-level enrichment claim 폐기. (iii) 그러나 spatial transcriptomics (28 samples, 3 cohorts)에서 PTC + LPTC 8/8 sample이 Moran's I 0.27–0.58 → tumor-specific niche structure 확인. PT/HT/GD에서는 ≈ 0. TROP2는 scattered tumor population이 아니라 spatial niche이며 bulk가 평균화시켜 못 본 것.

Q2 — 공간 전사체 왜 필요한가?

6가지 distinct reason: (1) DM1 region heterogeneity 검증 [A], (2) TROP2 scattered vs niche 분리 [E], (3) HT-overlap PTC TLS niche 직접 확인 [C, Moran's I 0.27–0.84] — Paper 2 Pillar II의 TCGA-bulk-only 약점 보강, (4) Driver-orthogonal axis spot 검증 [B], (5) H&E→DM1 closure battery (Paper 2A NO-GO) 자체가 spatial 기반, (6) Autoimmune-only negative control [D]. 6개 모두 bulk-level로 답할 수 없는 질문.

가장 강하게 허용되는 한 문장: "Spatial transcriptomics across 28 samples × 3 cohorts identifies a tumor-specific TROP2 spatial niche (Moran's I 0.27–0.58 in PTC and LPTC, ≈ 0 in normal and autoimmune controls), reframing the bulk-level TROP2 enrichment failure as signal averaging across spatially heterogeneous niches; the same spatial framework directly demonstrates HT-overlap PTC TLS / B-cell / IGHV niche organization (Moran's I 0.27–0.84 in 4/4 GSE230424 slides), strengthening Paper 2 Pillar II beyond TCGA-bulk-only evidence."
