# Review Submission Targets

## Recommendation

Primary route:
1. Nature Reviews Clinical Oncology pre-submission synopsis
2. Nature Cancer Perspective pre-submission enquiry
3. Trends in Cancer review/perspective pitch
4. Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer full review submission

Fast fallback:
1. JITC
2. Frontiers in Oncology Review or Mini Review
3. Briefings in Bioinformatics only if we emphasize benchmark contamination/top-k evaluation rather than a broad computational-method catalogue

## Best Fit

### 1. Nature Reviews Clinical Oncology

Best prestige target for a clinically framed review:
`From neoantigen discovery to vaccine-ready prioritization`

Use if the manuscript emphasizes:
- personalized neoantigen vaccines
- clinical decision bottleneck
- manufacturing/assay top-k constraints
- benchmark contamination as a translational risk
- evaluation contract for vaccine-ready rankers

Risk:
- Nature Reviews is heavily editor-developed and review-type articles are usually scope-shaped by editors.
- It only publishes non-primary content, so CROSS-Neo can be mentioned only as an example or future companion work, not as a new result.

### 2. Nature Cancer Perspective

Best if we sharpen the article into an opinionated but balanced argument:
`Neoantigen prediction needs vaccine-ready benchmarks, not another AUROC leaderboard`

Use if the manuscript emphasizes:
- field-wide benchmark failure
- top-k clinical utility
- source shift and public-overlap risk
- a proposed evaluation contract

Risk:
- Reviews/Perspectives are often invited and need presubmission enquiry.
- Must not be dominated by our own work.

### 3. Trends in Cancer

Best high-impact readable target:
`The new bottleneck in personalized cancer vaccines: ranking the manufacturable neoantigens`

Use if the manuscript is concise, visually strong, and forward-looking.

Risk:
- Less suitable for a dense computational benchmark catalogue.

### 4. JITC

Best practical target:
`A practical evaluation contract for neoantigen vaccine prioritization`

Use if we want high relevance, open access, and a tumor-immunology readership.

Risk:
- Lower prestige than Nature Reviews/Nature Cancer/Trends, but much more straightforward.

### 5. Briefings in Bioinformatics

Only use if the article becomes computational-method heavy.

Risk:
- A 2025 Briefings in Bioinformatics review already covers computational methods and data resources for tumor neoantigen prediction, so our manuscript must be clearly differentiated around benchmarking, contamination, top-k utility, and deployment evaluation.

## Submission Order

1. Send 1-page synopsis to Nature Reviews Clinical Oncology.
2. In parallel prepare Nature Cancer Perspective synopsis.
3. If no positive editorial signal within 2-3 weeks, convert to Trends in Cancer/JITC.
4. If speed matters more than prestige, skip directly to JITC.

## Claim Boundary

- Do not include unpublished CROSS-Neo results as the core evidence in the review.
- CROSS-Neo can be positioned as a planned companion original article.
- Keep public predictors balanced: strong and useful, but benchmark-overlap-sensitive.
- Avoid "external validation" and "quantum advantage" language.
