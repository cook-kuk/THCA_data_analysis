 CONCLUSIONS
The viral vectored personalized vaccine approach was feasible: relevant targets could be identified in all patients, and time of manufacturing and drug release was compatible with the clinical course of treatment. Administration of the vaccine was safe
and induced tumor specific T cell response against multiple targets. Early signs of clinical activity are encouraging with changes of tumor markers observed in a treated patient under vaccine monotherapy.
