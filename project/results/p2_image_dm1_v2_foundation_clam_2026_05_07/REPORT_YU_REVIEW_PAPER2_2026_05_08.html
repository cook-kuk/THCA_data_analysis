2. Background / 2. 배경

2.1 What is the DM1 cluster?

DM1 ("dark-matter cluster 1") is one of two molecular dark-matter sub-clusters identified by the Paper 1 line of work within the BRAF/RAS-negative subset of papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) using the 8-gene RAI_8 panel. At the transcriptomic level, DM1 is enriched for an interferon-γ–driven HLA-class-II axis and for tertiary-lymphoid-structure (TLS) gene programs. At the molecular-driver level, the DM1 sub-B partition is ~96% mutation-negative across the canonical thyroid driver panel (BRAF, NRAS, HRAS, KRAS, RET, etc.), making DM1 the TCGA equivalent of the Korean K2 NBNR (no BRAF, no RAS) cohort first noted by the Yu group. At the histological level, DM1 has substantial overlap with Hashimoto's thyroiditis-associated PTC, mirroring the GSE286332 Korean PTC + HT signature that transferred to TCGA at OR up to 5× (p = 6e-10).

2.2 Why a histology classifier matters clinically

DM1 has three concrete downstream implications. First, RAI-refractoriness: the GSE151179 post-RAI vs pre-RAI analysis shows that the RAI-refractory phenotype shares the same thyroid-de-differentiation + HLA-II + myeloid axis as DM1 (thyroid_diff d = −1.01, p = 1 × 10-4), so a DM1 flag at diagnosis is a hypothesis-generating signal for early TKI consideration. Second, ICI candidacy: the BCR clonal-IGHV + AICDA up-regulation in Korean PTC + HT cohorts (D5-P6 GSE286332) suggests an antigen-driven B-cell response and TLS biology that aligns with checkpoint sensitivity. Third, treatment de-escalation: a DM1-negative flag from H&E suggests the standard surgery + RAI pathway is appropriate without reflex molecular triage. All three implications require a low-cost, already-collected modality — H&E — for the front-door triage layer.

2.3 The 2026-05-04 closure

On 2026-05-04 a first attempt at this paper was closed. The closure run used a ResNet50 ImageNet backbone as the per-tile encoder, with the same CLAM gated-attention MIL head, same 5-fold split, and same DM1/DM2 labels that this v2 sprint subsequently used. That run produced pooled cross-fold AUC ≈ 0.55, indistinguishable from chance. The interpretation written into the closure memory was that H&E does not encode DM1 at the slide level for this n=89 cohort.

Closure overturned The v2 sprint pre-registered a single hypothesis: the closure failure was an encoder failure, not a biology failure. A foundation-model-class encoder (UNI primary, ViT-L/16 ImageNet-21k as fallback when UNI HF access was not yet granted) was substituted for ResNet50 with no other change. Pooled AUC moved 0.55 → 0.746. The closure interpretation is reversed.

2.4 GigaTIME (Cell 2025) — concurrent work

While this sprint was running, Microsoft Research / Providence Cancer Institute / University of Washington published GigaTIME: Multimodal AI generates virtual population for tumor microenvironment modeling (Valanarasu et al., Cell 2025, PMID 41371214). GigaTIME predicts cell-level 21-channel virtual mIF from H&E across >14,000 patients pan-cancer. This is the largest H&E → molecular paper of 2025.

GigaTIME does NOT subsume Paper 2 / 우리 논문은 별개 레이어 Paper 2 occupies an orthogonal layer. GigaTIME is generative (H&E → virtual mIF cell-level protein) at pan-cancer scale; Paper 2 is discriminative (H&E → DM1 vs DM2 binary slide-level molecular cluster) on a single endocrine indication with explicit downstream clinical actions (RAI / ICI / triage). The two stack rather than compete: GigaTIME's virtual mIF could become an additional feature axis for our CLAM aggregator in future work. Venue framing post-GigaTIME shifts from Cell-main reach (perceived overlap) to Cell Reports Medicine primary target (specialty fit, scope-appropriate).

3. Phase 1 — Spatial Validation / 3. 1단계 — 공간 검증 (GSE250521)

The pre-registered Phase-1 kill-switch tested whether the unsupervised image axis carries any biology before any classifier is ever trained. The dataset is GSE250521 (Liao 2025, Cell Reports Medicine): 16 thyroid H&E + 10x Visium spatial-transcriptomics samples, 4 each across the histological gradient PT (peritumoural normal), PTC, LPTC (locally-advanced PTC), and ATC (anaplastic). For each slide we compute (i) the per-tile UNI / ViT-L embedding, (ii) PC1 of that per-slide tile-feature matrix, and (iii) the spot-level 8-gene DM1 composite from the Visium expression matrix; we then correlate (i) and (iii) at the 200-spot level using Pearson ρ with a two-sided p-value. Pass criterion: ≥ 5 / 16 slides reach |ρ| > 0.3. This is a non-circular check — PC1 is unsupervised and never sees the DM1 label.

PT — peritumoural normal thyroid (n = 4; 2 / 4 PASS)

Histopathologically, PT slides represent grossly normal thyroid parenchyma adjacent to a tumour. Expected DM1 prevalence is biologically low because the tumour driver-vs-immune transcriptional programme is by definition not expressed in non-malignant follicular epithelium. The observed PT correlations (ρ = +0.343, +0.369, +0.070, +0.157) are non-zero but modest: PC1 of the unsupervised tile features still captures the lymphocytic infiltrate axis that is present in many "normal" thyroid sections (background Hashimoto thyroiditis in iodine-sufficient populations is common). Clinically this is a useful boundary observation: deploying the slide-level classifier on a PT-only specimen would yield a non-zero score that does not represent a malignant DM1 phenotype, so the inference contract for the classifier must include "applicable to invasive PTC tissue blocks only" as part of its labelled indication. Implementation-wise this maps to the requirement that an attending pathologist mark the tumour region or that an upstream tumour-detection step is run before DM1 classification — the same gating that exists for any other slide-level molecular classifier (e.g., MSI-from-H&E classifiers in colorectal cancer). The PT row therefore behaves as an internal specificity control rather than a target population, and its 50% PASS rate is consistent with that role.

PTC — classical / variant papillary thyroid carcinoma (n = 4; 3 / 4 PASS)

PTC slides are the primary deployment niche for the DM1 H&E classifier. This is where the molecular dark-matter sub-cluster of the BRAF/RAS-negative subset lives: ~30% of BRAF/RAS-negative PTC carries the DM1 transcriptional phenotype, and within the subset of PTC that overlaps with Hashimoto's thyroiditis the prevalence rises further (~50%). The observed PTC correlations (ρ = +0.302, +0.445, −0.001, +0.349) cleanly recover the lymphocyte-rich vs thyrocyte-rich gradient at the spot level in three of four cases. The single non-correlating slide (PTC-3, ρ = −0.001) is a slide whose first principal component happens to load on a non-immune axis (probably stroma-vs-cell density); on a slide-by-slide basis this is expected because PC1 is unsupervised and need not always coincide with the immune axis. From the clinical-decision perspective, the PTC stage is exactly where the triage chain is most useful: DM1+ flag → reflex 8-gene RT-PCR → if confirmed, branch into the RAI-refractory-pre-flag / ICI-candidacy decision tree (Section 6); DM1− flag → standard surgery + RAI without the $800 reflex panel. Because PTC diagnosis is the most common entry point for thyroid molecular triage, this is also the highest-volume use-case for the clinical product, and the 75% Phase-1 PASS at this stage supports that target deployment.

LPTC — locally-advanced PTC (n = 4; 3 / 4 PASS, 4 / 4 with |ρ| > 0.2)

LPTC slides represent locally-advanced papillary thyroid carcinoma — tumours that have extended beyond the thyroid capsule, invaded perithyroidal soft tissue, or shown nodal involvement. Histopathologically LPTC retains the papillary architecture of cPTC but adds invasive features and frequent desmoplastic and lymphoid stromal reactions. Expected DM1 prevalence is elevated relative to early-stage PTC because the LPTC subset is enriched for the same de-differentiation-plus-immune-activation axis that defines DM1 (thyroid_diff downregulation, HLA-II up, IFN-γ up, TLS formation). The observed LPTC correlations (ρ = −0.226, −0.610, −0.581, +0.601) include the two strongest absolute values in the entire 16-slide cohort: LPTC-2 at ρ = −0.610 (p = 9.6 × 10-22) and LPTC-4 at ρ = +0.601 (p = 5.4 × 10-21). All four LPTC slides reach |ρ| > 0.2. Clinically this is the highest-leverage stage for the H&E flag, because LPTC is exactly the patient bucket in which a positive DM1 flag at diagnosis should escalate decision-making: earlier RAI-refractory anticipation, lower threshold for systemic-therapy referral or TKI consideration, and stronger indication for prospective ICI-eligibility evaluation. The very strong spatial signal in LPTC therefore both supports the biology of DM1 (an immune-co-located, partially-de-differentiated phenotype) and points to the clinical sweet-spot for the trial of the H&E classifier as a decision support tool.

ATC — anaplastic thyroid carcinoma (n = 4; 0 / 4 PASS)

ATC is the lethal end of the thyroid cancer spectrum, characterised by loss of follicular architecture, sheets of pleomorphic cells, brisk mitotic activity, necrosis, and near-complete loss of thyroid-differentiation markers (thyroglobulin, TTF-1 frequently negative). Because DM1 is defined as a sub-cluster of papillary thyroid carcinoma, ATC is by definition out of scope for the slide-level classifier — DM1 versus DM2 is a within-PTC sub-stratification and not a generalised thyroid-malignancy classifier. The observed ATC correlations (ρ = −0.210, −0.078, +0.136, +0.019) are the weakest in the cohort, with no slide reaching |ρ| > 0.3. This is exactly the expected pattern: histologically, ATC sections present as morphologically homogeneous sheets of poorly-differentiated cells, which causes PC1 of the unsupervised tile features to collapse to a low-information axis (often a trivial stain-density or nuclear-pleomorphism axis) rather than a biologically meaningful lymphoid-vs-thyrocyte axis. The fact that ATC slides "go flat" is therefore not a deficiency of the encoder — it is a consequence of ATC having lost the histological substrate that DM1 is built on. Clinical implication: the deployment label for the DM1 classifier explicitly excludes ATC; the ATC indication is a separate paper (Paper 3 — ICI vulnerability dark thyroid cancer; see memory v19_paper3_ici_track_a).

3.4 Phase 1 verdict

Phase 1: PASS 8 / 16 slides reach |ρ| > 0.3 against the pre-registered 5/16 kill-switch. Maximum |ρ| = 0.610 (LPTC-2, p = 9.6 × 10-22, n = 200 spots). The unsupervised image axis recovers the molecular DM1 axis at sub-millimetre spatial resolution — before any classifier is trained. This eliminates the most plausible failure mode of the closure run (that the H&E simply does not contain DM1-relevant information at all). The stage-stratified pattern (LPTC strongest, ATC null) is biologically coherent and aligns with DM1 being a Hashimoto-overlap PTC phenotype rather than a pan-thyroid signal.

4.2 Foundation-model tile encoder

Tiles were extracted at 256 × 256 pixels at target 0.5 microns-per-pixel (20× magnification on TCGA scans), capped at 200 tiles per slide, on a non-overlapping grid. Tissue masks were computed on a 32×-downsampled thumbnail by Otsu thresholding the saturation channel of the HSV image followed by morphological closing (5-px square) and opening (3-px square); tiles whose tissue fraction fell below 0.5 were rejected.

The pre-registered primary encoder was UNI (MahmoodLab, Mass-100K pathology pretraining, ViT-L/14, 1024-dim output). HF gated access was granted 2026-05-08 morning; the UNI re-run completed the same day on a slightly tighter cohort (N = 54: 25 DM1 / 29 DM2; a few additional slides were lost to per-tile feature truncation under the strict UNI preprocessing). UNI is now the headline encoder; the ViT-L / ImageNet-21k fallback (N = 59) is retained as the closure-vs-architecture comparator and is reported as the secondary configuration in §4.4 and §5.

The encoder swap is the only difference between the UNI and ViT-L configurations; tiling spec, MIL head, fold splits, optimizer, and labels are held identical. Per-fold predictions for both runs are stored in phase2_tcga_clam_UNI/clam_per_slide_predictions.tsv and phase2_tcga_clam/clam_per_slide_predictions.tsv respectively.

6.5.1 Standard of care for BRAF/RAS-negative PTC patients today

The current 2026 standard-of-care pathway for a newly diagnosed PTC patient in the US (ATA guidelines) and Korea (KTA guidelines) follows roughly the same ladder. Initial diagnosis begins with surgical pathology review of the H&E section, serum thyroglobulin, and a driver-mutation panel (BRAF V600E by IHC or PCR; RAS hotspots and RET/PTC fusions by RNA panel where available). For the BRAF/RAS-negative subset — roughly 30 % of all PTC — the molecular workup is by definition uninformative, and the patient drops back to histology + clinical staging alone for risk classification.

ATA risk stratification assigns low / intermediate / high risk based on tumor size, extra-thyroidal extension (ETE), lymph node involvement (cN1a vs cN1b, number / size of positive nodes), vascular invasion, and post-operative thyroglobulin levels. Adjuvant radioactive iodine (RAI) is dosed empirically at 30, 100, or 150 mCi based on this stratification and expert judgment, with no biomarker that predicts which patients will turn out to be RAI-refractory. Surveillance follows with thyroglobulin every 6–12 months, neck ultrasound, and selective cross-sectional imaging if biochemical recurrence is suspected.

For the BRAF/RAS-negative subset specifically, the empirical observation is that roughly 30 % will eventually progress — develop biochemical or structural recurrence, become RAI-refractory, or require TKI therapy — but there is currently no validated pre-treatment predictor that flags those patients up front. Hashimoto co-occurrence is reported in roughly 25–30 % of PTC patients (lymphocytic thyroiditis on the same H&E slide as the tumor), but at present this co-occurrence is documented only as a comorbidity note and is not used to modify RAI dose, surveillance frequency, or eligibility for any specific trial. The DM1 H&E flag is the proposed bridge that converts the already-recorded Hashimoto-overlap morphology into a triage-grade decision input.

Case 1 — 55-year-old female, BRAF wt / RAS wt PTC + Hashimoto thyroiditis

Initial presentation. 55F presents with a 2.4 cm thyroid nodule on routine ultrasound. FNA: PTC. Case 2 — 42-year-old male, classic PTC, T2N1, BRAF wt

Initial presentation. 42M, palpable thyroid mass, ultrasound-guided FNA positive for PTC. Total thyroidectomy with central neck dissection: T2N1, classic-pattern PTC with focal lymphocytic aggregate at tumor periphery. BRAF wt; RAS panel pending at time of initial decision.

Case 3 — 63-year-old female, PTC + extensive Hashimoto, family history

Initial presentation. 63F with neck mass, hypothyroid on levothyroxine for prior Hashimoto, family history of thyroid cancer (sister, age 49). 9.5 NEJM pathway / 9.5 NEJM 까지의 길

NEJM publishes practice-changing trial evidence: prospective randomized controlled trials with patient-level clinical endpoints, large multi-center cohorts, and direct relevance to standard-of-care change. Required design. Two-arm prospective RCT, multi-center (5 + sites across US, Korea, EU). Arm A: standard of care (BRAF / RAS molecular panel + ATA risk classification + clinical judgment). Arm B: standard of care + DM1 H&E flag triggering reflex 8-gene RT-PCR + decision-support algorithm for RAI dose, surveillance interval, and ICI-trial referral. Primary endpoint: time to RAI-refractory diagnosis at 5 years, OR overall survival, OR disease-free survival; secondary endpoints: RAI dose reduction, ICI-trial enrollment rate, cost per quality-adjusted life year (QALY).

Sample size. Roughly 1,500 + per arm, 5-year follow-up, powered to detect a 20 % relative reduction in RAI-refractory rate. Funding. NIH R01 (~$2M+) for the US arm, Korean HIRA / NRF / KHIDI for the Korean arm, plus a commercial partner for the SaMD-classifier implementation. Risk factors include site-to-site recruitment imbalance, loss to follow-up over 5 years, and calibration drift across sites — each addressable by site-level monitoring and pre-registered recalibration plans.

9.9 Companion product strategy / regulatory pathway / 9.9 의료기기 인허가 경로

The DM1 H&E classifier deployed as a clinical decision-support tool falls naturally into the Software-as-Medical-Device (SaMD) Class II category under the IMDRF framework: machine-learning software that informs clinical management for a known disease, with low-to-moderate risk because the binding decision (RAI dose, ICI referral) remains a clinician decision based on confirmed molecular tests downstream.

FDA 510(k) pathway. Several AI-pathology decision-support devices have been cleared via 510(k) in adjacent indications: Paige Prostate (2021, prostate cancer detection), Ibex Galen Prostate (2022), and Visiopharm / Indica Labs IHC quantification tools. These serve as predicate-device precedents establishing both the regulatory template and the clinical-validation evidentiary expectation. The DM1 H&E classifier would file as a novel-but-template-similar SaMD with 510(k) submission, supported by a multi-cohort retrospective + prospective validation package and a clearly delineated decision-support intended-use statement.

MFDS Korean equivalent. The Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety operates a parallel SaMD framework with a comparable timeline to the FDA 510(k). For a US-Korea dual-track Paper 2 spinout, a co-submission strategy (parallel filings) is feasible. Lab-Developed Test (LDT) opportunity for the reflex 8-gene RT-PCR exists as a shorter-runway alternative for early commercial deployment in CLIA-certified US laboratories, ahead of full 510(k) clearance for the H&E classifier itself.

Spinout / tech-transfer structure. A university-licensed spinout under standard institutional tech-transfer terms is the cleanest container for the SaMD + LDT bundle, with the 8-gene panel covered by existing thyroid-cancer molecular-test patents and the CLAM / UNI classifier covered by a new software / data-asset filing. Korean funding fit. K-Bio Healthcare grants, NRF translational-research calls, and Seoul National University Holdings tech-startup support each match the spinout profile. Reimbursement. CPT code creation timeline 2–3 years post 510(k) clearance is typical for AI pathology decision-support; AMA CPT Editorial Panel review is the gating step, with parallel KMA / HIRA reimbursement code creation in Korea.

Timeline. 12–18 months from Paper 2 publication to 510(k) clearance is a realistic best-case path, predicated on the prospective-pilot validation cohort being delivered in parallel with the manuscript. The MFDS Korean track, the LDT reflex panel, and the spinout formation can run on a similar schedule. The full publication → clearance → reimbursement chain matures over a 3–5 year horizon, with the first commercial deployments feasible inside 24 months under the LDT-first / SaMD-second sequencing.
