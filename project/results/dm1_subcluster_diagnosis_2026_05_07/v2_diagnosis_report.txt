Why 'survival ordering swapped':
  - DM2 has highest OS event rate (6%) despite lowest advanced-stage % (16%)
  - Reason: DM2 patients are older (median age 55.6y) → age dominates OS hazard
  - DM1_subA/subB are younger (median 36y), 0 events in this small cohort
  - not_DM has most advanced-stage (37%) but events spread thin over n=334
  - On OS only, age confounds DM signal entirely.

Why 'stage doesn't dramatically separate':
  - DM2 is 50% Stage II (Hashimoto-like, indolent histology) vs Stage I dominant elsewhere
  - DM1_unassigned + not_DM both ~30% Stage III/IV (similar)
  - The transcriptomic axis (DM) is orthogonal to AJCC stage — not a contradiction;
    AJCC stage measures size/extension at presentation, DM measures dedifferentiation biology.
