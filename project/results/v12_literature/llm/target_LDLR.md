# LDLR — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
LDLR (low-density lipoprotein receptor) mediates cellular uptake of LDL cholesterol and is central to cholesterol homeostasis. The provided abstracts do not address LDLR function in BRAF-like thyroid cancer or any thyroid malignancy context; instead, they focus on cardiovascular disease, familial hypercholesterolemia, and other cancers unrelated to thyroid pathology.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
PCSK9 and ANGPTL3 inhibitors are mentioned in the context of homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia treatment, which indirectly modulates LDLR signaling. No direct LDLR inhibitors, antibodies, ADCs, or repurposed drugs targeting LDLR itself are discussed in these abstracts.

**CLINICAL:**
Clinical evidence is limited to meta-analyses of PCSK9/ANGPTL3 inhibitors in familial hypercholesterolemia. No clinical-trial or FDA data specifically linking LDLR to thyroid cancer treatment or BRAF-like/RAS-like thyroid stratification are present in the provided abstracts.
