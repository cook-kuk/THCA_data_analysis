# Tepotinib (sonnet)

**MECHANISM:** Tepotinib is a highly selective MET kinase inhibitor that targets the hepatocyte growth factor receptor (c-MET). It demonstrates potent activity against MET exon 14 skipping mutations and MET amplifications, blocking downstream signaling pathways involved in tumor cell proliferation, survival, and metastasis.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:** Direct evidence for tepotinib in thyroid cancer appears limited in the provided literature. No specific thyroid cancer studies or case reports were identified in the abstract titles, suggesting this application remains largely unexplored in published research.

**CROSS-CANCER:** Tepotinib is FDA-approved for MET exon 14 skipping mutation-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), with multiple studies demonstrating efficacy in this indication. The drug has shown activity across various tumor types harboring MET alterations, including pulmonary pleomorphic carcinoma, supporting its broader oncological utility.

**REPURPOSING RATIONALE:** MET alterations occur in thyroid cancers, particularly aggressive subtypes, providing biological rationale for tepotinib repurposing given its proven efficacy in MET-driven malignancies. However, the absence of thyroid-specific clinical data and the availability of established targeted therapies for BRAF-mutant thyroid cancer (like vemurafenib) may limit immediate clinical adoption without supportive evidence.
