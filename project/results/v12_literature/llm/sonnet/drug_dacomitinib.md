# Dacomitinib (sonnet)

**MECHANISM:** Dacomitinib is an irreversible pan-HER tyrosine kinase inhibitor that covalently binds to EGFR, HER2, and HER4 through formation of covalent bonds with cysteine residues in their ATP-binding sites. This pan-HER inhibition blocks downstream signaling pathways including PI3K/AKT and RAS/RAF/MEK that drive cellular proliferation and survival.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:** The provided abstracts contain no specific evidence for dacomitinib efficacy in thyroid cancer. One computational study examined dacomitinib-DNA binding interactions but this represents basic mechanistic research rather than clinical thyroid cancer evidence.

**CROSS-CANCER:** Dacomitinib is FDA-approved as first-line therapy for advanced EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer, demonstrating superior progression-free survival compared to gefitinib. The drug has shown activity across multiple EGFR-driven malignancies, though resistance mechanisms including ERBB4 mutations have been identified that may limit its broader applicability.

**REPURPOSING RATIONALE:** The strongest argument for thyroid use is that some thyroid cancers exhibit EGFR overexpression and pan-HER inhibition could target multiple growth factor pathways simultaneously. However, the strongest counter-argument is the lack of specific clinical evidence in thyroid cancer and the fact that BRAF mutations, not EGFR mutations, are the primary oncogenic driver in most aggressive thyroid cancers.
