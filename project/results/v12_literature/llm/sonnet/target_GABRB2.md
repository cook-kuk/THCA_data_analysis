# GABRB2 (sonnet)

**BIOLOGY:** GABRB2 encodes the β2 subunit of GABAA receptors, which mediate inhibitory neurotransmission through chloride ion channels. In thyroid cancer context, GABRB2 appears to function as a tumor suppressor, with miR-144-3p-mediated downregulation promoting thyroid cancer progression in vitro. The gene's role in BRAF-like versus RAS-like thyroid cancer subtypes and specific MAPK pathway interactions remains poorly characterized.

**DRUGGABILITY:** Limited druggability evidence exists for GABRB2 in cancer contexts. GABAA receptor modulators like benzodiazepines and barbiturates are well-established drug classes, but their application to thyroid cancer lacks development. The PDE4 inhibitor apremilast shows interaction with GABAA receptor signaling pathways, suggesting potential for repurposing phosphodiesterase inhibitors, though thyroid cancer-specific efficacy remains unexplored.

**CLINICAL:** No clinical trial evidence exists for GABRB2-targeted therapies in thyroid cancer specifically. Current clinical applications of GABAA receptor modulators are limited to neurological and psychiatric disorders, with no established oncological indications.

**RESEARCH GAP:** The most useful next experiment would be functional validation of GABRB2's tumor suppressor role across BRAF-like and RAS-like thyroid cancer subtypes, coupled with screening of existing GABAA receptor modulators for anti-thyroid cancer activity in relevant preclinical models.
