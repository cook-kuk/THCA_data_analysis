# Sacituzumab govitecan (sonnet)

**MECHANISM:** Sacituzumab govitecan is an antibody-drug conjugate targeting TROP2 (trophoblast cell surface antigen 2), a transmembrane glycoprotein overexpressed in many solid tumors. The antibody delivers SN-38, the active metabolite of irinotecan, directly to TROP2-expressing cancer cells, enabling targeted topoisomerase I inhibition and DNA damage.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:** The provided abstract titles contain no specific evidence for sacituzumab govitecan in thyroid cancer treatment. Literature on this drug's activity in thyroid malignancies appears to be sparse or absent in current publications.

**CROSS-CANCER:** Sacituzumab govitecan is FDA-approved for metastatic triple-negative breast cancer and has shown activity in other solid tumors including cervical cancer as evidenced by the clinical case reports. The drug represents a significant advancement in antibody-drug conjugate therapy for cancers expressing TROP2, though toxicity management including neutropenia remains a clinical challenge.

**REPURPOSING RATIONALE:** The strongest argument for thyroid cancer application is that TROP2 is commonly overexpressed in various solid tumors, potentially including thyroid cancers, making it a viable target for this established ADC platform. However, the absence of preclinical or clinical data specifically in thyroid cancer represents a significant limitation, and the drug's substantial toxicity profile may outweigh benefits without demonstrated efficacy in this indication.
