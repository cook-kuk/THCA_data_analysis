# LDLR (sonnet)

**BIOLOGY:** LDLR encodes the low-density lipoprotein receptor, which mediates cellular cholesterol uptake through endocytosis of LDL particles. In thyroid cancer context, LDLR expression may influence MAPK pathway activity indirectly through cholesterol metabolism's effects on membrane composition and signaling platform organization, though direct mechanistic connections to BRAF-like vs RAS-like classification remain poorly characterized.

**DRUGGABILITY:** PCSK9 inhibitors (evolocumab, alirocumab) represent the primary therapeutic approach targeting LDLR pathway enhancement and are FDA-approved for hypercholesterolemia. Recent studies identify peptide inhibitors disrupting PCSK9-LDLR interactions and explore adeno-associated virus-mediated LDLR gene therapy approaches. Novel small molecules targeting cholesterol metabolism pathways are in preclinical development.

**CLINICAL:** No specific clinical trial evidence exists for LDLR-targeted therapies in thyroid cancer treatment. Current clinical applications focus primarily on cardiovascular disease management through cholesterol regulation.

**RESEARCH GAP:** The most useful next experiment would be comprehensive analysis of LDLR expression patterns across BRAF-like versus RAS-like thyroid cancer subtypes, coupled with functional studies examining whether cholesterol metabolism modulation affects MAPK signaling dynamics and therapeutic sensitivity in these distinct molecular contexts.
