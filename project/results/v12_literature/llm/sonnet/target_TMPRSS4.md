# TMPRSS4 (sonnet)

**BIOLOGY:** TMPRSS4 (transmembrane protease serine 4) is a cell surface serine protease that promotes epithelial-mesenchymal transition and tumor progression through multiple signaling pathways. In cancer contexts, TMPRSS4 activates AKT/ERK/p38/β-catenin signaling while inhibiting E-cadherin/GSK3β pathways, facilitating metastasis and proliferation. The protease appears to function downstream of transcription factors like TEAD4 and integrates with MAPK-related signaling networks relevant to aggressive tumor phenotypes.

**DRUGGABILITY:** Recent computational studies have identified potent TMPRSS4 inhibitors through structural modeling and molecular dynamics simulations, though specific compounds and development stages are not detailed in available abstracts. The enzyme represents a targetable cell surface protease, making it potentially amenable to small molecule inhibitors or targeted antibody approaches. Natural compounds like honokiol have demonstrated activity against TMPRSS4-mediated signaling pathways, suggesting possible repurposing opportunities for serine protease inhibitor drug classes.

**CLINICAL:** No specific clinical trial evidence for TMPRSS4-targeted therapies in thyroid cancer was identified in the available literature. While TMPRSS4 appears in molecular testing panels for predicting aggressive clinical course in papillary thyroid carcinoma, therapeutic targeting remains preclinical.

**RESEARCH GAP:** The most useful next experiment would be functional validation of TMPRSS4's role in BRAF-like versus RAS-like thyroid cancer subtypes, particularly investigating whether TMPRSS4 expression correlates with specific molecular classifications and could serve as a stratification biomarker for targeted therapy selection.
