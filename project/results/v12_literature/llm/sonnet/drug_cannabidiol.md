# Cannabidiol (sonnet)

**MECHANISM:** Cannabidiol (CBD) demonstrates anticancer activity through multiple molecular pathways, including induction of heme oxygenase-1-dependent cell death, mitochondrial dysfunction, and inhibition of extracellular vesicle secretion. CBD appears to modulate various cell signaling molecules and may interact with TRPV2 channels, though its precise molecular targets in cancer remain under investigation.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:** The provided abstracts contain no specific literature on cannabidiol's effects in thyroid cancer models or clinical studies. Current evidence for CBD in thyroid malignancies appears to be extremely limited or absent in the published literature.

**CROSS-CANCER:** CBD shows promising preclinical activity across multiple cancer types, including breast, colorectal, melanoma, and oral squamous cell carcinomas, often demonstrating enhanced efficacy when combined with conventional therapies like 5-fluorouracil. However, CBD currently lacks FDA approval for any oncology indication, remaining an investigational compound despite extensive preclinical research.

**REPURPOSING RATIONALE:** The strongest argument for thyroid cancer repurposing lies in CBD's demonstrated multi-target anticancer mechanisms and favorable safety profile, potentially offering a well-tolerated adjuvant therapy. The strongest counter-argument is the complete absence of thyroid-specific evidence and uncertain relevance of mechanisms observed in other cancer types to thyroid carcinoma biology.
