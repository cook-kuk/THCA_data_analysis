# CYP1B1 (sonnet)

BIOLOGY: CYP1B1 encodes cytochrome P450 1B1, a phase I metabolic enzyme involved in xenobiotic metabolism and steroid hormone biosynthesis. In BRAF-like thyroid cancers, CYP1B1 may contribute to altered metabolic reprogramming and hormone-dependent signaling pathways that characterize papillary thyroid carcinomas, though its specific role in MAPK pathway modulation requires further investigation.

DRUGGABILITY: Several classes of CYP1B1 inhibitors have been developed, including 2-phenylethyl chromones and novel selective small molecule inhibitors currently in preclinical stages. Natural compounds like paeoniflorin demonstrate mixed-type CYP1B1 inhibition with favorable drug-likeness properties. The aryl hydrocarbon receptor agonist ZSTK3744 shows promise in chemotherapy-resistant cancers, suggesting potential for CYP1B1-targeted therapeutic approaches.

CLINICAL: No specific clinical trial evidence for CYP1B1 targeting in thyroid cancer is available in the current literature. The MetBreCS trial examining metformin effects on transcriptomic profiles in breast cancer survivors provides indirect evidence for metabolic enzyme modulation in oncology, but thyroid-specific clinical validation remains absent.

RESEARCH GAP: The most useful next experiment would be functional validation of CYP1B1 expression patterns in BRAF-like versus RAS-like thyroid cancer subtypes, coupled with assessment of selective CYP1B1 inhibitor efficacy in relevant thyroid cancer cell line models.
