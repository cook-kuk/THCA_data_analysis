# Tesevatinib (sonnet)

**MECHANISM:** Tesevatinib is a multi-kinase inhibitor that targets several receptor tyrosine kinases including EGFR, HER2, and VEGFR family members. The drug demonstrates activity against both wild-type and mutant forms of these kinases, potentially disrupting multiple oncogenic signaling pathways simultaneously.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:** Limited published evidence exists specifically examining tesevatinib in thyroid cancer models or clinical settings. The absence of recent abstracts suggests minimal active research focus on this indication for tesevatinib.

**CROSS-CANCER:** Tesevatinib has shown preclinical activity across various solid tumor models, particularly those driven by EGFR or HER2 overexpression. However, the drug has not achieved regulatory approval for any cancer indication, with clinical development appearing to have stalled in early-phase trials.

**REPURPOSING RATIONALE:** The multi-kinase inhibition profile could theoretically address the complex signaling networks active in BRAF-like thyroid cancers, potentially overcoming single-pathway resistance mechanisms. However, the lack of thyroid-specific data and apparent discontinuation of clinical development raises significant concerns about both efficacy and safety profiles that would need extensive validation before consideration for thyroid cancer treatment.
