# TACSTD2 (sonnet)

**BIOLOGY:** TACSTD2 encodes TROP2 (Trophoblast cell-surface antigen 2), a transmembrane glycoprotein that regulates cell proliferation, migration, and epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition. In thyroid cancer context, TROP2 expression patterns and mechanistic connections to BRAF-like versus RAS-like molecular subtypes or MAPK pathway activation remain poorly characterized in the available literature.

**DRUGGABILITY:** Sacituzumab govitecan, an anti-TROP2 antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) carrying SN-38 payload, is FDA-approved for triple-negative breast cancer and has shown therapeutic efficacy in multiple solid tumor types including germ cell tumors. This ADC represents the most clinically advanced TROP2-targeting therapeutic, with additional anti-TROP2 ADCs in various development stages across oncology indications.

**CLINICAL:** No specific clinical trial evidence for TROP2-targeted therapies in thyroid cancer was identified in the reviewed abstracts. Current clinical experience with sacituzumab govitecan and other TROP2-targeting agents has focused primarily on breast, lung, urothelial, and other solid tumor types.

**RESEARCH GAP:** The most valuable next experiment would be comprehensive immunohistochemical analysis of TROP2 expression across BRAF-like and RAS-like thyroid cancer molecular subtypes, coupled with functional studies examining TROP2's role in thyroid cancer cell migration and therapeutic resistance mechanisms.
