# PLEKHA6 (sonnet)

BIOLOGY: PLEKHA6 encodes a pleckstrin homology domain-containing protein that modulates β-catenin signaling pathways. Recent evidence demonstrates its role as a regulator of Wnt/β-catenin signaling in lung adenocarcinoma, suggesting potential involvement in MAPK-independent oncogenic pathways that could be relevant to RAS-like thyroid cancer classification.

DRUGGABILITY: Limited druggability evidence exists for PLEKHA6 as a direct target. The identification of PLEKHA6-NTRK3 fusion in Langerhans cell histiocytosis suggests potential targetability through NTRK inhibitors such as larotrectinib or entrectinib, which are FDA-approved pan-TRK inhibitors. However, no specific small molecule modulators of wild-type PLEKHA6 have been reported in clinical development.

CLINICAL: No clinical trial evidence exists for PLEKHA6-targeted therapies specifically in thyroid cancer. While NTRK fusions have been identified in thyroid cancers and are targetable with approved TRK inhibitors, PLEKHA6-NTRK3 fusions have not been reported in thyroid malignancies.

RESEARCH GAP: The most useful next experiment would be comprehensive genomic screening of BRAF-like versus RAS-like thyroid cancer cohorts to determine PLEKHA6 expression patterns and identify potential fusion partners. Additionally, functional studies examining PLEKHA6's role in thyroid-specific MAPK pathway regulation would clarify its utility as a classification biomarker.
