# CYP1B1 — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
CYP1B1 is a cytochrome P450 enzyme involved in xenobiotic and endogenous metabolism, particularly estrogen catabolism and aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) signaling. In gastric cancer, H3K27 acetylation-activated CYP1B1 promotes invasion, migration, and radioresistance via p38 MAPK-mediated fatty acid metabolism. Limited direct evidence in BRAF-like thyroid cancer was identified in these abstracts.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
ZSTK3744, an aryl hydrocarbon receptor agonist, shows efficacy in chemotherapy-resistant triple-negative breast cancer and may modulate CYP1B1 activity. Direct CYP1B1 inhibitors or antibodies targeting this enzyme are not reported in the provided abstracts; repurposing of AhR-modulating compounds represents a potential approach.

**CLINICAL:**
Clinical evidence is sparse. The MetBreCS trial examined metformin in breast cancer survivors with transcriptomic profiling but did not focus on CYP1B1. No FDA-approved drugs specifically targeting CYP1B1 or completed Phase II/III trials for thyroid cancer were identified in these abstracts.
