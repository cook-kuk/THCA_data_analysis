# Cannabidiol — LLM evidence synthesis

**MECHANISM:**
Cannabidiol (CBD) induces mitochondrial dysfunction and triggers heme oxygenase-1-dependent cell death pathways in cancer cells. It also modulates multiple cell signaling molecules implicated in cancer progression, though a single dominant molecular target for BRAF-like disease remains unspecified in the supplied abstracts.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:**
The provided abstracts contain no direct evidence of CBD activity in thyroid cancer or BRAF-mutant thyroid disease. Thyroid-specific studies are absent from this abstract set.

**CROSS-CANCER:**
CBD demonstrates activity across melanoma, squamous cell carcinoma, colorectal, and breast cancers through mitochondrial stress and cell-death mechanisms. However, CBD lacks FDA approval for oncology; current clinical use is limited to seizure disorders, and anticancer evidence remains largely preclinical.
