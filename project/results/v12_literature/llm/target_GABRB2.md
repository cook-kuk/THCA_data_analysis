# GABRB2 — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
GABRB2 encodes the beta-2 subunit of the GABAA receptor, a ligand-gated ion channel involved in inhibitory neurotransmission. In thyroid cancer, GABRB2 expression appears dysregulated—miR-144-3p suppresses thyroid cancer progression by targeting GABRB2, and methylation-driven downregulation of GABRB2 distinguishes oral carcinoma subtypes, suggesting epigenetic silencing may promote tumorigenesis.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
No inhibitors, antibodies, or ADCs targeting GABRB2 are described in these abstracts. GABAA receptor modulators exist (benzodiazepines, barbiturates) but their specificity for GABRB2 and anti-cancer potential remain unstudied here. Evidence for druggability in thyroid cancer is absent.

**CLINICAL:**
No clinical trials or FDA approvals for GABRB2-targeted therapy in thyroid cancer are mentioned. The prognostic association of GABAA receptor genes with survival in brain tumors (medulloblastoma) suggests potential relevance, but thyroid-specific clinical data are lacking.
