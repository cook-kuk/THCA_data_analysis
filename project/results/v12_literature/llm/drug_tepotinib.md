# Tepotinib — LLM evidence synthesis

MECHANISM:
Tepotinib is a selective MET tyrosine kinase inhibitor that targets hepatocyte growth factor receptor signaling. It has high potency against MET exon 14-skipping mutations, the primary driver of its clinical activity in MET-altered cancers.

THYROID EVIDENCE:
Direct thyroid cancer data for tepotinib are sparse in the provided literature. MET alterations occur in thyroid cancer, but specific efficacy or clinical trial evidence in BRAF-like thyroid cancers is not evident from these abstracts.

CROSS-CANCER:
Tepotinib is approved for MET exon 14-mutated non-small-cell lung cancer, with established efficacy and safety in this indication. Broader kinase profiling suggests potential utility across mutation-specific contexts, though repurposing to BRAF-driven thyroid cancer would require validation of MET dependency.
