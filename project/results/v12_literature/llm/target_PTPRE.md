# PTPRE — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
PTPRE is a receptor protein tyrosine phosphatase that regulates phosphorylation-dependent signaling. In thyroid carcinoma, PTPRE promotes an oncogenic phenotype by activating AKT and ERK1/2 signaling pathways, suggesting a role in BRAF-like and RAS-like thyroid cancer progression and metastasis.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
PTPRE inhibition has been shown to suppress tumor progression and improve sorafenib response in hepatocellular carcinoma models. Specific PTPRE-targeting inhibitors or antibodies have not been identified in the provided abstracts; repurposing of kinase inhibitors with PTPRE activity remains unexplored.

**CLINICAL:**
No FDA-approved or clinical-trial data for PTPRE-directed therapeutics in thyroid cancer is reported in these abstracts. Evidence remains preclinical and limited to in vitro or animal model studies.
