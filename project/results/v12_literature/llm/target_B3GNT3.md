# B3GNT3 — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
B3GNT3 is a glycosyltransferase involved in protein glycosylation and carbohydrate metabolism. Recent evidence suggests it facilitates non-canonical NF-κB activation through NFKB2 processing in lung adenocarcinoma, and interacts with FUT3 to promote pancreatic cancer progression via NF-κB-mediated autophagy. Its expression correlates with glycolysis, tumor progression, and lymph-node metastasis across multiple cancer types.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
No specific inhibitors, antibodies, or ADCs targeting B3GNT3 are described in the provided abstracts. Potential approaches might leverage its role in NF-κB signaling, but direct druggability evidence is lacking.

**CLINICAL:**
B3GNT3 has been identified as a prognostic biomarker in pan-cancer analysis and associated with cisplatin resistance in lung adenocarcinoma; however, no clinical-trial data or FDA-approved therapeutics targeting B3GNT3 are mentioned. Clinical translation remains at the biomarker-discovery stage.
