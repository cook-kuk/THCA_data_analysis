# Sacituzumab govitecan — LLM evidence synthesis

MECHANISM: Sacituzumab govitecan is an antibody-drug conjugate targeting Trop-2 that delivers the topoisomerase I inhibitor SN-38 to cancer cells, triggering DNA damage and cell death.

THYROID EVIDENCE: No direct evidence for sacituzumab govitecan in BRAF-mutant or other thyroid cancer subtypes was identified in the supplied abstracts. Thyroid-specific clinical data appears sparse or absent.

CROSS-CANCER: Sacituzumab govitecan is FDA-approved for triple-negative breast cancer and shows activity in recurrent/metastatic cervical cancer. Toxicity includes neutropenia and nausea; efficacy in hormone receptor-positive breast cancer appears limited, raising questions about its broader applicability beyond Trop-2–high tumors.
