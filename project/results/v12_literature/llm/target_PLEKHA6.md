# PLEKHA6 — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
PLEKHA6 (pleckstrin homology domain-containing protein 6) is a PH-domain protein implicated in cell signaling pathways. Recent multi-omics work identifies PLEKHA6 as a modulator of β-catenin signaling in lung adenocarcinoma, suggesting a role in Wnt pathway regulation. Direct evidence in BRAF-mutant thyroid cancer is absent from these abstracts.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
A PLEKHA6-NTRK3 fusion has been reported in Langerhans cell histiocytosis, indicating potential NTRK inhibitor sensitivity in fusion-positive contexts. No small-molecule inhibitors, antibodies, or ADCs specifically targeting PLEKHA6 itself are mentioned in the provided abstracts.

**CLINICAL:**
No clinical trials, FDA approvals, or clinical-stage development of PLEKHA6-directed therapeutics are documented in these abstracts. Evidence remains preclinical and largely limited to lung adenocarcinoma and rare fusion-driven malignancies.
