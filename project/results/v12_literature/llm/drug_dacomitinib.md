# Dacomitinib — LLM evidence synthesis

MECHANISM:
Dacomitinib is an irreversible pan-HER tyrosine kinase inhibitor that targets EGFR, HER2, and HER4. It covalently binds the ATP-binding pocket of these receptor tyrosine kinases, blocking downstream signaling cascades essential for cancer cell proliferation.

THYROID EVIDENCE:
Direct evidence for dacomitinib in thyroid cancer is sparse in the supplied abstracts. One study examines dacomitinib-DNA binding interactions using spectroscopy and computational methods, but does not demonstrate thyroid-specific efficacy or BRAF-mutant activity.

CROSS-CANCER:
Dacomitinib is established as first-line therapy for EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer and is approved in this indication. Its pan-HER activity offers broader coverage than selective EGFR inhibitors, though the abstracts do not address BRAF-like thyroid malignancies specifically.
