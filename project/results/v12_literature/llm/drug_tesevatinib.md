# Tesevatinib Evaluation

**MECHANISM:**
Tesevatinib is a multi-targeted tyrosine kinase inhibitor that potently inhibits EGFR and HER2, with activity against additional receptor tyrosine kinases. It may have relevance to BRAF-like thyroid cancers through off-target effects on downstream signaling pathways, though direct BRAF inhibition is not a primary mechanism.

**THYROID EVIDENCE:**
No recent PubMed abstracts on tesevatinib in thyroid cancer were retrieved. Direct clinical or preclinical evidence for this agent in BRAF-mutant or BRAF-like thyroid malignancies is not available in the current literature search.

**CROSS-CANCER:**
Tesevatinib has been evaluated in solid tumors with EGFR/HER2 alterations and has shown activity in gastric and other adenocarcinomas. However, no FDA approval specifically for thyroid cancer or BRAF-driven malignancies has been established.
