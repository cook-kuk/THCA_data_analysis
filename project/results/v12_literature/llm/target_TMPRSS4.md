# TMPRSS4 — LLM evidence synthesis

**BIOLOGY:**
TMPRSS4 is a transmembrane serine protease involved in cell surface proteolysis and signaling regulation. It has been identified as a prognostic biomarker in multiple cancer types including gastric and pancreatic cancers, and recent evidence suggests TEAD4 regulates TMPRSS4 expression to promote gallbladder cancer proliferation and metastasis, implicating it in oncogenic signaling pathways. Specific functional roles in BRAF-like thyroid cancer remain unclear from the available abstracts.

**DRUGGABILITY:**
Recent structural modeling and molecular dynamics simulations have identified potent TMPRSS4 inhibitors, indicating the protease domain is tractable for small-molecule inhibition. No antibody, ADC, or repurposed drug approaches are mentioned in the available literature.

**CLINICAL:**
TMPRSS4 serves as a prognostic biomarker in multicenter retrospective studies post-gastric cancer surgery. However, no clinical trials, FDA approvals, or clinical evidence specifically for TMPRSS4-targeted therapy are documented in these abstracts.
