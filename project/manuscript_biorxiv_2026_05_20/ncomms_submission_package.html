Dear Editor,

[AUTHOR DRAFT REQUIRED · voice-protected]
Suggested opening (1 paragraph): position the paper as a translational discovery that closes a long-standing gap in thyroid cancer molecular stratification, with explicit cross-ethnic validation and an immediate druggable hook. Author should re-voice freely; the anchor facts below are the binding constraint, not the wording. Lead with what is new rather than what is incremental.

We are pleased to submit our manuscript "Cross-ethnic 8-gene RAI-lineage panel stratifies fusion-driven epigenetically silenced thyroid cancer and identifies MAPK-inhibitor-sensitive subgroups" for consideration as an Article in Nature Communications.

Discovery in brief. Approximately 23% of papillary thyroid carcinomas lack BRAF or RAS driver mutations and constitute a clinically heterogeneous "dark matter" compartment that remains mechanistically unguided in current radioiodine treatment decisions. We define a 3-tier transcriptomic panel architecture — TF₃ (FOXE1/NKX2-1/PAX8) as a cross-ethnic identity anchor, RAI₈ (TF₃ plus the canonical RAI uptake effectors DIO1, SLC5A5/NIS, TG, TPO, TSHR) as the biology-complete deployment readout, and NONOVERLAP₈ (SLC26A4, IYD, DUOX1/2, TFF3, HHEX, GLIS3, DIO2) as a zero-overlap defense layer — and demonstrate that this architecture stratifies the BRAF/RAS-negative compartment into DM1 (silenced) and DM2 (differentiated) subgroups with reproducible biology across 10 distinct cohorts.

Why Nature Communications. Three features make this work appropriate for a broad readership: (i) cross-ethnic generalizability under fully within-cohort unsupervised analysis, with 13 entries across Western and Korean primary, advanced, single-cell, protein, and microarray cohorts (mean Cohen's d = 2.91, all direction-consistent); (ii) multi-layer mechanism connecting fusion enrichment (76.8% of DM1 are RET/NTRK/ALK/BRAF-fusion-positive, OR 7.41 vs DM2), promoter hypermethylation (TPO Cohen's d = 2.99, fusion-independent), and CRISPR essentiality (MYC d = −0.50, NAMPT d = −0.45 in DM1-high cell lines); (iii) direct druggability hook — PRISM drug screen identifies 7-of-11 DM1-high-selective drugs as MAPK pathway inhibitors at FDR < 0.05 with AZD-0364 (MEK) leading (Cohen's d = −0.59, FDR = 5.9 × 10⁻⁷), consistent with the published thyroid-cancer MEK-inhibitor clinical literature (Ho et al., NEJM 2013 selumetinib RAI re-induction; 2025 ATA cobimetinib/dabrafenib guidelines; dabrafenib + trametinib FDA-approved for BRAFV600E ATC).

Honest disclosures. We pre-specify and disclose three limitations transparently: the primary-PTC DM1 progression-free interval Cox PH is underpowered in TCGA-THCA (HR 0.78 [0.18, 3.48], p = 0.74, 27 events; the pooled OS signal HR 2.53 is driven by MSK-IMPACT advanced disease); HM450 methylation is currently TCGA only; the prospective Korean Bundang validation cohort is at the institutional outreach stage. The clinical prognostic claim for primary PTC is therefore explicitly reserved for prospective follow-up; what we offer is a robust molecular sub-stratification framework with druggable biology, not a validated clinical predictor.

Anticipated reviewers. Suggested expert reviewers in thyroid molecular pathology, cross-ethnic cancer genomics, epigenetic silencing mechanisms, and MEK-inhibitor pharmacology in thyroid cancer are provided in the suggested-reviewer field of the submission portal.

Publication preference. We confirm exclusive submission to Nature Communications; this manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. A companion bioRxiv preprint (deposited 2026-05-21) is available at [bioRxiv DOI placeholder] for community review during peer review.

We thank you for considering our submission.

Yours sincerely,
Seungho Cook (국승호), Ph.D. candidate
& Yu Hyeong-won, M.D., Ph.D. (corresponding author)
Seoul National University Bundang Hospital
