# Supplementary Data Bundle — Paper 1 Cook & Yu 2026
## Cross-ethnic 8-gene RAI-lineage panel — NComms submission

Contents (15 files):
- 01_TCGA_d4p2_clin_mut.tsv : TCGA-THCA reference DM1/DM2 partition + clinical + driver mutations (n=500)
- 02_Lee_2024_unsup.tsv : Lee 2024 GSE213647 all-cohort within-cohort unsup labels (n=632, d=5.93)
- 03_Lee_2024_tumour_unsup.tsv : Lee 2024 tumour-only subset unsup (n=370, d=5.48)
- 04_Mun_2025_protein_unsup.tsv : Mun 2025 protein within-cohort unsup labels (n=336, d=2.86)
- 05_per_gene_contribution.tsv : Per-gene Cohen's d across 4 cohorts × 10 contrasts (77 cells)
- 06_effect_sizes_master.tsv : 12-contrast master effect size table (TCGA + GSE286332 + Lee + Mun)
- 07_DepMap_DM1_dependencies.tsv : DepMap DM1-state stratified gene essentiality (n=372 vs 394)
- 08_DepMap_thyroid_lineage.tsv : DepMap thyroid lineage-specific subset (n=11 cell lines)
- 09_PRISM_top15_drugs.tsv : PRISM drug screen top 15 DM1-high selective drugs (FDR<0.05)
- 10_per_gene_x_cohort_matrix.tsv : Per-gene × per-cohort Cohen's d matrix (8 genes × 10 contrasts pivot)
- 11_Lu2023_sc_panel_score.tsv : Lu 2023 GSE193581 single-cell DM_score per cell (n=15,330 thyrocytes)
- 12_GSE286332_panel_scores.tsv : GSE286332 g8_RAI scores per sample (n=18, Korean PTC vs PTC+HT)
- 13_DepMap_lineage_essentiality.tsv : DepMap lineage-stratified essentiality (16 panel genes × 24 lineages)
