1.2 Panel definitions and provenance

The 8-gene RAI-lineage panel (RAI₈: SLC5A5/NIS, TPO, TG, TSHR, PAX8, NKX2-1, FOXE1, DIO1) was derived by RandomForest feature-importance ranking (sklearn 1.5, default 100 trees, gini criterion) on a 55-gene TIERA67-clean candidate pool (TIERA67 67-gene curated framework with Driver_anchor 12-gene category removed; documented in project/notebooks_or_scripts/rerun_v2.py:121-200) using TCGA-THCA RNA-seq z-scores against the d4p2 reference DM1/DM2 labels. The 3-gene TF₃ subset (FOXE1, NKX2-1, PAX8) corresponds to the developmental master TF triad (Damante & Di Lauro series); the 8-gene zero-overlap NONOVERLAP₈ (SLC26A4, IYD, DUOX1, DUOX2, TFF3, HHEX, GLIS3, DIO2) was hand-curated from the TIERA67 candidate pool sharing zero overlap with RAI₈.

1.3 Within-cohort z-score panel scoring

For each cohort C and gene g in a panel P, the within-cohort z-score is zC,g,i = (xC,g,i − meanC(xC,g)) / sdC(xC,g) where x is log2(TPM+1) for RNA-seq cohorts and platform-normalised log2 intensity for microarrays. The panel score for sample i is the arithmetic mean across panel genes: panel_zi = meang∈P(zC,g,i). The within-cohort normalisation step removes ethnicity-correlated mean shifts and platform bias to first order; we explicitly verify the same axis emerges under cohort-pooled ComBat batch correction (Johnson et al., Biostatistics 2007) for sensitivity.

1.4 Within-cohort unsupervised k=2 KMeans clustering

For each cohort, sample-level panel z-scores (8-dimensional for the full 8-gene panel, or per-gene z values stacked) were clustered with sklearn KMeans (n_clusters=2, n_init=10, random_state=42). The cluster with the lower mean panel_z was assigned as DM1 (silenced lineage) by biological convention. Cohen's d between clusters was computed as |mean(DM1) − mean(DM2)| / pooled_SD where pooled_SD uses the unbiased two-sample pooled formula. This procedure is fully unsupervised within each cohort and does not use any TCGA-derived label transfer.

1.5 Supervised classification — LogReg, RBF-SVM, quantum kernel SVM

Logistic regression: sklearn 1.5 LogisticRegression with L2 penalty and default regularisation strength C=1.0; stratified 5-fold cross-validation; reported AUC is mean across folds. RBF-SVM: sklearn SVC with kernel="rbf", gamma="scale", C=1.0; same stratified 5-fold CV protocol. Quantum kernel SVM: PennyLane 0.44.0 with default.qubit backend; Havlíček 2019 ZZ-feature-map (Nature 567:209) with reps=2 and one qubit per panel feature (3-8 qubits depending on panel size); n=80 stratified subsample due to circuit cost; quantum kernel matrix computed via inner-product of feature-mapped states.

1.6 Meta-analysis methods

DerSimonian-Laird random-effects model (REM): inverse-variance random-effects pooling of cohort-level AUC values across k cohorts with τ² estimation. Han-Eskin RE2 (Han & Eskin, AJHG 2011): threshold by Cochran's Q I² (I² < 30% → fixed effect; I² ≥ 30% → REM). Stouffer's weighted Z: sum of sqrt(n)-weighted per-cohort Z-statistics, direction-consistent. ComBat empirical Bayes: Johnson 2007 method applied to TCGA + K2 expression matrices before pooled scoring; ComBat-Seq variant available for count-based future extensions. LOCO sensitivity: leave-one-cohort-out pooled AUC across the four labelled cohorts (TCGA, K2 n=260, K2 balanced n=34, GSE286332).

1.7 Robustness battery

Permutation null: 5,000 random shuffles of DM1/DM2 labels with panel fixed; empirical p = (1 + #shuffles ≥ observed AUC) / 5,001. Panel-conditional null: 10,000 label shuffles, panel fixed, tracking max |Cohen's d| under shuffled labels. Thyroid-matched bio-prior null: 1,000 random 8-gene draws from the top-quartile thyroid-expressed gene pool (n=12,921 genes, defined as TCGA-THCA mean TPM > 75th percentile), recomputing the within-cohort z-mean panel score and Cohen's d each time. Knock-out n-1 floor: drop each panel gene in turn and recompute the DM1 vs DM2 Cohen's d. Bootstrap cluster stability: 500 bootstrap iterations of the d4p2 sig_score distribution with KMeans k=2 re-clustering; per-sample stability = fraction of iterations the sample is assigned to its original cluster.

1.8 Clinical / survival analysis

Cox proportional hazards regression was performed using lifelines 0.30 (Davidson-Pilon Python package). The multivariable model adjusted for age (continuous, per year), sex (binary, male reference), AJCC pathologic stage (ordinal score 1-4 from Stage I through Stage IV), BRAF V600E status (binary), and RAS status (binary). Endpoints: progression-free interval (PFI, primary; events 11.6%) and overall survival (OS, secondary; events 3.4%). DM1 vs DM2 covariate added as the variable of interest. The Cox PH assumption (proportional hazards across covariates) was verified by Schoenfeld residual tests (Therneau-Grambsch); no significant violations were detected. Pooled meta-analysis of TCGA + MSK-IMPACT OS HR estimates used DerSimonian-Laird random-effects (statsmodels 0.14 meta_analysis module); between-cohort heterogeneity assessed via Cochran I². NRI / IDI computed under 5-fold stratified-CV LogReg probabilities per Pencina 2008 continuous-NRI definition (event = DM1, non-event = DM2; equal weighting between cases and controls); 95% CI from 2,000 stratified bootstrap iterations of test-fold predictions. Decision curve analysis per Vickers & Elkin 2006 on TCGA 5-fold CV probabilities.

1.9 HM450 methylation analysis

TCGA-THCA HumanMethylation 450K array β-values were downloaded from GDC for n=503 primary tumours. Promoter probe-set summarisation used the mean β-value across probes within 1500 bp upstream of the canonical transcript start site (Ensembl GRCh38 annotation) per gene. Per-gene Cohen's d between DM1 (n=87) and DM2 (n=25) was computed using the unbiased two-sample pooled standard-deviation formula; significance was assessed by Wilcoxon rank-sum test with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction across the 8 panel genes. Within-DM1 fusion+/− stratification used cBioPortal SV calls.

1.10 cBioPortal SV / fusion analysis

Structural-variant calls were downloaded via the cBioPortal REST API for the thca_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018_structural_variants molecular profile (n=542 SV-tested primary tumours). Fusion-positive tumours were defined as harboring at least one fusion involving RET, NTRK1, NTRK3, ALK, or BRAF as either the 5' or 3' partner. Missingness was assessed via chi-square MAR test against clinical features (p=0.56, MAR confirmed). Sensitivity analyses imputing missing-as-negative, missing-as-prevalence-matched, and complete-case all retained the DM1 vs DM2 OR in the range 7.18-9.07.

1.11 Single-cell analysis (Lu 2023 GSE193581)

Pre-processed single-cell RNA-seq data (15,330 thyrocytes/epithelial cells from 21 patient samples; 67,678 total including non-tumour) were downloaded from project/results/v17_lu2023/GSE193581_cell_panel_score.tsv. The 8-gene panel z-score (DM_score in the source TSV) was computed per cell using within-cohort z-mean of normalised log-counts. Per-patient mean DM_score in malignant cells was used for the forest plot in Fig 6B. Cohen's d between ATC vs PTC malignant cells (n=6,034 + 8,590 cells) was computed at the single-cell resolution.

1.12 DepMap CRISPR + PRISM analysis

DepMap 23Q4 release: 1,141 cell lines × CRISPR Chronos essentiality scores were stratified by DM1_like_score (computed via the 8-gene panel z-mean from CCLE expression data per paper9_sl_first_pass/ccle_dm1_state.tsv); DM1-high vs DM1-low groups defined by median split (n=372 vs n=394). Per-gene Δ effect (DM1-high − DM1-low) was computed with Welch t-test and Cohen's d effect size. PRISM drug screen log-fold-change values for 1,518 compounds tested across 470-480 cell lines were stratified by the same DM1-state grouping; FDR correction by Benjamini-Hochberg. Thyroid-lineage-specific subset (n=11: CAL62, BHT101, BCPAP, TT2609C02, FTC133, 8305C, 8505C, MB1, ASH3, IHH4, HOTHC) absolute essentiality medians were extracted from /data/thca/repo_results/paper11_pancancer/phase_D_depmap/lineage_dependency_summary.tsv.

1.13 Statistical reporting

All p-values are two-sided unless explicitly stated as one-sided. Effect sizes use Cohen's d with the unbiased two-sample pooled standard-deviation formula. 95% confidence intervals for Cohen's d use bootstrap resampling (2,000 iterations stratified by class). 95% CIs for Cox hazard ratios use the Wald approximation on log-HR. Multiple-testing correction uses Benjamini-Hochberg FDR within each analysis family (e.g., 8-gene HM450, 16-panel scan, 12-contrast effect-size matrix). Exact n per panel/test/comparison is reported in each figure caption.

3. Inclusion & ethics statement

Cohort inclusion rationale. Korean cohorts (K2 PRJEB11591, GSE286332, Lee 2024/GSE213647) are explicitly included because thyroid cancer incidence is disproportionately concentrated in East Asia (South Korea has the world's highest reported incidence), and prior Western-cohort-only molecular taxonomies inadequately characterize the BRAF/RAS-negative compartment in this population. Cross-ethnic validation is the deciding analysis pre-specified in §1.4 of the main text. We acknowledge that Japanese, Chinese, and Taiwanese RNA-seq cohorts of comparable depth were not available at the time of writing; a true Pan-Asian extension is reserved for the planned Paper 4 backlog.

Data-use compliance. All datasets used in this study are publicly available and were re-analysed under their original data-use agreements. TCGA-THCA and MSK-IMPACT (Landa 2016) data were accessed through GDC and cBioPortal with appropriate institutional research-use authorisation. ENA PRJEB11591 (K2) was accessed under its standard public re-use terms. GEO datasets (GSE286332, GSE213647, GSE76039, GSE29265, GSE33630, GSE53157, GSE65144, GSE193581) were accessed under GEO's public re-use terms. DepMap and PRISM data are released by Broad Institute under permissive CC BY 4.0. No new patient samples were collected for this study; no human-subject institutional review board approval was required for the secondary analysis of de-identified public datasets.

Authorship and contributorship. S.C. (국승호, Seungho Cook) is a graduate-level researcher; Y.H.-W. (유형원, Yu Hyeong-won) is a senior clinician-investigator at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital.
