Executive summary

Master synthesis figure: 8 layers across 4 pillars (RNA · sc · protein · methylation), single panel. Click to view full-size.

Paper 1 uses two label systems on TCGA THCA: d4p2 sig.DM (Hashimoto/HT-overlap axis) and 8-gene panel RAI_8 / DM_call (RAI-lineage differentiation axis). Memory `DM1 Round 8` reports 74.5% concordance between d4p2 sig.DM and dm_master (both HT-axis derived). R17 instead asks the orthogonal question — how often do the HT-axis and RAI-axis call the same sample DM1? — and finds 14.8% concordance on n=500. The 85.2% discordance is driver-pattern biology, not a labeling bug: BRAF V600E silences RAI without HT overlap (panel-DM1 / d4p2-DM2), RAS preserves RAI but acquires HT overlap (panel-DM2 / d4p2-DM1). This holds at single-cell level in Lu 2023 (n=14,624 malignant cells) and replicates cross-ethnically in Korean GSE286332 (n=18).

Reviewer Q-defense matrix

Q1. Why don't d4p2 DM1 and panel DM1 coincide?
Two orthogonal axes by design. d4p2 tracks the Hashimoto/HT-overlap immune axis, panel RAI_8 tracks the RAI-lineage differentiation axis. BRAF silences RAI without HT overlap (60% of TCGA discordant cells), RAS preserves RAI but acquires HT overlap (47/54 RAS samples). 14.8% concordance is the *biology*, not a labeling bug. See L1 cross-tab.
Q2. Is the panel just measuring tumor purity / immune fraction?
No. Panel DM1 is dominant in BRAF (low immune-fraction driver, 82% panel-DM1) and rare in RAS (higher immune-fraction driver, 11% panel-DM1). The orthogonality to the HT-axis argues directly against a purity confound. Also covered by R6 purity-adjusted Cox (memory `paper11_pancancer`).
Q3. Does the bulk discordance hold at single-cell level?
Yes. Lu 2023 n=14,624 malignant cells: ATC 99.3% silenced (61% BRAF-like + 38.3% dark-matter), PTC 72.2% WT-like with 9% RAS-like and 3% dark-matter. The four-quadrant partition seen in bulk is reproduced cell-by-cell. See L2.
Q4. Does this generalize across ethnicity?
Yes, directionally, in n=18 Korean GSE286332. Korean PTC+HT cluster 100% on the HT-axis (78% dark-matter + 22% RAS-like), Korean PTC without HT cluster 89% WT-like. Korean cohort is small but the two-axis decomposition replicates. See L3.
Q5. What about the dark-matter zone in BRAF·RAS-neg?
BRAF·RAS-neg (TCGA n=128) shows the highest concordance (22.7%) with 9 samples in both-DM1 (concordant dark-matter). At sc level, 38.3% of ATC malignant cells occupy the dark-matter zone. This is the empirical cellular substrate for Paper 1's BRAF·RAS-neg dark-matter framing.

Caveats

  • n=500 TCGA merge after dropping samples missing either label. Original TCGA cohort = 504; 4 samples drop out.
  • Lu 2023 sc HT-overlap signature is HVG-restricted (6/23 genes); direction matches bulk, but absolute zone boundary positions could shift on full counts.
  • GSE286332 n=18 is small. Treat as directional cross-ethnic replication, not standalone claim.
  • 'BRAF-like zone' at sc level is a phenotypic label inherited from bulk R17, not an sc driver claim (GSE193581 lacks per-sample BRAF/RAS genotype).
  • Polarity correction applied to Lu 2023 sc per memory `DM1 Round 4` (raw panel z is preserved-high; sign-flipped to align DM1 = silenced).
