1. Bundang prospective cohort power analysis

The main draft repeatedly references the planned Bundang prospective Korean validation cohort. This section formalises the sample-size requirements using Schoenfeld 1983 log-rank power formula with two-sided α=0.05 and power=0.80.

1.2 Recommendation

To definitively detect the pooled TCGA+MSK HR=2.53 [1.31, 4.89] at α=0.05 power=0.80, the Bundang prospective cohort requires approximately:

  • n ≈ 384-430 patients at TCGA-equivalent PFI event rate (11.6%)
  • n ≈ 195-249 patients at enriched advanced-disease PFI event rate (20%)
  • Phase I single-site Bundang n=200 is underpowered for HR detection but adequate for axis replication (Cohen's d ≈ 1.5+ would be detectable at n=200)

For multi-centre Korean cohort expansion (Asan Medical Center, Samsung Medical Center, Yonsei Severance partnership):

  • Total Korean n ≈ 1000 would enable detection of HR down to 1.76 — sufficient to extend the framework to Korean-specific subtype-level prognostic claims.
  • This is the explicit Pan-Asian extension reserved for Paper 4 backlog.
