Lumenix · BRAF V600E · 2026-05-08

BRAF V600E Deep Dive · THCA + Melanoma

BRAF V600E is the dominant driver in papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC, ~60%) and melanoma (~50%). It creates a constitutively active MAPK signal that promotes immune-suppressive TME — but also generates a recurrent neoantigen target. Our master corpus has only 3 BRAF V600E records (2 unique peptides), exposing a major data gap.

2 · BRAF V600E mutation context

Wild-type BRAF: ...EDLTVKIGDFGLATVKSRWSGS...

BRAF V600E: ...EDLTVKIGDFGLATEKSRWSGS... (V600 → E)

The mutation is at position 600 of the BRAF protein, in the activation loop of the kinase domain. The V→E substitution mimics phospho-activation, locking BRAF in active state.

4 · BRAF V600E + dabrafenib synthetic lethality (THCA framing)

The synthetic lethality angle for BRAF V600E + thyroid:

1.BRAF V600E in PTC (~60%)
generates neoantigen + MAPK-driven immune suppression
2.Dabrafenib (BRAF inhibitor)
relieves MAPK suppression but tumor escapes via other pathways
3.BRAF V600E peptide vaccine
primes T-cell response while dabrafenib unmasks tumor
4.Anti-PD-1 ICI
maintains T-cell expansion, prevents exhaustion

→ This 3-component combination (BRAFi + vaccine + ICI) is being explored in clinical trials (NCT03554083 etc.) for melanoma and is a candidate strategy for PTC.

5 · Korean BRAF V600E THCA outlook

  • BRAF V600E in Korean PTC: similar prevalence (~60%) to Western cohorts.
  • Korean common HLA-A*24:02 (18.5% allele freq) — need to predict V600E peptide presentation on A*24:02 specifically.
  • NetMHCpan / MHCflurry on A*24:02 + V600E candidate peptides → action item for Paper 4 (Korean GD HLA backlog).
  • Wet-lab pilot: HLA-A*24:02-tg mouse + dabrafenib + V600E peptide vaccine + anti-PD-1.

6 · Honest gap

Our master corpus is severely under-supplied for BRAF V600E neoantigen prediction:

  • Only 3 BRAF V600E records in master — barely above noise.
  • All 3 are class II HLA-DR / DQ — no class I representation.
  • Dominant cancer in our records is gastric (n=2), not thyroid (n=0) or melanoma (n=1) — opposite of clinical relevance.
  • This is a top priority for Korean K2/Bundang FFPE cohort acquisition.
