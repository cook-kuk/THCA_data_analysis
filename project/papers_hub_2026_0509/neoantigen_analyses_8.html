Headline: Even with biophysics-only features (hydrophobicity, charge, aromatic, length, 20-aa frac), Random Forest on the leakage-free split reaches AUROC=0.839 / AUPRC=0.887. Aromatic content is the strongest signal (p=1.4e-210, depleted in immunogenic). HLA representation is heavily Western-biased: HLA-A*02:01 dominates (n=6,830) while Korean A*24:02 (n=1,979) is under-represented despite higher South Korea allele frequency — argues for Korean K2 cohort as critical complement.

D · ESMFold 3D structures (30 TESLA peptides)

pLDDT (per-residue confidence, 0-1 scale; ESMFold writes to PDB B-factor):

  • POS μ pLDDT = 0.72, NEG μ pLDDT = 0.71, Δ = +0.01, p = 0.384
  • Pairwise Cα RMSD: within_pos μ = 6.92Å (n=43), within_neg μ = 6.92Å (n=33), across μ = 6.82Å (n=84)
HONEST: pLDDT and RMSD do NOT separate POS vs NEG. ESMFold on isolated 9-15mers produces extended/disordered conformations (mean RMSD ~7Å is large), so peptide-only structure isn't predictive. Production needs AlphaFold-Multimer with full pMHC complex (peptide constrained in HLA groove); that's a GPU job we'd run for real-paper version. Live 3D viewer at neoantigen_3d_viewer.html is for show-and-tell, not for prediction.
