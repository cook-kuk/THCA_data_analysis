Tab 2 · Post-TESLA field map · binding → presentation → recognition → clinical response

The neoantigen immunogenicity prediction field has moved through four eras. Each card = a milestone paper.

2017

Łuksza 2017 · Balachandran 2017 Nature

Neoantigen quality framework R × D. Foundation of "binding alone is not enough — foreignness + dissimilarity-to-self matter."

2017

Sahin 2017 Nature · IVAC

First personalized peptide melanoma vaccine. Proof of concept that personalized neoantigen pools can elicit T-cell responses in humans.

2020 ★

Wells 2020 Cell · TESLA

Canonical benchmark. 608 pMHCs · 37 patient-matched T-cell-bound · 310 independent. Definitively showed binding ≠ immunogenic.

2020

NetMHCpan-4.1 · MHCflurry-2.0 · HLAthena

Reynisson 2020 NAR · O'Donnell 2020 Cell Syst · Sarkizova 2020 Nat Biotech. Binding + presentation predictors with MS-validation.

2021

Schmidt 2021 Cell Systems · PRIME

Joint binding + immunogenicity model. License-gated for commercial use.

2023

Müller 2023 Immunity · NeoRanking

Harmonized WES/RNA-seq dataset. ML method comparison. Gartner subset = patient-matched ground truth.

2023

Albert 2023 Bioinformatics · BigMHC

Presentation predictor with transfer learning to immunogenicity. MANAFEST T-cell validation.

2024

Borch 2024 Front Immunol · IMPROVE

17,500 tested neoepitope candidates · 467 T-cell-recognized · 70 patients. Broad-scale T-cell screen.

2023 ★★

Rojas 2023 Nature · BNT122 PDAC

Personalized mRNA neoantigen vaccine + atezolizumab + mFOLFIRINOX in PDAC. 8/16 vaccine-responders. RFS HR ≈ 0.08. First clinical vaccine efficacy evidence.

2024 ★★

Weber 2024 Lancet · KEYNOTE-942

mRNA-4157/V940 + pembro adjuvant melanoma. RFS HR 0.561. Phase IIb proof. Combined with adjuvant ICI.

2024

Pant 2024 Nat Med · ELI-002

Lymph-node-targeted mKRAS amphiphile. Off-the-shelf cassette anchor for KRAS G12 hotspots.

2025

Sethna 2025 Nature · BNT122 long-FU

3-yr durability of vaccine-induced TCRs in PDAC. Long-lived CD8+ T-cell memory.

2025

Braun 2025 Nature · RCC vaccine · Yarchoan 2024 Nat Med · HCC DNA

Cancer-type expansion: RCC peptide + ipi · HCC DNA + checkpoint. Cold-tumor / TMB-low feasibility test.

2026

Berbís 2026 Nat Med · SPARK

Agentic-AI architecture template. Our hub + agent inherits this pattern: planner / tool-router / verifier / memory.

Tab 3 · Validation level ladder · LEVEL_0 → LEVEL_7

This ladder is the rule that keeps "neoantigen candidate" and "validated neoantigen" from being collapsed into one. Required by the brief — enforced in every loader.

L7
TCR-pMHC structure / affinity
Crystal / cryo-EM structure of the TCR bound to peptide-MHC, OR measured K_D / K_off of the cognate TCR.
TCR3d · NeoTCR with structures · CEDAR cdr3 entries (15 records)
L6
Clinical vaccine response
Vaccine-induced T-cell expansion in a patient who received the vaccine, OR patient-level clinical response (RFS / OS / radiographic) linked to vaccine.
BNT122 (Rojas 2023) · KEYNOTE-942 (Weber 2024) · ELI-002 (Pant 2024) — 3 records in our master
L5
Patient-matched T-cell recognition
The patient's OWN T cells (PBMC or TIL) recognize the peptide on the patient's HLA. Highest non-clinical priority.
TESLA (37 patient-matched) · NeoRanking Gartner (46 records ingested) · CEDAR cancer-restricted T-cell entries (182 records)
L4
T-cell assay (any donor)
ELISpot / tetramer / IFN-γ release / CD107a degranulation / proliferation. Donor T cells, not necessarily patient-matched.
CEDAR T-cell entries (37 records · non-patient-matched subset) · NEPdb positive (manual)
L3
Mutation + MS presentation
Patient-specific mutation confirmed in tumor + peptide eluted from MHC complex by mass spectrometry.
dbPepNeo MC (manual) · TSNAdb validated subset (manual)
L2
MS eluted ligand
Peptide identified by ligand-elution MS. No patient-mutation crosswalk required.
CEDAR MS-ligand entries (4,794 records) · BigMHC el_test (Mendeley)
L1
MHC binding assay
In-vitro IC50 or competitive binding measured.
CEDAR binding-only · BigMHC example1 (9 records)
L0
Predicted only
Computational prediction (NetMHCpan / MHCflurry / PRIME) without any experimental validation.
TSNAdb predicted (manual) · all-cohort virtual neoantigens
Critical rule: any claim with the word "immunogenic" requires L4 or higher. Any claim with "vaccine target" requires L5 or higher. Any claim of "clinical efficacy" requires L6. The verifier in the agent rejects mismatched claims.

Distribution in our master: L2 = 4,794 · L0 = 500 · L5 = 182 · L4 = 37 · L1 = 27 · L7 = 15 · L6 = 3. Most records are MS-eluted-ligand level (CEDAR-dominant). The L4–L7 subset (237 records) is the publishable training/validation set.

Cross-reading for thyroid: Rojas / Weber / Braun / Yarchoan all converge on the same design template — vaccine + adjuvant ICI + standard-of-care backbone, with adjuvant-MRD timing. A thyroid trial would copy this template but must confront the low-TMB problem (PTC ≈ 0.4 mut/Mb) by leaning on shared antigens (BRAF, RET fusion, MAGE-A, CT antigens) rather than personalized pools. ELI-002-class off-the-shelf cassette is the closest match.
