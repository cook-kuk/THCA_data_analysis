Paper 2 image-DM1 audit conclusion · 2026-05-09

결론: Paper 2는 죽은 게 아니라, 훨씬 정직해졌다.

RAS-like/FVPTC AUC 1.000은 TSS artefact라서 폐기한다. 하지만 UNI-final 모델은 GPU leave-one-TSS-out에서 overall AUC 0.852로 살아남았다. 따라서 새 결론은 “perfect subgroup claim”이 아니라 center-holdout을 통과한 TCGA pilot + K2 H&E 외부검증 gate다.

Reject

“RAS-like AUC=1.000” 또는 “FVPTC AUC=1.000”은 main claim으로 쓰면 안 된다. TSS-only logistic regression이 같은 16 slides에서 AUC=1.000을 만들기 때문에 image-independent biology claim이 아니다.

Keep

UNI-final image-DM1는 center-holdout overall AUC=0.852로 TCGA pilot signal이 남는다. Paper 2는 K2 H&E 외부검증을 붙이면 다시 high-impact path가 열린다.

Allowed: TCGA-THCA H&E foundation-model features contain a DM1-associated signal that survives leave-one-TSS-out validation at the overall level (UNI LOTO AUC=0.852), while the original RAS-like/FVPTC perfect subgroup AUC is a TSS-confounded small-N artefact and requires K2 H&E or multi-site FFPE validation.

Claim Ladder

what should and should not be said

Do not claim

“RAS-like/FVPTC image-DM1 prediction is perfect.” The TSS-only model already produces AUC=1.000 in the same subgroup.

Careful claim

“RAS-like signal is suggestive.” UNI LOTO RAS-like AUC=0.718, but n is small and subgroup positives are sparse.

Safe claim

“UNI H&E features retain overall DM1 signal under leave-one-TSS-out validation.” This is backed by overall LOTO AUC=0.852.

Upgrade claim

“H&E-derived DM1 risk generalizes externally.” This requires K2 H&E or multi-site FFPE validation before it becomes a main-text pillar.

Impact Verdict

practical journal-level read, not wishful thinking

Current paper value

credible, not complete

TCGA-only Paper 2 is no longer a “perfect pathology biomarker” paper. It is now a credible H&E-DM1 pilot plus a unusually strong confound audit. That is scientifically cleaner, but it needs external validation before it can carry a high-impact standalone claim.

Impact ceiling

K2 H&E is the switch. If it validates UNI image-DM1, the paper moves from pilot/methods tier back toward a serious translational pathology story.
