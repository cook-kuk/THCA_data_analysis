The honest LOSO finding: when we train on n-1 sources and test on the held-out source, AUROC drops to 0.40-0.55 — barely above chance, sometimes below. This means our 0.854 CV AUROC is partially capturing source-specific curation signal (which sources publish positives vs negatives) rather than universal immunogenicity. This is what reviewer-2 will catch; reporting it ourselves is the right move. The fix is per-source rebalancing or domain-adaptation training.
