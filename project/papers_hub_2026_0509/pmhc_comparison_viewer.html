Lumenix · pMHC method comparison · 2026-05-08

pMHC Structural Method Comparison

Side-by-side: ESMFold pseudo-pMHC (single-chain linker hack, 9 sec) vs RCSB crystal structure 1Q94 (HLA-A*11:01 + β2m + 9-mer peptide, real X-ray). Same target geometry — different methods, different costs, different accuracy. AlphaFold-Multimer (Layer 2) result will be added when CPU run completes.

★ ESMFold pseudo-pMHC · KRAS G12D

Single-chain construct via linker trick: HLA-stub(155aa) + GGGGSGGGGS + β2m-stub(45aa) + GGGGSGGGGS + GADGVGKSAL(10aa) = 225 aa total.
method: ESMFold v1 (Lin 2023 Science)
cost: free CPU API (api.esmatlas.com)
time: 9 seconds
output: 141 KB PDB · pLDDT in B-factor
caveat: linker hack means HLA + β2m in same chain → unrealistic geometry

RCSB 1Q94 · HLA-A*11:01 + peptide (real X-ray)

Real crystal structure: HLA heavy chain (A) + β2-microglobulin (B) + 9-mer peptide (C). HLA-A*11:01 is the same allele that presents KRAS G12D peptides in TESLA.
method: X-ray crystallography (real lab experiment)
cost: years of synthesis + crystallography
output: 568 KB PDB · multiple chains, exact geometry
caveat: peptide is NOT KRAS G12D (different test peptide)

RCSB 1HHK · HLA-A*02:01 + 9-mer (real)

Different HLA allele (A*02:01, Western common). Same overall pMHC architecture: α1+α2 groove + α3 + β2m. The peptide sits in the groove with anchor at P2 + P9.
method: X-ray
allele: HLA-A*02:01
peptide: 9-mer (P2 = anchor L, P9 = anchor V/L)

RCSB 1AO7 · TCR + HLA-A*02:01 + Tax (3-component)

Full TCR-pMHC complex: HLA + β2m + peptide + TCR Vα (gold) + TCR Vβ (orange). The TCR docks on top of the peptide-MHC complex with CDR3α/β contacting the peptide.
method: X-ray crystallography
chains: HLA(A) + β2m(B) + peptide(C) + TCRα(D) + TCRβ(E)
covers: pMHC binding + TCR engagement + immune synapse architecture

What to look for in 3D viewers above:
- HLA α1+α2 groove: two parallel helices forming a "wall" with the peptide between them.
- β2-microglobulin: globular domain underneath the HLA, like a base.
- Peptide: extended chain with side-chains at P2 and P9 dipping INTO pockets in the HLA groove.
- TCR (only in 1AO7, 2BNQ): large two-chain (Vα + Vβ) cap on top of the pMHC, with CDR3 loops contacting the peptide.
- ESMFold pseudo-pMHC: should resemble the above in topology BUT with a long unstructured linker connecting HLA to β2m (artifact of the construct).

Why ESMFold pseudo-complex is NOT a substitute for AlphaFold-Multimer

  • ESMFold expects single-chain — when forced multi-chain via linker, the linker is unstructured and dominates conformation.
  • HLA + β2m heterodimer interface is the wrong geometry (chain ends not at correct positions).
  • Peptide may not actually sit in the groove — could fold into the linker.
  • For PUBLICATION-grade: must use AlphaFold-Multimer or real crystal/cryo-EM.
  • For VISUALIZATION + first-pass: ESMFold pseudo-complex is fine.
