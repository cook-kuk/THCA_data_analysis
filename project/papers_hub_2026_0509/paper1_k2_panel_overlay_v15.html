Paper 1 mechanism v15 · K2 panel-only overlay · 2026-05-09

K2 is a panel-only calibration stress test, not a MAPK validation cohort.

PRJEB11591/K2 has local 8-gene mini-index quantification for 260 runs. K2 tumor runs score DM2-like, but K2 normal runs also score high, so this is a cross-platform score overlay and calibration audit only. Because MAPK-output genes are absent, no causal, tumor-normal, prevalence, or MAPK-correlation claim is made from K2.
