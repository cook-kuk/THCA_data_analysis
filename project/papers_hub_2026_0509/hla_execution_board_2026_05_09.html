Decision

what changed after the HLA level-up

Paper 2 is the near-term push

Use the expression ecology spine: HT-overlap, HLA-II/AP, IFN-gamma, B/TLS, scRNA/spatial and negative stress-tests. This is the strongest current HLA story.

Paper 4 stays separate

GD/AITD HLA genetics has a real Pan-Asian architecture, but NComm-level claim needs Korean adult GD HLA validation or equivalent genetics access.

Boundary is non-negotiable

Do not claim PTC HLA allele risk. Do not call tissue expression genotype replication. Do not call DRB1*04:05 validated.
