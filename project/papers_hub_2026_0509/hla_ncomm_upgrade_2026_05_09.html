Final Verdict

what level is this now?

결론: 2개 paper가 맞다. Paper 2는 HLA-II/AP, IFN-gamma, B/TLS, spatial burden을 묶은 PTC+Hashimoto immune-ecology paper로 Nature Communications 도전권이 생겼다. Paper 4는 Pan-Asian Graves/GD HLA allele paper로 독립성이 충분하지만, Korean adult GD NGS 또는 source-level meta extraction이 들어오면 NComm급으로 올라간다.

Paper 2 status

NComm 도전권. 이미 한국 bulk, HT/PTC bulk, HT/PTC scRNA, thyroid Visium, negative invasion stress-test까지 붙었다.

Paper 4 status

강한 GD-HLA backbone. DPB1*05:01, B*46:01, A*02:07, C*01:02 축이 살아 있고, AITD spatial/array가 tissue biology를 받친다.

절대 금지

Paper 2 PTC expression 결과를 allele risk로 말하지 않는다. Paper 4 GD allele 결과를 PTC case-control evidence로 쓰지 않는다.

Paper 2: PTC+HT HLA Ecology

expression, antigen-presentation, TLS, spatial validation

Paper 2의 제목감은 “Hashimoto-overlap PTC에서 HLA-II antigen-presentation/TLS ecology가 반복 검증된다”다. allele association paper가 아니라, tumor-normal, HT/PTC, scRNA, spatial에서 같은 immune program이 반복되는 paper다.

Paper 4: Graves/GD HLA Genetics

allele/genotype track only, with AITD tissue support

Core allele story

  • Pan-Asian GD candidates: DPB1*05:01, B*46:01, A*02:07, C*01:02.
  • C*01:02 looks most GD-specific in the current triangulation.
  • DPB1*05:01 is likely AITD-broad, so it needs careful disease-specific language.

Tissue biology support

  • GSE248205 AITD spatial: CD74/MIF d=3.62, B/TLS d=2.57, AP/TLS d=2.26.
  • GSE29315 HT array: HLA-II/AP d=5.45, exact p=0.0007, FDR=0.0023.
  • These do not prove GD genotype; they support antigen-presentation biology.

Boundary Lock

this is what keeps the two papers publishable

심사자 공격 1순위는 disease mixing이다. 그래서 문장을 고정한다: Paper 2 = expression ecology only. Paper 4 = GD/AITD allele-genotype genetics only. Shared HLA names are mechanistic motivation, not cross-paper evidence transfer.

Reviewer Defense

pre-answer the attacks

Attack: this is just inflammation

Answer: GSE6004 invasion stress-test is negative, while HT/PTC, Korean bulk, scRNA, and spatial all align on AP/TLS modules.

Attack: bulk is cell composition

Answer: We intentionally make that biology visible with scRNA pseudobulk, B/TLS modules, and spatial spot burden. The claim is ecology, not tumor-cell intrinsic HLA mutation.

Attack: GD alleles do not prove PTC

Answer: Correct. That is why Paper 2 and Paper 4 are separated and the boundary audit is clean for the new HLA pack.
