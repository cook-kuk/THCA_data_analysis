Method Cards

method ledgerRNA/module scoring

Software: pandas, numpy, scipy, ssGSEA/singscore-style z-score/module scoring

How to interpret: Used for DM1/DM2, HLA/IFN/TLS, thyroid differentiation, MAPK output. Interpret as cohort-level molecular axis, not direct causality.

method ledgerMethylation/HM450

Software: TCGA HM450 beta tables, scipy/statsmodels, effect sizes

How to interpret: Used for promoter/mean-beta evidence. Interpret as correlative epigenetic silencing support.

method ledgerSurvival/meta-analysis

Software: Cox/log-rank style tables, fixed/random-effect summaries, confidence intervals

How to interpret: Used for OS/PFI/prognosis. Check endpoint, event count, and cohort definition before claim use.

method ledgerDeconvolution

Software: Lu 2023 scRNA pseudobulk, scipy NNLS, ridge-NNLS, LR-clip, sklearn NuSVR

How to interpret: Used to test whether bulk DM1 signals survive cell-type composition adjustment. NuSVR is primary because NNLS collapses T cells.

method ledgerSpatial transcriptomics

Software: GSE250521/GSE193581 spatial/scRNA score overlays and neighborhood summaries

How to interpret: Used for anatomy/context/caveat support. Current spatial v15B-v18 is caveat/autopsy, not main Paper 1 proof.

method ledgerWSI/image

Software: OpenSlide-style tiling, DINOv2 ViT-L fallback, CLAM/UNI when available, sklearn LOSO

How to interpret: Used for H&E projection of DM1. Interpret as small pilot signal, not clinical pathology model.

method ledgerDepMap/PRISM drug response

Software: DepMap dependency matrices, PRISM drug LFC/AUC-like response, Spearman/FDR/Cohen's d

How to interpret: Used for IC50/drug-response map. Treat PRISM as public screen proxy; wet-lab validation needed.

method ledgerNeoantigen benchmark

Software: CLEAN-Neo/CROSS-Neo harness, sklearn baselines, ESMFold/structure proxy, public-overlap audits

How to interpret: Used for leakage-aware ranking and abstention. Interpret strict splits before pooled AUROC/AUPRC.

method ledgerHTML dossier builder

Software: project/scripts/build_papers_hub_2026_0509.py, local HTML/CSS, generated JSON/TSV inventories

How to interpret: Used to make the repo-style review surface. It does not create new biological evidence.
