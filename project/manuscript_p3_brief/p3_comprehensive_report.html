Glossary — autoimmune-thyroid 약어

Paper 3 scope = autoimmune-thyroid disease only. Paper 1 (cancer) / Paper 2 (autoimmune-overlap PTC) territory term 0건.
Graves' disease (GD)
"갑상선을 자극하는 자가항체가 생성되어 갑상선 호르몬이 과다 분비되는 자가면역 질환" (advisor 인용). TSAb / TRAb (TSH-receptor stimulating antibody) 가 TSH receptor 에 결합하여 hyperthyroidism 유발. 안구 돌출 + diffuse goiter + 갑상선 기능항진 의 triad.
TSAb · TRAb
Thyroid-stimulating antibody (TSAb) = TRAb stimulating subset. TSH receptor 에 binding 후 cAMP-PKA signaling 활성화 → 갑상선 호르몬 분비 증가. GD 의 핵심 mechanism.
cookHLA
본인이 first-author 로 개발한 HLA imputation tool (Cook et al. Nat Commun). SNP panel 데이터에서 4-digit HLA allele 추론 — Pan-Asian reference panel 사용 시 imputation accuracy > 95%.
arcasHLA
RNA-seq 기반 HLA typing tool (Orenbuch 2020). Bundang prospective cohort 응답 시 Task 1 에서 사용.
Chu 2018 reference
Chu X et al. 2018 J Med Genet 55(10):685–692, doi:10.1136/jmedgenet-2017-105146 (PMC 6161647). Han Chinese GD n=1,468 vs ctrl n=1,490 의 4-digit SNP2HLA imputation reference data.
DerSimonian-Laird random-effects
Forest meta-analysis 표준 random-effects pooling. Cohort-specific log-OR + standard error 로 inverse-variance weighting + τ² heterogeneity estimate.
5-Pillar plan
(I) Korean GD HLA cohort · (II) Chu 2018 replication · (III) Pan-Asian forest meta · (IV) cookHLA SNP cross-platform validation · (V) GD-specific HLA haplotype.

Disease scope + isolation policy

Paper 3 = autoimmune-thyroid hyperfunction ONLY. Paper 1 / Paper 2 territory term 등장 시 즉시 STOP + 재작성.

3.1 Disease definition

Graves' disease 는 "갑상선을 자극하는 자가항체가 생성되어 갑상선 호르몬이 과다 분비되는 자가면역 질환" (advisor 인용). 임상적으로 hyperthyroidism + 안구 돌출 + 갑상선 미만성 종대 (diffuse goiter) 의 triad. Graves 안병증 (Graves' ophthalmopathy) 동반율 25–50%. 한국 평균 발병율 ~5/10,000 person-year, 여성:남성 = 5–7:1.

3.2 Mechanism distinction (Paper 1 · 2 와의 차별점)

Table 1 — 3 paper 의 disease mechanism distinction
PaperDiseaseMechanismCell-level
Paper 1Papillary thyroid carcinoma (BRAF/RAS-negative subtype)Thyroid neoplasia · driver fusion + epigenetic silencingThyrocyte clonal expansion
Paper 2Autoimmune-overlap papillary thyroid carcinomaAutoimmune destructive infiltration overlay on neoplasiaB-cell zone formation + lymphocytic destruction
Paper 3 ★Graves' disease (autoimmune-thyroid hyperfunction)TSAb-mediated TSH receptor stimulation → hyperthyroidismAntibody-driven thyrocyte stimulation, no destruction
3 disease 의 핵심 차이
Paper 2 vs Paper 3 — autoimmune 공통점 있으나 mechanism 정반대: Paper 2 = destructive infiltration, Paper 3 = stimulating antibody-mediated hyperfunction. 갑상선 조직이 Paper 2 에서는 destruction, Paper 3 에서는 hyperstimulation. 이 mechanism divergence 가 distinct paper 정당화의 핵심.

3.3 Forbidden term boundaries

Paper 3 본문에 다음 term 등장 시 즉시 STOP + 재작성:

  • Paper 2 territory (autoimmune-overlap PTC): Hashimoto · HT · lymphocytic thyroiditis · TLS · tertiary lymphoid structure · AICDA · BCR clonality · PTC+HT · Hashimoto-overlap
  • Paper 1 territory (cancer): BRAF V600E · RET fusion · DM1/DM2 cluster · 8-gene RAI panel mechanism · LIBRETTO-001 · selpercatinib
  • Forbidden conflations: autoimmune-PTC · PTC+GD overlap · thyroid neoplasia framing → "autoimmune thyroid disease" 로 정정

Cross-paper symmetric reference

Paper 2 ↔ Paper 3 references 가 symmetric 해야 reviewer 의 "왜 한 paper 로 안 묶었나" Q 사전 차단.
Paper 2 Discussion 한 줄
"Korean PTC HLA susceptibility (this paper) shares background with Korean Graves' (Paper 3, Cook et al. in prep), but PTC ≠ Graves' as diseases — PTC = neoplasia, Graves' = autoimmune hyperthyroidism, autoimmune-overlap PTC = autoimmune destructive infiltration. Pan-Asian thyroid HLA axis is shared, disease mechanisms distinct."
Paper 3 Discussion 한 줄 (reciprocal)
"The Pan-Asian HLA susceptibility axis identified here for Graves' disease shares background alleles with Korean PTC (Paper 2, Cook et al. in prep) and autoimmune-overlap PTC, but mechanism diverges — Graves' = TSAb-driven hyperthyroidism, autoimmune-overlap PTC = destructive infiltration, PTC = thyroid neoplasia. Shared HLA risk reflects population-level susceptibility; disease specificity is determined downstream."
★ Mechanism divergence framework — symmetric phrase
"Shared HLA risk reflects population-level susceptibility; disease specificity is determined downstream." Paper 2 + Paper 3 Discussion 양쪽에서 동일 phrase 로 trajectory 정당화. 합치지 않은 이유 = HLA risk 는 shared 하나 disease mechanism (neoplasia vs destructive infiltration vs hyperfunction) 은 distinct. Reviewer 가 "한 paper 로 합칠 수 있지 않나?" 묻는 시점에 두 paper 의 Discussion 이 동일 framework 으로 답변 가능.
