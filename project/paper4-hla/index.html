Pan-Asian Graves HLA evidence: what is executable now, what still blocks the full meta-analysis

Paper 4 is the Graves/GD HLA track. The page now separates a count-backed Chu 2018 anchor forest, an extractable-source screening meta-analysis, and the broader Pan-Asian registry that still requires source extraction before final pooled inference.

⚠ Screening meta executed — final full-source Pan-Asian meta still requires extraction

Executive Verdict

Paper 4 already has a strong Asian GD HLA anchor: Chu 2018 reports large Han Chinese GD/control fine-mapping signals for DPB1*05:01, B*46:01, C*01:02, A*02:07, DQB1*02:01, and DRB1*07:01. We now also ran a conservative screening Pan-Asian meta-analysis for extractable Chu/Shin/Chen rows: B*46:01, C*01:02, and DPB1*05:01 can be pooled at screening level, while A*02:07, DQB1*02:01, and DRB1*07:01 remain Chu-only anchors. This supports a serious Paper 4 direction, but a publishable final meta-analysis still needs exact source-level extraction from Liao 2022, Park 2005, Ueda 2014, and older Korean/Japanese/Taiwan/Hong Kong studies.

What it does NOT prove

  • It is not yet the final full-source Pan-Asian meta-analysis.
  • It does not validate Paper 2 PTC HLA.
  • It does not include Bundang Korean GD samples.
  • It does not harmonize all historical frequency metrics.

⚠Critical Red Box: Screening meta-analysis is not the final Paper 4 meta-analysis

We can run a screening meta-analysis only where source-level OR and uncertainty can be reconstructed. Chu 2018 is count/CI-backed. Shin 2019 and Chen 2011 add extractable OR+p rows for selected alleles, but several SEs are approximated from corrected p-values and exact count tables still need extraction. Liao, Park, Ueda, Cho, Naito, Tsai, Hong Kong, and review/meta sources remain extraction targets before final Paper 4 pooled claims.

Why We Are Doing This Analysis

Paper 4 needs a disease-specific identity. GD/Graves is clinically and mechanistically different from Hashimoto-overlap PTC, so it deserves a separate HLA paper rather than being folded into Paper 2.
Asian GD HLA literature is unusually rich. DPB1*05:01, B*46:01, C*01:02, A*02:07, DRB1, and DQB1 repeatedly appear in East-Asian studies.
Chu 2018 gives the first executable anchor. It has n=1,468 GD and n=1,490 controls, OR/CI/p values, and HLA amino-acid fine-mapping.
The broader registry defines the next work. Liao/Shin/Park/Chen/Ueda and historical studies tell us exactly which tables and metrics must be extracted next.
The output must be honest. We show what is statistically visible now and, in red boxes, what blocks final inference.

What Is Still Missing?

  1. Exact table extraction from Liao 2022, Shin 2019, Park 2005, Chen 2011, Ueda 2014, Cho 1987, Naito 1987, Tsai 1989, Hong Kong sources.
  2. Clear metric labels: allele frequency, carrier frequency, genotype frequency, phenotype/serology frequency.
  3. Consistent 4-digit allele harmonization and explicit handling of old serologic antigens.
  4. Country/ancestry model: Korean, Han Chinese, Taiwanese, Japanese, Hong Kong Chinese should not be naively pooled.
  5. Random-effects meta-analysis only after source-level SE/counts are ready.
  6. Bundang Korean GD validation if sample size and phenotype labels are sufficient.
  7. Clinical covariates: sex, age, TRAb/TSI, ophthalmopathy, treatment, recurrence, Hashimoto overlap.
  8. HLA LD/haplotype modeling for B*46:01-C*01:02 and DP/DQ/DR blocks.

Allowed vs Forbidden Interpretation

Allowed

  • Chu 2018 provides a strong Paper 4 GD HLA anchor.
  • DPB1*05:01 and B*46:01 deserve top extraction priority.
  • Paper 4 can become a Pan-Asian GD HLA paper if extraction and harmonization succeed.
  • Shared allele names can motivate a mechanistic bridge to thyroid autoimmunity.

Forbidden

  • Do not call the current output a full Pan-Asian meta-analysis.
  • Do not use GD rows as Paper 2 PTC controls.
  • Do not claim Bundang validation before data exist.
  • Do not pool serology, genotype, carrier, and allele frequency without harmonization.
