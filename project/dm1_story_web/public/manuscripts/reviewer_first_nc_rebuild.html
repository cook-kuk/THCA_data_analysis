Article architecture

A reviewer-first rebuild of the DM1 thyroid-lineage state manuscript

The current manuscript should be reconstructed around five figures, where each figure answers one predictable reviewer attack: artifact, biology, mechanism, validation and clinical relevance.

Prepared 8 July 2026. Scope: figure architecture, reviewer defense and submission package triage. Voice-protected manuscript sections remain author-only.
Download PDF Current Nature-style article Current PDF

Editorial verdict

The paper should stop behaving like a repository of every successful analysis. Nature Communications needs one clean claim ladder, with side analyses moved into Extended Data or Supplementary Information.

Weak framing

"Here is an 8-gene panel that predicts RAI failure."

This invites attacks on panel selection, retrospective endpoints, clinical overclaim and lack of wet-lab causality.

Submission-grade framing

"A hidden thyroid-lineage state exists in papillary thyroid cancer, is not explained by canonical drivers, converges with known dedifferentiation biology, has an epigenetic correlate, reproduces across cohorts and is positioned for prospective RAI harm-avoidance validation."

Main claim ladder

1. ExistenceDM1/DM2 is a real transcriptomic state, not an eight-gene artifact.
2. BiologyDM1 means coordinated loss of thyroid lineage and iodine-handling machinery.
3. Mechanism correlateDM1 shows promoter hypermethylation of lineage genes across driver contexts.
4. ValidationThe state recurs across independent cohorts and modalities.
5. Clinical relevanceDM1 aligns with adverse outcome, RAI-failure biology and FFPE deployability.

What must be cut from the main story

Main-text standard

  • One figure equals one claim.
  • Every panel answers a reviewer attack.
  • Clinical language remains retrospective and prospective-validation bounded.
  • Mechanism language says "epigenetic correlate", not causality.

Demote or remove

  • Spatial, PRISM, DepMap and pan-cancer analyses.
  • Sub-A/B, quantum kernel and algorithm-flex panels.
  • Full 14-cohort forests inside the main figure.
  • Therapy schematics that imply immediate treatment selection.

Reviewer-grade central claim

We identify a driver-orthogonal thyroid-lineage state in papillary thyroid cancer that is recovered by pan-genome expression structure, corresponds to coordinated iodine-handling loss, has an epigenetic silencing correlate, recurs across independent cohorts and modalities, and is associated with retrospective RAI-failure biology and adverse outcome.
Do not claim: "DM1 predicts RAI response and should guide treatment." That is prospective-validation territory.
