한국인 PTC 6-allele HLA forest는 탐색적 후보 우선순위 지도입니다.

엄격한 metric 정합화 후 6개 중 4개 enrichment 신호가 사라지고, 2개 depletion 신호(C*01:02, DQB1*02:01)만 BH-q < 0.05를 통과합니다. Matched-control NGS validation 없이는 case-control association으로 보고되지 않습니다.

⚠ Critical Caveat — read this first

일차 forest(F13)는 단위가 다른 두 수치를 직접 비교한 결과입니다. 한국인 PTC 측은 개인 단위 carrier 빈도(n = 874명), baseline 측은 출판된 allele frequency × 2n(In JW 2015 등 5개 alleles, AFND South Korea pool DPB1 1개). 두 단위는 같은 양이 아닙니다. HWE 가정 하에서만 환산이 가능하며 (carrier = 1−(1−AF)², AF = 1−√(1−carrier)), 실제 분포가 HWE를 따른다는 보장은 없습니다.

F13 — Why this figure matters (and what it does NOT prove)

What this figure shows

  • A*02:07, B*46:01, DPB1*05:01, DRB1*07:01의 OR이 1보다 크게 보이는 enrichment-shaped 신호.
  • C*01:02도 OR=1.47로 enrichment 방향으로 표시.
  • DQB1*02:01은 PTC carrier=0인 zero-cell depletion 신호.
  • n=6 후보 내 BH-q가 모두 0.05 미만(매우 작음).

Why it matters

  • Paper 2 Pillar I에 대한 구체적 6-allele 후보 리스트를 제공.
  • 한국인 PTC와 immune-genetic background 사이의 가능성 있는 연결을 탐색적 hypothesis로 제시.
  • 이후 matched-control NGS validation을 위한 가설 우선순위 지도가 됨.
  • Yu lab과의 논의에서 어떤 allele를 어떤 방향으로 검증해야 하는지 명확화.

What it does NOT prove

  • Causal HLA susceptibility를 입증하지 않습니다.
  • Case-control association 결과가 아닙니다.
  • Carrier 빈도와 allele 빈도를 동등하게 비교하기 때문에 population-level 추론은 불가.
  • 임상 위험 예측 / 환자 선별의 근거가 아닙니다.

Missing evidence — what we don't have yet

Critical gaps

  • Matched-control NGS HLA typing. Currently we compare Korean PTC carrier counts to published allele frequencies from independent populations. We do not have an independent Korean healthy-control cohort typed by the same NGS pipeline.
  • Allele-level dosage on baseline. Public baselines are aggregated allele frequencies — we do not have per-individual genotype data on the baseline side.
  • Multi-cohort PTC replication. The 874-individual pool combines K2, Lee2024, GSE286332_PTC — but no second independent Korean PTC cohort with the same pipeline exists locally.
  • Genome-wide HLA testing. Only six pre-specified candidate alleles tested; we cannot rule out broader signals or deflate winner's-curse on the candidate set.
  • Pre-registered analysis plan. The S0 → S3 progression here is exploratory; future tests must be pre-registered.

Partially-available controls (already attempted, limited)

  • Kim 2014 Korean reference panel — used as a partial baseline (F17_kim2014_*). Not typed on PTC pipeline; allele-level allele-vs-allele not yet computed.
  • Baek 2021 PLOS NGS — used as cross-check (F19_allele_vs_baek2021_ngs_forest); needs S3-equivalent extension.
  • GSE213647 adjacent normal — RNA-seq adjacent-normal arcasHLA calls exist, but these are cancer-patient paired normal tissues, not independent healthy controls.

DQB1*02:01 zero-cell QC audit (2026-05-06)

⚠ Verdict: technically suspicious — most likely arcasHLA subtype-resolution artifact

DQB1*02:01 = 0 carriers in n=874 Korean PTC pool does not pass locus-level callability and resolution checks. DQB1 callability is 72.2% (vs A/B/C ≥ 99.9%), and the DQB1*02 family at 2-digit level appears at the expected Korean rate (13.3% carriers, family-level) but is split into DQB1*02:02 and 40 rare/suspect subtypes (*02:253, *02:276, *02:225Q, *02:199, *02:214N…) instead of the canonical *02:01. The DQB1*02:01-specific depletion signal is suspended pending orthogonal NGS-typer validation.

What the audit checked

  • Per-locus callability (A, B, C, DRB1, DPB1, DQB1)
  • DQB1 4-digit allele distribution + 2-digit family breakdown + DQB1*02 family detail (DQB1*02:01 vs *02:02 vs rare *02:xxx)
  • Subcohort consistency (K2, Lee2024, GSE286332_PTC) of DQB1 callability + carrier counts
  • Cross-locus diversity (n_unique_4digit, n_unique_2digit, top-1 share)
  • Rare/impossible allele patterns (subtype number ≥ 50 in DQB1)
  • arcasHLA per-sample QC for GSE286332 (genotype.json + genes.json inspection)

What the audit found

  • DQB1 callable 72.2% (631/874) vs A/B/C ≥ 99.9%, DRB1 98.5%, DPB1 89.1%.
  • DQB1*02 family carrier rate 13.3% (84/631) — matches published Korean baselines (~13–17%).
  • Within DQB1*02 family: 0 *02:01, 53 *02:02, and ~28 observations across 8 rare subtypes (*02:253, *02:276, *02:225Q, *02:199, *02:214N, *02:215, *02:272, *02:274, *02:205).
  • 140/1262 = 11.1% of DQB1 observations in the high-subtype-number tail (≥ 50). This is unusual for canonical Korean HLA distributions.
  • Absence is consistent across all 3 subcohorts (K2 0/163, Lee2024 0/464, GSE286332_PTC 0/4).

Why this is consistent with technical artifact

  • A real biological zero-rate for *02:01 specifically would not coincide with elevated DQB1-only callability failures.
  • If *02:01 were truly absent, we would expect either reduced overall *02 family carriership or compensatory increase in *02:02 — but the *02 family carrier rate matches published baselines, and *02:01 mass appears redistributed into rare/suspect subtype-number assignments.
  • arcasHLA on low-coverage / ambiguous reads can output rare allele numbers rather than canonical ones (especially when run against a newer IMGT/HLA database than the published Korean baselines were typed against).
  • K2's sub-set with full arcasHLA pipeline output (n=63) shows DQB1 alleles at canonical 4-/6-digit resolution; the 235-pool merges this with non-arcasHLA-pipeline calls.

What is required to resolve

  • Re-type DQB1 with an orthogonal NGS HLA caller (HLA-LA, OptiType, T1K, …) on a stratified subset (n ≥ 50 across K2 + Lee2024).
  • If orthogonal typer collapses the rare *02:xxx tail to *02:01, the artifact is confirmed and the depletion claim is dismissed.
  • Verify the arcasHLA IMGT/HLA database version vs the database era of the published Korean baselines.
  • Audit per-sample DQB1 call confidence from arcasHLA genes.json; flag low-confidence calls as ambiguous, not zero-vs-one.
Final allowed conclusion (this audit): "DQB1*02:01 remains a high-priority depletion candidate only if locus-level callability and resolution checks do not indicate technical failure. In the current Korean PTC pool, locus-level callability (72.2%) and 4-digit subtype distribution within the DQB1*02 family (canonical *02:01 absent, rare-tail *02:xxx subtypes inflated) do indicate technical issues, so DQB1*02:01 zero-cell depletion is suspended pending orthogonal NGS-typer validation. The DQB1*02 family-level carrier rate (13.3%) is consistent with published Korean baselines, supporting the technical-artifact interpretation."
