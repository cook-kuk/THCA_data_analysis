Why "negative" is curator-dependent

  • IEDB tcell v3: 43,339 negatives — patient-tested-but-not-recognized peptides aggregated from many studies. Mixed assays (ELISpot, tetramer, cytotoxicity).
  • NEPdb: 15,392 negatives — Wu lab's curation philosophy: include all tested peptides regardless of outcome. Most negatives have weak MHC binding affinity.
  • IMPROVE benchmark: 2,436 — the 17,500 tested peptides minus 467 recognized — the "purest" negative set (one paper, one cohort, one assay).
  • TESLA mmc4: 568 negatives — patient-matched but failed T-cell validation in TESLA.
  • BigMHC example: 5 negatives — illustrative only.
★ Honest framing for benchmarking
- "Negative" = tested by an assay and not recognized. NOT proven non-immunogenic.
- Different curators tested with different assays at different sensitivities. → cross-source negative set is heterogeneous.
- Best practice: report per-source LOSO breakdown (which we do) instead of pooled metrics.
- A model trained without IEDB negatives may not transfer to IEDB negatives — this is why our LOSO numbers vary so much.
