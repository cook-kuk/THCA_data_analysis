01Disposition

Moderate reserve signal, not overclaim

Use as reserve evidence

The corrected result shows image embeddings can separate DM1/DM2 above chance in this balanced TCGA-THCA subset. It should be framed as a recovered pilot and technical bridge, not as a primary pathology claim for Paper 1.

Do not make a HoVer-NeXt claim

The HoVer-NeXt proof-of-concept did not run because the remote environment lacked toml. The final result here is foundation embedding only.
