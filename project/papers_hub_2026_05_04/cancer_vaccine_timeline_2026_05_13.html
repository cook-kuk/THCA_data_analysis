03What data was used

Two layers matter: the core cancer vaccine corpus and the broader public comparator layer.
Core corpus

Primary cancer vaccine data spine

13 sources, 119,237 cleaned records, and 19,817 leakage-free benchmark rows form the main evaluation base. The structural layer adds 115 ESMFold structures and the executable shortlist adds the 7-peptide set cover plus the KRAS lead GADGVGKSAL.

Public comparator layer

External and industrial context

59 public industrial sources, 54 fetch successes, 3,668 extracted entities, 26,323 candidate pairs, 11,511 Class I score-ready rows, and 666 Class II rows were used to build the external comparison map and the customer-facing boundary.

Why the comparison is favorable

Our stack is not just a better score. It keeps more of the decision path explicit.

  • RF biophysics is the honest floor.
  • ESM2 + LogReg is the rescue branch when distribution shift appears.
  • XGBoost is the nonlinear challenger.
  • MHCflurry / PRIME / DeepImmuno / NetMHCpan / TransPHLA are the public comparator context.
  • BAR-Neo / KG_GA / CROSS-Neo show what strong same-board champions look like.

07Why this is high impact

The impact is in the structure, not in a single headline number.
1

It is traceable

Every claim in the stack points back to a source file, a result file, or a page in the hub. That makes the package auditable.

2

It is decision-ready

The stack does not stop at scores. It converts scores into a shortlist, a router, and a prospective wetlab scaffold.

3

It is customer-readable

A first-time customer MD can follow the chronology without having to reverse-engineer the repo.

Bottom line

The project is strongest when it is presented as a chronology of increasingly strict claims: literature map, corpus build, model build, router, comparator landscape, wetlab scaffold, and then the customer-facing brief. That is the path this page teaches.
