01Thesis

BioDarwin is a regime-aware routing system. The paper should show that routing improves selection quality when sample regime is explicit, while keeping universal SOTA out of scope.

03Abstract contract

Include only six items: problem, method, public-pool result, industrial locked result, 96-well scaffold, and boundary.
  1. Biomedical benchmarks are heterogeneous and no single expert wins everywhere.
  2. BioDarwin uses public metadata, GA/RL search, LOBO blending, and contextual MoE routing.
  3. Frozen public bundles improve from lookup to the current operating champion.
  4. Locked industrial TG4050 control beats BigMHC_IM in diagnostic mode.
  5. The 96-well plate/interpreter defines hit/fail/helper/probe lanes for prospective use.
  6. The paper is not universal SOTA and not prospective superiority until a new locked batch is tested.

06Supplement boundary

Keep out of the main text: overlap audit details, reviewer-safe comparator tables, low-power bundles, caveat-only stress tests, and RF_biophys diagnostics.

07Reviewer defense

questionanswer
Is this just a tuned benchmark?No. The claim is routing under explicit regime control.
Why not claim SOTA everywhere?Dataset-specific winners differ; a router is the correct model class.
What about overlap?Handled explicitly in reviewer-safe tables.
Where is prospective validation?The 96-well interpreter is prospective-ready; a new locked batch is still needed.
Why keep RF_biophys?As an anchor/disagreement feature, not the final model.
