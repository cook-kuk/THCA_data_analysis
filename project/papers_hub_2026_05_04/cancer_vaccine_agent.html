01 · Why thyroid cancer

A vertical worth solving — not one model fits four diseases.

PTC, RAI-refractory DTC, ATC/PDTC, and MTC are immunologically and genomically distinct. Lumenix runs them as four parallel tracks on shared infrastructure — so antigen class, backbone, and PD readout stay subtype-correct.
PTC · DTC · ~85%

Papillary / Differentiated Thyroid Cancer

0.4TMB mut/Mb
Cold→WarmTME state
6.8%KEYNOTE-158 ORR
Clinical problem

2025 ATA 4-tier risk stratification; lobectomy default for cT1 ≤2 cm; molecular testing (Afirma GSC, ThyroSeq v3) drives Bethesda III/IV management. Most "indolent" — yet ~5% become RAI-refractory.

Immune / vaccine rationale

Largely "cold" tumor; the PTC + Hashimoto subset shows HLA-II / IFN-γ enrichment (GSE286332 d=+3.65) and ectopic TLS biology — an antigen-presenting niche worth exploiting.

Available data

TCGA-THCA n=496, GSE184362 (158k cells, 11 PTC), GSE191288 (PTC+HT), GSE250521 spatial atlas. Korean public PRJEB11591 n=260.

Translational opportunity

BRAF-anchored differentiation-state vaccine + ICI in adjuvant / MRD setting. The HLA-II–biased PTC+HT subgroup is the most antigen-presenting niche.

RAIR DTC · 5–15% of DTC

RAI-refractory DTC

1.2TMB mut/Mb
VariableTME state
65.5%Lenva+Pembro ITOG ORR
Clinical problem

Lenvatinib (SELECT mPFS 18.3 vs 3.6 mo), sorafenib (DECISION), cabozantinib (COSMIC-311 HR 0.22). NTRK→larotrectinib · RET→selpercatinib. Redifferentiation: trametinib + ¹³¹I (MERAIODE).

Immune / vaccine rationale

Heterogeneous; subset acquires BRAF/RAS-driven dedifferentiation programs with rising MHC-I but exhausted T-cell signatures post-MKI exposure — vaccine prime + ICI maintenance is a rational sequence.

Available data

GSE76039 (Landa MSK n=84 PDTC + 33 ATC), thca_mskcc_2016, COSMIC-021 cohort 18 RNA-seq, ITOG NCT02973997 translational bank.

Translational opportunity

Post-redifferentiation iodine-uptake recovery → vaccine prime → anti-PD-1 maintenance is the most defensible MRD design after lenvatinib.

ATC · PDTC · ~1–2%

Anaplastic / Poorly-Differentiated Thyroid Cancer

6.1TMB mut/Mb
HotTME state
43.2 moAtezo+Vem+Cobi mOS
Clinical problem

BRAF V600E test within days → dabrafenib + trametinib (ROAR 12-mo OS 51.7%). Neoadjuvant DTP (pembro+D+T) at MDACC: mOS 17 mo, surgical subset 63 mo. ATLEP: lenva+pembro ATC ORR 51.9%.

Immune / vaccine rationale

Highest TMB in thyroid (~6 mut/Mb), inflammatory infiltrate, T-cell exclusion reversible by MAPK inhibition. CT-antigens MAGE-A 62%, MAGE-C1 57%, GAGE 67%, NY-ESO-1 14%.

Available data

GSE76039 (33 ATC), GSE33630 (11 ATC), GSE232237 (50 thyroid cancers including ATC), MDACC FAST registry-linked NGS.

Translational opportunity

Highest-priority histology for personalized neoantigen mRNA + MAGE-A4 TCR-T + checkpoint backbone. BRAF/MEK inhibition opens the immunopeptidome window.

MTC · ~2–3% · 75% sporadic / 25% MEN2

Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma

0.7TMB mut/Mb
ColdTME state
HR 0.28LIBRETTO-531 selper vs cabo/vand
Clinical problem

RET-driven biology. LIBRETTO-531: selpercatinib first-line vs cabo/vand HR 0.28 — new SoC. Calcitonin/CEA biomarkers; MEN2B M918T thyroidectomy in first 6 months of life.

Immune / vaccine rationale

Immune-excluded "cold" microenvironment (Pozdeyev 2021), low TMB — but lineage antigens (calcitonin, CEA, GFRA4) are restricted and tractable. CGRP shapes the suppressive niche.

Available data

TCGA-MTC fragments, Agrawal Cell 2013, Ji Nat Commun 2015, MSK-MetTropism MTC subset, ARROW + LIBRETTO-001 translational.

Translational opportunity

GFRA4 CAR-T (Bhoj 2021) + selpercatinib priming + yeast-CEA / calcitonin DC vaccine prime → anti-PD-L1 release. Lineage-target stack with PD readouts (Ctn, CEA).

Why deterministic

Why a wetlab plan, not just a number

Reagent-level resolution

Honest correction

The widely-cited HLA-A*02:01 BRAF-V600E peptide GLATEKSRWS is not robustly validated by mass-spec immunopeptidomics in thyroid. Validated BRAF-V600E T-cell biology is dominated by CD4⁺ HLA-DQB1*0303/0302 restriction (Veatch JCI 2018). Lumenix flags this as a caution — not a pass.

06 · Immune Readiness Layer

Immune Readiness ≠ Response Prediction.

Pooled thyroid TME analysis (n=415, 8 cohorts) + pan-cancer ICI direction-invariance audit. Every immune score is treated as a hypothesis until it passes cross-cohort direction-invariance, HLA/neoantigen architecture, and thyroid-specific validation.
⚠

Hypothesis-grade only — not a clinical predictor.

Lumenix immunogenomics layer outputs research-grade ICI-readiness, immunogenomic vulnerability prioritization, and vaccine-combination hypothesis generation. It does not output thyroid ICI response prediction, clinical treatment recommendation, patient selection biomarkers, or validated ICI sensitivity. The boundary is enforced at every score, every figure, every interpretation.
