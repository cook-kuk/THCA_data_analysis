1 · Why THCA?

Thyroid cancer is the canonical "dark cancer" for immunogenomic study. Three properties matter:

  • Lowest TMB of major solid tumors (~0.5 mut/Mb) — almost no neoantigens by mutation count. Standard ICI response baseline is essentially nil.
  • Driver-mutation-dominated — BRAF V600E in ~60% of papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC), RET fusions in ~10%, NTRK fusions in ~5%. A small set of recurrent peptide targets.
  • Hashimoto-overlap subgroup (HT-PTC) — patients with concomitant Hashimoto's thyroiditis show 5× higher tertiary lymphoid structure (TLS) density and a different immunogenomic profile (v17 D5-P6 BCR clonal+TLS finding: TLS d=+1.96, IGHV clonality d>0.5). This sub-population may be ICI-responsive.

Figure 1 · TMB landscape — THCA at the bottom (darkest) end of the immunogenomic spectrum, three orders of magnitude below melanoma.

The dark-cancer paradox: in TMB terms THCA looks like the worst possible target for neoantigen vaccines or ICI. Yet the Hashimoto-overlap PTC subgroup (HT-PTC) shows immune-active features — IFN-γ, HLA-II up-regulation, B-cell tertiary lymphoid structures, and clonal IGHV expansion. This is the central tension Paper 2 + Paper 3 address: which dark thyroid cancer is actually ICI-vulnerable, and how do we identify it without TMB?

★ Per-allele finding (Q-X analysis, n=115): HLA-A*24:02 has per-allele AUROC = 0.74 ± 0.03 — the highest of any single HLA in our benchmark. This means: when you train a model only on A*24:02 peptides, the prediction quality is high. The Korean K2/Bundang FFPE cohort is the missing dataset to scale this up.

4 · ICI vulnerability of dark thyroid cancer (Paper 3)

Paper 3 (Track A FROZEN) found that anaplastic thyroid carcinoma (ATC) — the most aggressive thyroid cancer subtype — has a paradoxical immunogenomic signature compared to its differentiated counterparts (DTC, PTC, FTC):

Cross-axis: RAI-refractoriness is on the same axis as partial-ATC immunogenomic phenotype — meaning the patients who fail standard radioiodine therapy are immunologically heading toward ATC, and may be ICI-vulnerable in a way that would not show up on TMB alone.

5 · Synthetic lethality framing

Two angles emerge from combining Paper 3 (ICI vulnerability axis) with Paper 5 (neoantigen vaccine benchmark):

Synthetic lethality A · BRAF V600E + ICI: BRAF V600E is in 60% of PTC. The mutation creates a constitutively active MAPK signal that promotes immune-suppressive TME. BRAF inhibitor + neoantigen vaccine + anti-PD-1 — three-component combo could be synergistic where any one alone fails.

Synthetic lethality B · HT-overlap + ICI: HT-PTC subgroup has TLS, IFN-γ, B-cell clonality (Paper 2 finding) — already partially "primed" for immunity. ICI + neoantigen vaccine in HT-PTC could exploit existing tumor-reactive memory.

6 · Why THCA paper portfolio is coherent

  • Paper 1 DM1 identifies the 56-patient sub-B cluster (96% driver-mutation-negative) — the "dark" thyroid cancer.
  • Paper 2 HT-isolated identifies the immune-active HT-PTC subgroup — the part of dark cancer that may respond to ICI.
  • Paper 3 ICI vulnerability shows the immunogenomic axis from DTC → post-RAI → ATC — providing the molecular target for ICI selection.
  • Paper 4 Korean GD HLA brings Asian-specific allele context — needed for any Korean clinical translation.
  • Paper 5 neoantigen benchmark (this work) provides the prediction infrastructure — the "what actually matters" for translating any thyroid neoantigen finding into a vaccine candidate.

Each paper individually is solid. Combined, they form a complete pipeline: identify dark cancer (P1) → stratify by immune state (P2) → predict ICI response axis (P3) → contextualize for Korean patients (P4) → prioritize neoantigen vaccine candidates with leakage-aware benchmark (P5). This is the integrated thyroid-cancer immunogenomic platform.

8 · Honest limitations

  • THCA-relevant master corpus is small (~3k records); most are TSNAdb predicted-only (L0 evidence level).
  • BRAF V600E peptides have very few experimentally validated immunogenicity records in our corpus (n=3).
  • Korean cohort (K2/Bundang) is gated and may not arrive in time for current submission cycle.
  • ICI vulnerability axis (Paper 3) is based on bulk RNA-seq deconvolution, not single-cell or patient-matched ICI response data.
  • Synthetic-lethality combo trials are speculative; no preclinical model run in this session.
