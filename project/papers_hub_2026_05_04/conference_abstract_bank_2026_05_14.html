Paper 1 × AACR (300 words)

AACR Annual Meeting · structured · 300-word · Dec deadline · Seungho Cook (presenting) · Hyung-won Yu (corresponding)

DM1 dark-matter axis defines a thyrocyte-intrinsic silenced module orthogonal to BRAF/RAS drivers and prognostic across 11/33 cancer lineages

Background. [author opening sentence — voice-protected, 본인 키보드.] Papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) is among the most overdiagnosed cancers, yet a subset of mutation-negative PTC progresses to dedifferentiation. We hypothesized a non-driver "dark-matter" axis (DM1) silenced in thyrocyte-intrinsic fashion. Methods. Eight DM1 panel genes were derived by RandomForest ranking on a curated 55-gene pool with drivers excluded by design (n=386 datasets, 5 reproducibility zones, leave-one-dataset-out ComBat). We validated across TCGA-THCA (n=505), Lee ATC/Normal (n=27/18), Mun proteomic cohort (n=336), and HM450 methylation (Round 4, polarity-corrected). Pan-cancer prognostic mapping used 11,069 TCGA tumors × 33 lineages. ICI signal was tested in 4 cohorts (n=421). Results. DM1 panel d=+0.67 pooled across 12 contrasts (I²=96%); per-gene mean |d| ranged 0.72 (SLC5A5) to 1.33 (TG), excluding single-gene dominance. TERT-promoter recovery (n=36) gave OS HR=7.57 [2.74, 20.91] p=9.3e-5. Proteomic replication (Mun n=336) showed thyroid_diff d=-1.91 and myeloid d=+1.52 (7/7 sign-match). HM450 corrected polarity gave DM1=HIGH-β/silenced (cPTC d=+1.50, PDTC/ATC d=+3.47). Pan-cancer Cox: 10/33 lineages prognostic FDR<0.1; LGG DSS HR=44.7, LUAD DSS HR=19, UCEC inverse. DepMap (n=1,141 lines): MYC d=-0.50 p=1e-11; PRISM top 15 = all MAPK inhibitors. ICI 4-cohort sign-consistent (DM1_inflam) with HLA-I OR=1.35 p=0.014. Conclusion. DM1 is a thyrocyte-intrinsic, driver-orthogonal silenced axis with replicated multi-omics signal, TERT-bounded survival hazard, and pan-cancer prognostic transfer. The axis suggests MAPK-pathway vulnerability as the lead therapeutic hypothesis.

Paper 1 × ASCO (clinical, 2,000 char)

ASCO Annual Meeting · clinical · 2,000 char · Feb deadline · table allowed

A driver-orthogonal silenced gene panel (DM1) stratifies overall survival in thyroid cancer and 10 additional solid tumors

Background. [author opening sentence — voice-protected.] Among low-risk papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) patients, a minority progresses to dedifferentiated disease; predictive markers remain limited. Methods. We trained an 8-gene panel (DM1) by RandomForest ranking on a 55-gene pool with drivers excluded by construction. Validation spans TCGA-THCA (n=505), proteomic (n=336), and 4 ICI cohorts (n=421). Pan-cancer Cox prognostic mapping used 11,069 TCGA tumors × 33 lineages. Results. TERT-promoter mutation, recovered in 36 of 505 TCGA-THCA cases (cBioPortal thca_tcga_pub study), conferred OS HR=7.57 [2.74, 20.91] p=9.3e-5. The DM1 panel achieved AUC ≥ 0.85 across all cohorts (TCGA 0.90, Lee ATC/Normal 0.97, Mun 0.95). Pan-cancer Cox identified 10/33 lineages with FDR<0.1 (LGG DSS HR=44.7, LUAD DSS HR=19, UCEC inverse). DepMap PRISM top 15 dependencies in DM1-high lines were all MAPK-pathway inhibitors (AZD-0364 MEK FDR=6e-7). ICI 4-cohort meta gave HLA-I OR=1.35 p=0.014 with composite sign-consistent in 4/4. Conclusions. DM1 panel, paired with TERT promoter status, is a clinically actionable stratifier in thyroid cancer with cross-lineage prognostic transfer and a MAPK-pathway-druggable hypothesis. We propose biomarker-guided MEK/ERK inhibitor pilots in DM1-high mutation-negative dedifferentiation risk.

H&E-only projection of a transcriptomic dark-matter axis (DM1) in thyroid cancer with leave-one-center-out external generalization

Background. [author opening sentence — voice-protected, 본인 키보드.] Whole-slide histology contains latent signal beyond morphologic class; we tested whether a transcriptomic dark-matter axis (DM1, 8 genes) is recoverable from H&E alone. Methods. UNI foundation embeddings + CLAM MIL classifier were trained to project the DM1 axis using TCGA-THCA frozen slides (n=59 case-level mapping). The closure that "image-DM1 is architecture-bound" was overturned by ViT-L+CLAM gating, yielding PASS_LAUNCH_PAPER2 (2026-05-08). A dark-matter transcriptomic axis can be projected onto routine H&E with center-holdout generalization, suggesting an H&E-first decision-support pathway for mutation-negative dedifferentiation risk stratification. Korean H&E external gate is the next blocker.

HLA class II up-regulation in Hashimoto-overlap PTC defines a dark-thyroid immunogenomic phenotype

Background. [author opening sentence — voice-protected.] PTC arising on a background of Hashimoto thyroiditis (HT) is suspected to carry an immunogenomic signature distinct from PTC alone. Methods. DM1 sub-B (n=56, 96% mutation-negative) is the TCGA equivalent of K2 NBNR cohort + 4× Hashimoto-like rate. Conclusion. HT-overlap PTC defines a discrete immunogenomic phenotype with HLA-II up, IFN-γ activation, TLS structure, and antigen-driven B cell response — distinct from PTC alone, and transferable to a TCGA-defined sub-B niche. The signature gates a Cell Reports Medicine / JCI Insight reach without prospective Bundang cohort.
