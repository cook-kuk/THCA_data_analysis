5-minute EN review pitch — pre-emptive defense

Let me give you the honest version. Our work centers on a driver-orthogonal 8-gene dark matter axis (DM1) in thyroid cancer, locked across four independent layers — RNA, protein, phospho, and methylation. TERT⁺ DM1⁺ patients carry an OS HR of 7.57 (95% CI 2.74-20.91, p=9.3e-5). AUCs are 0.90 (TCGA), 0.97 (Lee ATC vs Normal), and 0.95 (Mun 2025 proteogenomics, n=336). Ten of twelve panel contrasts pass FDR<0.01, and the axis is prognostic in 10 non-thyroid cancer lineages (LGG, LUAD, GBM, PRAD, and others). DepMap nominates MYC (d=-0.50, p=1e-11) and NAMPT (d=-0.45, p=1.5e-9) as druggable dependencies.

I will preempt the three questions you are likely to ask.

Q3 — Robustness: our permutation null is p=0.29 — honest and not lucky. Thyroid-matched null is p=0.007, which is the fair pool. Label-permutation null is p<10⁻⁴. Knock-out n-1 floor is d=1.54, so no single gene dominates. We are transparent about these.

Q9 — Reviewer prep / HLA-II null: in Phase C v2 across four ICI cohorts (n=421), HLA-I shows OR=1.35 (p=0.014), checkpoint OR=1.32 (p=0.020), cytolytic OR=1.27 (p=0.046), and DM1_inflam is sign-consistent in 4/4 cohorts. However, HLA-II is null (OR=1.02, p=0.84). We disclose this and frame HLA-I as load-bearing, not HLA-II. We do not claim what we did not find.

Q14 — Cross-cohort heterogeneity: the MAPK × Panel-8 correlation pooled across 5 cohorts is ρ=-0.327 (n=1,287), with I²=72.9%. This heterogeneity is predicted by the model (v12 prediction confirmed by v14): HT-route GSE286332 decouples from MAPK, and advanced GSE76039 saturates. We provide per-cohort breakdown rather than treating heterogeneity as noise.

On histology (Paper 2): we report UNI center-holdout LOTO AUC=0.852, with the TSS-only-LR confound (AUC 1.000) explicitly retired as leakage diagnostic, not biology. ViT-L LOTO 0.308 is disclosed as honest negative — encoder dependence is named, not hidden. N=59 mean AUC 0.83±0.14, pooled AUC 0.75. K2 H&E external is the gate; if it fails, the paper folds into Paper 1 supplementary.

On HLA #1: GSE286332 (Korean PTC vs PTC+HT, n=18) shows HLA-II d=+3.65, IFN-γ FDR=2e-4, TLS d=+1.96. We acknowledge n=18 single cohort and counter it with D4-P2 TCGA transfer (DM2 enrichment OR up to 5×, p=6e-10) plus the DM1 sub-B NBNR cluster (n=56, 96% mutation-negative).

On spatial (GSE250521): our v15-v18 spatial-rescue analysis honestly fails to recover the MAPK×Panel anti-correlation in epithelial subsets (pooled ρ=+0.034 after epithelial / QC / microenvironment adjustment). This is a strong reviewer caveat, not a hidden negative. We use it as a limitation, not as mechanism support.

On translational claim: we do not claim a thyroid ICI response predictor. We claim vulnerability and readiness only, with HLA-I load-bearing and HLA-II disclosed null. The Phase E v2 transferability (32 cancers × 10,978 samples, 10/32 prognostic) is a clear lineage-portable claim, not a SOTA predictor.

Our preprint will land on bioRxiv first to preserve priority. The Korean K2 PRJEB11591 (n=260) RNA-seq + arcasHLA imputation is publicly anchored. Yu lab's K-Thyro 62 CTC/EMT cohort provides functional validation. The grant under review (Samsung 30B-KRW, Yu-Cook dual PI) extends the axis to causal inference but does not gate publication.

Portfolio honest grade: B+. One A-grade paper (Paper 1), two B/B+ papers (Paper 2, HLA #1), one C+ grant (Samsung), one D-grade backlog (HLA #2). We do not inflate. We disclose limitations up front. The axis is real, lineage-portable, and druggable.

Q1 — "왜 NC급(Nature Commun)이라고 보는가?"

Paper 1은 4-pillar로 잠겼고 (RNA + 단백질 + phospho + 메틸레이션) TERT⁺ OS HR=7.57이다. 단순한 single-cohort 신호가 아니라 10개 다른 암 lineage에서 prognostic이고 DepMap에서 MYC/NAMPT가 druggable. permutation null p=0.29 honest, thyroid-matched p=0.007. 이 정도 외부 검증이 붙은 thyroid driver-orthogonal axis는 thyroid 분야에 전례가 없음. NPJ는 floor venue, NComm은 reach venue. Nature Cancer까지 reach 가능하지만 cover letter para 1 voice section 작성 후 결정.

Q2 — "K2 H&E access 안 되면 어떻게 할 거냐?"

Plan B 4가지가 잠겨 있음. (1) Paper 2를 Paper 1 ST1으로 흡수 — Paper 1 단독으로 NComm급 유지. (2) LUAD/BRCA methods route — UNI/CLAM audit framework를 다른 cancer로 transfer해 method paper로 NPJ Digital Med 권역. (3) Honest closure — submission 안 하고 "thyroid H&E pilot N=59 internal"으로만 reference. (4) TCIA TCGA-THCA slide subset 대체 (n≈100, cohort confound disclosed). R1이 critical이지만 portfolio 전체는 anti-fragile.

Q3 — "Samsung 30억 선정 가능성은?"

현재 정직 60-70%. 방향(causal inference + K-Thyro 62명 + Yu lab + Mun 2025 + DM1 axis)은 정확하지만 5 약점(small-N · single-PI · causal claim ladder · access depth · novelty positioning)에 대한 답안이 미완. access 4건 (cohort, biospecimen, follow-up, imaging) 확정 + external 1건 확정 + WP2 retrofit (E-value + tipping point + power analysis) 추가 시 선정 가능성 60-70%로 stable. Yu-Cook dual PI 구조라 single-PI exposure는 이미 redundancy로 막힘. 약점 답안 작성 우선순위는 voice section 다음.
