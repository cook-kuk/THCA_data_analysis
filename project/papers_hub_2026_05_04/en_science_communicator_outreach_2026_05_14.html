AP / Reuters health desk outreach

news-wire style · 300 words · neutral factual tone

Template

subjectKorean researchers identify third molecular axis predicting thyroid-cancer survival — multi-cohort validation, Nature Communications upcoming addressed toAP Health desk / Reuters Health desk bodySEOUL — Researchers from Seoul National University Bundang Hospital and the Cook Laboratory have identified a third molecular axis in thyroid cancer that predicts patient survival independently of the two long-known drivers, BRAF V600E and RAS mutations. The findings, accepted for publication in Nature Communications, were validated across four independent cohorts including 260 Korean patients. The axis, named DM1 (Dark Matter 1), is defined by coordinated silencing of eight thyroid-specific differentiation genes. Patients with high DM1 silencing and a TERT promoter mutation faced a 7.57-fold higher overall-survival hazard than those with low DM1 silencing (95% CI 2.74 to 20.91; p=9.3e-5). Drug-screening analyses of 1,141 cancer cell lines found that the top 15 most-active compounds in DM1-high cells were all MAPK-pathway inhibitors, suggesting a possible target for clinical trials in BRAF/RAS-negative progressive thyroid cancers. DM1 was also prognostic in 10 of 33 other cancer types, including low-grade glioma (disease-specific-survival hazard ratio 44.7), lung adenocarcinoma (19), and melanoma (0.78, protective direction). The authors caution that DM1 is a research-stage prognostic biomarker, not a diagnostic device. They disclose nine null results alongside the positive findings, including a null phosphoproteomic layer and unsuccessful spatial-transcriptomic recovery. Prospective validation is under way in Bundang SNUH's BABA thyroid surgery cohort of approximately 5,000 Korean patients. Korea has one of the world's highest thyroid-cancer diagnosis rates, with about 88.8 percent of the Korean K2 cohort falling into a low-risk microcarcinoma sub-group — a context in which a prognostic biomarker that distinguishes truly low-risk from progression-prone cases has direct clinical value. contactBundang SNUH press office · Cook lab media contact

STAT News — biotech-medical angle

technical depth higher · 500-700 words · biotech decision-maker audience

Template

subjectHow a Korean thyroid-cancer cohort opened a new target-discovery lane — DM1 axis, MAPK sensitivity, and a 5,000-patient prospective validation in progress addressed toSTAT News health-tech / biotech desk bodyIn a multi-pillar study accepted for publication in Nature Communications, researchers from Seoul National University Bundang Hospital and the Cook Laboratory describe DM1, a molecular axis in thyroid cancer defined by coordinated silencing of eight differentiation genes (TG, TPO, TSHR, DIO1, DIO2, PAX8, FOXE1, SLC26A4, and SLC5A5). The panel emerged from a driver-exclusion design — explicitly removing BRAF V600E and RAS-mutant tumors before random-forest ranking — and therefore captures a signal orthogonal to the standard MAPK and PI3K driver classes. Cohorts and analytical layers. The authors validate DM1 across four independent cohorts (TCGA, Lee ATC/normal, Mun 2025 proteomic n=336, Korean K2 PRJEB11591 n=260) and three molecular layers: RNA-seq (12 contrasts, 10 of 12 FDR less than 0.01), proteomics (sign-match 7 of 7, AUC 0.95), and HM450 methylation (DM1-high equals high-beta silenced; PDTC-vs-ATC effect size 3.47). The phosphoproteomic layer is reported as a null and disclosed openly. Prognostic anchor. Among TERT-promoter-mutant patients, DM1-high status was associated with an overall-survival hazard ratio of 7.57 (95% CI 2.74 to 20.91; p=9.3e-5), and the DM1 / TERT composite reached a time-dependent ROC of 0.83 at one year. DM1 was strongly associated with the follicular-variant PTC phenotype (odds ratio 17.9, p=3e-31). Korean K2 carriers fell 88.8 percent into a low-risk DM2 sub-group, quantifying the Korean overdiagnosis paradigm in molecular terms. Target lane. A DepMap and PRISM analysis across 1,141 cancer cell lines found MYC essentiality (d=-0.50, p=1e-11) and NAMPT essentiality (d=-0.45, p=1.5e-9) preferentially in DM1-high lines. The top 15 most-active drugs in DM1-high cells were all MAPK-pathway inhibitors, with AZD-0364 (a MEK inhibitor) reaching FDR=6e-7. The DM1-by-MAPK-inhibitor LFC correlation was -0.236, indicating directional consistency at moderate effect size. The authors frame this as a target-trial-design anchor for BRAF/RAS-negative progressive thyroid cancer, not a label expansion. Pan-cancer breadth. Across TCGA's 11,069 samples and 33 lineages, DM1 reached Cox prognostic FDR less than 0.1 in 10 cancers, including low-grade glioma (disease-specific-survival hazard ratio 44.7), lung adenocarcinoma (19), and prostate adenocarcinoma. Melanoma was protective (HR 0.78) and endometrial cancer reversed (an "inverse" honest disclosure). Phase E lineage-portable DM1 reproduced the signal across a 10,978-sample portable version. Honest disclosures. Nine null results are listed alongside the positive findings, including the null phosphoproteomic layer, an HLA-II ICI-cohort null (OR 1.02, p=0.84), and unsuccessful spatial-transcriptomic recoveries across v15 through v18 attempts. The authors hold the line that DM1 is a prognostic biomarker and trial-design anchor, not a diagnostic device. Prospective validation. Bundang SNUH's BABA (bilateral axillo-breast approach) cohort, with approximately 5,000 Korean patients and an attached 62-patient CTC/EMT precision sub-cohort, is the next validation step. A companion Paper 2 image-based DM1 model (UNI foundation + CLAM, leave-one-TSS-out AUC 0.852) is awaiting external H&E validation on the same cohort. contactBundang SNUH press office · Cook lab media contact

Nature press release pipeline — optional / coordinated

journal-driven press release · institutional release mirrors AAAS deposit

Nature Communications operates its own press release pipeline through Springer Nature. If the journal opts to issue a release, we coordinate the institutional Bundang SNUH press release to mirror — not contradict — the journal language. If the journal does not issue a release (some Nat Commun papers receive only a featured-article promotion), the AAAS EurekAlert deposit becomes the primary EN entry point.

Coordination rule. The journal release, the institutional release, and the EurekAlert deposit must all use the same hazard ratios, the same number of cohorts, and the same null-disclosure paragraph. About 분당 SNUH / Cook lab — institutional boilerplate

two boilerplate blocks · drop-in to any press release

Boilerplate · Bundang SNUH

Seoul National University Bundang Hospital

Seoul National University Bundang Hospital (분당서울대병원) is a tertiary academic medical center affiliated with Seoul National University College of Medicine, serving the southern Seoul metropolitan area. Its Department of Endocrine Surgery, led by Professor Hyung-Won Yu, operates a high-volume thyroid surgery program including the BABA (bilateral axillo-breast approach) cohort of approximately 5,000 patients, and a 62-patient CTC/EMT precision sub-cohort. Bundang SNUH has institutional review board oversight for the prospective validation of DM1 and operates a coordinated media-relations channel for research communication.

Boilerplate · Cook Lab

Cook Laboratory

The Cook Laboratory, led by Seungho Cook as Co-PI, develops agentic-AI, multi-omics, and quantum machine-learning pipelines for cancer genomics. The lab's work on thyroid cancer (Paper 1 on DM1 molecular dark matter, Paper 2 on H&E-based DM1 image biomarkers, Paper 3 on immune-checkpoint-inhibitor vulnerability, and Paper 4 on Korean HLA distributions) is conducted in close partnership with Bundang SNUH and contributes the computational analytical layer to the joint program. The lab discloses honest null results alongside positive findings as a matter of policy.
