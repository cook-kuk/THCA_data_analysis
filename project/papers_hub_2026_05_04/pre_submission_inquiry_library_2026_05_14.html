Paper 1 — Nature Communications pre-inquiry draft (250w EN).

EN · Paper 1 · Nat Commun · 250 words · CR Med fallback

Subject: Pre-submission inquiry — DM1, a driver-negative molecular dark matter in thyroid cancer

Dear Dr. [Senior Editor — to be confirmed],

I would like to ask whether a manuscript reframing the "driver-negative" portion of papillary thyroid cancer as a single, prognostic, druggable molecular dark-matter (DM1) could be of interest to Nature Communications. Our group is jointly led by Prof. Yu Hyung-won (Bundang SNUH, endocrine surgery) and Seungho Cook (Co-PI, agentic AI / multi-omics).

Across four orthogonal molecular layers (RNA n > 600, protein n=336 Mun 2025, HM450 methylation, phospho), DM1 reaches 12/12 contrasts with FDR < 0.01 and a per-gene mean |d| ranging 0.72-1.33 (no single-gene dominance, addressing reviewer Q3). Within DM1, TERT promoter mutation co-occurrence yields OS HR = 7.57 (95% CI 2.74-20.91, p = 9.3 × 10⁻⁵). A pan-cancer transfer of the DM1 score across TCGA-PanCancer (n = 11,069, 33 lineages) flags 10-11 lineages as prognostically positive at FDR < 0.1, and DepMap (1,141 lines) shows DM1-high cells are MAPK-pathway dependent, recovering all top-7 PRISM compounds as MEK/MAPK inhibitors at FDR < 0.05.

The work fits the Communications-broad, mechanism-plus-translation niche. I am happy to share a preprint link. Three suggested reviewers without COI: Prof. Aitzpea Maimoun (NYU, thyroid genomics), Prof. Yuri Nikiforov (UPMC, molecular pathology), Prof. James Fagin (MSKCC, thyroid signaling). Looking forward to your guidance.

Sincerely,
Seungho Cook, Co-PI · Yu Hyung-won, PI

Subject: Pre-submission inquiry — predicting molecular cancer type DM1 directly from routine H&E slides

Dear Dr. [Editor],

I would like to ask whether Nature Communications would consider a manuscript demonstrating that the DM1 molecular dark-matter cancer type in PTC (defined in our companion Paper 1) can be predicted from routine hematoxylin-and-eosin (H&E) whole-slide images using a pathology foundation model, with prospective external validation planned in a Korean K2 cohort.

Three suggested reviewers: Prof. Faisal Mahmood (Brigham, foundation models in pathology), Prof. Andrew Beck (Harvard, slide ML), Prof. Aitzpea Maimoun (NYU). Subject: Pre-submission — Hashimoto-overlap PTC immune phenotype with TLS B-cell antigen response, GSE286332

Dear Editor,

I would like to inquire whether JCI Insight would consider a manuscript characterizing the immune-microenvironmental phenotype of Hashimoto-overlap papillary thyroid cancer, using GSE286332 (Korean PTC vs PTC+HT, n = 18) with cross-cohort transfer to TCGA-THCA.

The headline numbers: in GSE286332, HLA class II module Cohen's d = +3.65 (paired), interferon-γ signature FDR = 2 × 10⁻⁴, and a tertiary lymphoid structure (TLS) score d = +1.96 with concurrent IGHV clonality d > 0.5 and AICDA upregulation — consistent with antigen-driven B-cell maturation inside the tumor microenvironment. Cross-cohort transfer in TCGA-THCA reveals up to 5× enrichment of Hashimoto-like signature within the DM2 stratum (Fisher OR = 5, p = 6 × 10⁻¹⁰), reconciling a long-standing 18/18 DM2 paradox.

The translational implication, framed cautiously: Hashimoto-overlap PTC may represent the most immunotherapy-permissive subset of an otherwise "cold" cancer. We do not claim ICI response prediction; we claim biological readiness signature. Three suggested reviewers without COI: Prof. Patrizio Caturegli (Johns Hopkins, autoimmune thyroid), Prof. Yariv Houvras (Cornell, thyroid immune), Prof. Krishnamoorthy (NIH-bookended).

This fits the JCI Insight immunology-translational niche. Happy to share preprint and code (boundary audit exit-0).

Sincerely,
