★ Critical confounder finding (PP): the Histidine fraction signal that dominated XGBoost SHAP (|SHAP|=0.85, A-analysis Δ=+0.078, p=4.8e-22) nearly disappears when you stratify by HLA: per-allele Δf_H ranges from -0.003 to +0.003. The pooled signal was driven by HLA-imbalance correlations (which alleles have positive vs negative records), not by intrinsic biophysics. This is Simpson's paradox — the headline result of A-analysis was partially confounded. Aromatic Δ holds up better (still negative per-allele: -0.027 in A*02:01, -0.051 in A*11:01). Future model must use HLA-stratified training.
