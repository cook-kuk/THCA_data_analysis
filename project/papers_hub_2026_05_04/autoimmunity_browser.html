★ Safety filtering protocol
- Lev=0 (identical to self): EXCLUDE from any vaccine design.
- Lev=1 (1 substitution from self): EXCLUDE unless the substitution is at TCR-contact position (P3, P5, P7) AND there's wet-lab evidence of tumor specificity.
- Lev=2 (2 substitutions): REVIEW — likely safe but flag in vaccine design.
- Lev≥3: typically safe.
This is a conservative computational filter. Production vaccine design should additionally do: BLAST against UniProt human proteome, MHC-binding affinity comparison (mut vs wt), and TCR cross-reactivity prediction.
