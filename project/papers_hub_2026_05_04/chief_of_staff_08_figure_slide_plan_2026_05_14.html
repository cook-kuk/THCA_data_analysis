11 · Composition / color / typography rules

dark navy + gold accent · honest signals in sage green · red sparingly

Color palette

Background dark navy #071016 with panel #0e1924. Gold accent #ffd28a for key numbers and headings. Blue #8bbcff for secondary data. Sage #a9d8b8 for honest signals (positives that survived nulls). Red #ff8a6b sparingly for warnings / dead ends. Violet #c9a7ff for brand / vision.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond serif for cover headlines and figure titles. JetBrains Mono for axis labels, statistic anchors, and section meta. Inter / Noto Sans KR for body and Korean prose. Newsreader serif for lead paragraphs (quote-like).

Composition rules

Each figure carries one quantitative anchor in the title row. No more than 3 layers per panel. Caption ≤ 2 sentences for main; supplemental can go to 3. Always include sample N and statistical method. Honest nulls get their own panel, not buried.
