03Science Decision

Why Science, not Technology/ICT

Science identity

  • one disease: PTC
  • one perturbation: thyroidectomy
  • one transition: CTC epithelial → hybrid E/M → mesenchymal state
  • one anchor: matched tumor-normal NGS plus blood genetic signals
  • one primary endpoint: postoperative CTC-EMT state transition

Why not ICT

  • no proprietary CTC device
  • no validated AI primitive
  • no clinical deployment yet
  • AI is interpretation infrastructure, not the proposal identity
  • vendor sequencing is service, not novelty

Ruthless reviewer note

ICT로 제출하면 novelty가 algorithm/device가 아니라는 공격을 바로 맞는다. Science에서는 같은 약점이 오히려 장점이 된다. 질문이 좁고, perturbation이 명확하고, endpoint boundary가 방어 가능하기 때문이다.

Most dangerous attack

"CTC/cfDNA signal in early PTC may be noisy, nonspecific, or technically unrecoverable." The answer is not to overpromise. Preserve tissue-normal NGS and CTC phenotype as core; treat cfDNA and CTC-enriched NGS as staged genetic anchoring with explicit failure criteria.
