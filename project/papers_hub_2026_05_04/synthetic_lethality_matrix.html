1 · KRAS G12D vaccine + G12D inhibitor + anti-PD-1 (PAAD)

★ Vaccine armGADGVGKSAL · HLA-A*11:01 · 9-19% population coverage
Drug armMRTX1133-class G12D-specific inhibitor (preclinical)
ICI armanti-PD-1 (pembro/nivolumab) — releases T-cell exhaustion
Rationale: KRAS G12D drives ~40% of PAAD with HR=2.17 worst-survival. The vaccine creates the priming step that fails in PAAD's immune-excluded TME. G12D inhibitor unmasks tumor antigens. anti-PD-1 maintains T-cell expansion. Likely 3-strike strategy.
Preclinical (clinical trial running)

2 · KRAS G12C vaccine + sotorasib + anti-PD-L1 (LUAD)

★ Vaccine armGACGVGKSALT · HLA-A*02:01
Drug armSotorasib (Lumakras / AMG 510) — FDA-approved 2021
ICI armanti-PD-L1 (atezolizumab / durvalumab)
Rationale: LUAD G12C (40% of EGFR-WT LUAD, smoking signature) is the most-validated KRAS variant for combo therapy. Sotorasib monotherapy ORR ~37%. Vaccine + sotorasib + anti-PD-L1 is an emerging combo for relapsed/refractory disease.
Clinical (trials NCT03554083, NCT04117087)

3 · BRAF V600E vaccine + dabrafenib + anti-PD-1 (Melanoma + HT-PTC subset)

Vaccine armBRAF V600E peptide on HLA-DR or HLA-A*02:01 (limited corpus)
Drug armDabrafenib + trametinib (BRAF + MEK inhibition)
★ ICI armanti-PD-1 (most established combo)
Rationale: Melanoma — clinical efficacy established (~50% ORR for ICI mono; combo expected to push higher). For Hashimoto-overlap PTC subgroup (Paper 2 finding: TLS d=+1.96, IFN-γ FDR=2e-4) — preclinical hypothesis: HT-PTC is already immune-primed, BRAFi unmasks tumor, anti-PD-1 maintains response.
Computational

4 · TP53 R175H vaccine + APR-246 + anti-PD-1 (multi-cancer)

★ Vaccine armTP53 R175H peptide · multi-cancer (PAAD, COAD, OV, breast, lung)
Drug armAPR-246 (eprenetapopt) — TP53 reactivator (Phase 3)
ICI armanti-PD-1 / anti-PD-L1
Rationale: TP53 R175H is the most common TP53 mutation across many cancers (~5-10% of all TP53-mutant). Off-the-shelf vaccine candidate covers a huge patient population. APR-246 restores wild-type p53 function. Triple combo approaches "synthetic restoration".
Preclinical

5 · EGFR L858R vaccine + osimertinib + anti-PD-1 (Asian LUAD)

Vaccine armEGFR L858R peptide on HLA-A*24:02 (Korean common)
★ Drug armOsimertinib (Tagrisso) — covers L858R + T790M resistance
ICI armanti-PD-1 (osimertinib + ICI combo trials ongoing)
Rationale: Asian LUAD has ~50% EGFR mutation rate (vs 15% Western). Korean patients have HLA-A*24:02 (18.5%). Vaccine + osimertinib + anti-PD-1 in Asian-specific HLA-stratified trials is a high-priority Korean cohort opportunity.
Computational (early concept)

6 · ATC TP53 + BRAF combo + ICI (anaplastic thyroid)

Vaccine armMulti-epitope: TP53 R175H + BRAF V600E (concatemer)
Drug armBRAF inhibitor + MEK inhibitor + TP53 reactivator
★ ICI armanti-PD-1 (rationale: ATC has myeloid d=+2.53)
Rationale: ATC is the most aggressive thyroid cancer (mortality >90%) and has paradoxical myeloid-rich immune signature (Paper 3). The 4-component combo is a rescue strategy where ATC patients have no current effective therapy.

3 · Cancer × ICI response baseline

★ Why combo strategies are needed
ICI monotherapy response rates by cancer:
- Melanoma: ~50% (high TMB) — ICI alone often suffices
- LUAD: 15-30% (medium TMB) — combo improves outcomes
- COAD MSI-H: ~50%
- COAD MSS: ~5%
- PAAD: <5% (low TMB, immune-excluded) — ICI alone fails
- DTC / PTC: ~0% (lowest TMB) — ICI alone fails
- ATC: ~5-10% (myeloid-rich)

The cancers where ICI mono fails are exactly where vaccine + drug + ICI 3-arm combo might rescue response. This is the synthetic-lethality angle.

5 · Honest limitations

  • All combo strategies are computationally prioritized; clinical efficacy not proven for most.
  • HLA stratification depends on patient genotyping which is not always done.
  • Vaccine peptide selection requires our leakage-free benchmark + AlphaFold-Multimer validation (in progress).
  • Drug arm timing matters — sequential vs simultaneous administration affects outcome.
  • Autoimmunity risk increases with multi-arm combo — must filter peptides Lev≤1 to self.
  • Cost / accessibility / Korean clinical infrastructure must factor in.
