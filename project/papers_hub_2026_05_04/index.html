Immune Readiness ≠ Response Prediction

Pooled meta-analysis of 4 thyroid cohorts (n=415, 8 datasets) shows ATC has an inflamed-but-myeloid-suppressed, dedifferentiated immune state. A 2-cohort minimal DIAL audit (Hugo + Riaz pre-treatment melanoma ICI) shows pan-cancer ICI signatures hold sign-consistency in only 3 of 7 modules — direction-invariance is not established. The platform therefore treats every immune score as a hypothesis, not a prediction.
