Section 3 · Paper 1 — DM1 dark matter

Paper 1 acknowledgments block — TCGA · CPTAC · Mun 2025 · Landa cite save.

Cohort access

Cohort access

We acknowledge the patients and contributors of The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) Thyroid Cancer (THCA) and PanCancer cohorts (n=11,069 across 33 lineages); the Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium (CPTAC) for paired RNA/protein/methylation infrastructure; and the authors of Mun et al. 2025 (n=336 tumors) for the four-pillar protein cohort that allowed orthogonal protein-level replication of the DM1 axis. Institutional support

Individual contributors · cite save

We thank the authors of Mun et al. 2025 for direct correspondence on the proteomics layer. Cohort access

Quantum-inspired sidecar · honest framing

Quantum-inspired sidecar (Samsung proposal) — honest framing

The quantum machine-learning (QML) sidecar component included in the Samsung 30B KRW 5-year proposal (~3B KRW sidecar portion) makes no claim of clinical advantage at this time. We explicitly disclose that QML on current near-term quantum hardware (NISQ-era) has not been demonstrated to outperform classical machine learning on any clinically actionable thyroid-cancer task. The sidecar is included as an exploratory layer with pre-registered failure criteria; if pre-registered failure criteria are triggered, the sidecar will be retracted from the proposal with no penalty to the main classical-AI program.

Code availability

All analysis code is open-source under the THCA_data_analysis repository, hosted on the Cook lab github at https://github.com/[org]/THCA_data_analysis (release tag corresponding to each paper at acceptance). The repository includes: 4-pillar proteogenomic synthesis pipeline (Paper 1), UNI v1 + CLAM LOTO pipeline (Paper 2), arcasHLA RNA-seq HLA imputation pipeline (HLA #2), GSE286332 paired immune-phenotype pipeline (HLA #1), and the v18 agentic_research framework (5 patterns + 6 components, demo + 7 tests pass). Boundary-audit scripts at scripts/boundary_audit/ verify reproducibility floor (exit code 0 required before Paper 1 commits).

Compute environment

Compute environment

Pipelines were executed on a mix of RunPod RTX A6000 (48GB, $0.33/hr community cloud, thca-spark-dm-a6000-v4) and a 512GB Premium SSD mounted at /data on the analysis workstation. Disk-layout conventions (bind mounts at project/results and project/data; tmpdir at /data/thca/_tmp) are documented in the repository's CLAUDE.md. STAR / arcasHLA / GNU parallel jobs use --tmpdir /workspace/_tmp --compress to avoid /tmp overflow.
