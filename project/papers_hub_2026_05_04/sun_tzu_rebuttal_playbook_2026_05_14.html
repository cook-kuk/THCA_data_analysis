전제 — 적대적 reviewer를 가정한다

design assumption · pre-mortem · hostile by default

대부분의 reviewer는 친절하지만, Nature Communications 1st pass에서 reviewer 2를 적으로 만난다고 가정하고 설계한다. 5가지 hostile pattern을 미리 등록하면 답이 미리 준비된다. 방어가 아니라 선제 disclosure가 핵심.

Pattern 1 — Reviewer 2 wants you dead

attack vector · global rejection · "이 연구는 결함이 본질적이다"

Pattern 1 · existential threat

"본 연구는 8유전자 패널 selection bias 때문에 결론을 신뢰할 수 없습니다."

"The 8-gene panel was hand-picked from a curated 55-gene pool by RandomForest with drivers excluded by design. This is not unsupervised genome-wide discovery, and the central claim is therefore confounded."
Counter — invite the steelman, then exceed it reviewer의 비판을 더 강하게 만들어 인용하고, 그 위에서 답한다. "맞다 — 그래서 우리는 driver-exclusion forensic audit을 별도 페이지로 분리했고, knock-out n-1 floor d=1.54로 panel 안의 어떤 단일 gene도 dominate 하지 않음을 보였다. 또한 TDS-16 (검증된 standard) 과 ρ=0.954 superset capture를 통해 panel selection bias의 외부 anchor를 제공했다."
Evidence ready: 8-gene forensic audit · honest negatives wall · v17_8gene_pangenome_robustness · knock-out n-1 floor d=1.54 · TDS-16 superset ρ=0.954
Pattern 1 · existential threat (variant)

"K2 88.8% DM2 phenomenon은 Korean overdiagnosis artefact이지 phenotype이 아닙니다."

"What you call the Korean 'overdiagnosis paradigm' is just selection bias from a different screening culture. There is no biology here, only sociology."
Counter — double down on honest disclosure + mechanism "정확히 그것이 우리 paper의 핵심 frame이다. K2 88.8% DM2는 epidemiological context로 framed되며, biological claim은 별도 mechanism (RAS=98% DM1 vs BRAF=1% DM1 driver split, MAPK-pathway-driven methylation β=0.27-0.39)으로 carry된다. Korean overdiagnosis page에서 epi context를 정직히 disclosure했다."
Evidence ready: Korean overdiagnosis epi · driver split atlas · deconv v5-v12 (MAPK-pathway-driven methylation, BRAF V600E β=0.37 ≈ RET fusion β=0.39)

Pattern 2 — Cherry-pick against you

attack vector · selective null citation · "당신이 안 보여준 것이 진짜다"

Pattern 2 · cherry-pick

"GSE250521 spatial에서 MAPK × Panel anti-correlation은 +0.034로 사실상 zero이다."

"Your spatial validation actually fails — the residualized rho is +0.034, indistinguishable from the random module median (+0.099). You're publishing a positive when the data say null."
Counter — pre-publish all rejections, then reframe the scope "감사하다. 정확히 이 null은 honest_negatives_wall (deconv v15-v18)에 사전 등록되어 있다. 우리 main mechanism claim은 bulk RNA × methylation (TCGA+Lee MAPK×Panel ρ=−0.29/−0.40)에 anchored되며, spatial은 reviewer reserve로만 claim된다. v18 closure: KNN lag rescue도 실패 (raw KNN1-6 +0.090, residual +0.033). 우리는 이 caveat을 paper 내에서 명시적으로 disclosure한다."
Evidence ready: honest negatives wall (v15-v18 closure pre-published) · deconv v17 percentile 0.545 · v18 KNN1-6 raw +0.090 / residual +0.033
Pattern 2 · cherry-pick (variant)

"Round 7 permutation null p=0.288 — 당신 panel은 lucky thyroid signature일 뿐이다."

"The panel-specificity null collapses to non-significance under proper permutation."
Counter — show the n-1 floor + thyroid-matched null "Round 7 0.288은 unfair pool이었다 (모든 gene set에 대해). Round 8 thyroid-matched null에서 p=0.007, label-perm null p<10⁻⁴, TDS-16 ρ=0.954 (8⊂TDS-16, 90% capture), knock-out n-1 floor d=1.54. 우리는 reviewer가 더 가혹한 null을 요구할 것을 예상해 Round 8을 사전에 실행했다."
Evidence ready: dm1_round8_2026_05_08 · thyroid-matched null p=0.007 · n-1 floor d=1.54 · TDS-16 superset capture

Pattern 3 — Reviewer demands impossible external

attack vector · impossible-to-supply external · "K2 H&E가 없으면 publish 못 한다"

Pattern 3 · impossible demand

"외부 (분당 SNUH) H&E n≥200 validation 없이는 image-DM1 paper를 publish할 수 없다."

"Without independent histology validation from at least 200 external slides, the image-DM1 claim is unfalsifiable."
Counter — scope the K2 gate + offer methods angle as fallback "정확히 우리는 K2 H&E external validation을 main paper의 단일 결정적 gate로 미리 등록했다 (track2 command room). 만약 K2 access가 timely하지 않으면, paper는 자동으로 methods-route (LUAD/BRCA transfer demonstration)로 reframed된다. 우리는 reviewer가 main claim ladder를 부수면 자동으로 한 단계 아래로 내려가도록 venue ladder를 사전 등록했다."
Evidence ready: K2 H&E single decisive gate · venue ladder · Paper 2 ST1 fallback (P1 absorb) · methods-route LUAD/BRCA transfer demonstration
Pattern 3 · impossible demand (variant)

"Pan-Asian HLA Paper 4는 일본 BBJ + 중국 CGM raw access 없이는 의미 없다."

"Comparing Korean GD HLA against a public AFND pool is not a Pan-Asian comparison. You need patient-level raw data."
Counter — disclose the gating + propose a phased pre-registration "Paper 4는 backlog (의도된 정렬)이며, 4/4 gating으로 Paper 1 bioRxiv + Paper 2 A/B/C 완료까지 진행하지 않는다. Pan-Asian access는 phased pre-registration로 통과 — phase 1: AFND pool comparison (현재), phase 2: BBJ/CGM patient-level access (gating 후). 우리는 무리하지 않는다."
Evidence ready: v19_paper4_GD_backlog (4/4 gating 명시) · HLA Pan-Asian strategy · arcasHLA imputation pipeline ready

Pattern 4 — Reviewer cites your competitor

attack vector · adversarial citation · "X paper가 이미 했다"

Pattern 4 · competitor cite

"Krishnamoorthy 2025 Nat Commun에서 이미 8-gene이 ATC overlap이다."

"This work is largely scooped by Krishnamoorthy et al. 2025 Nat Commun, which already reported 8/8 gene overlap with ATC dedifferentiation."
Counter — cite-save model · never accept misattribution "정중하지만 확인 필요하다. Krishnamoorthy 2025는 4가지 fact 모두 misattributed되어 있음을 우리 cite-save model에서 사전에 식별했다. 정확한 cite는 Landa 2016 JCI 126(3):1052-1066 — 5/8 ATC overlap을 보고했다 (8/8 아님). 우리는 reverse-causality 차단 3-layer를 별도 audit에서 보였다 (8-gene = driver-exclusion forensic audit). 두 결과는 양립 가능하며, 우리 paper는 cross-cohort 4-pillar replication까지 가져 새 contribution이다."
Evidence ready: v17_landa2016_cite_save · Landa 2016 JCI 126(3):1052-1066 · 8-gene forensic audit · bibliography map · reverse-causality 차단 3-layer
Pattern 4 · competitor cite (variant)

"Foundation model paper 다 똑같다 — UNI vs Virchow vs CONCH vs Phikon"

"Image foundation models for pathology are now commoditized; UNI vs others is irrelevant detail."
Counter — pivot from FM to phenotype + transfer "Paper 2의 contribution은 'UNI가 ViT-L보다 좋다'가 아니라, H&E 단일 modality로 RNA-level molecular dark matter (DM1)를 portable하게 prediction한다는 것이다. UNI는 단지 fragility-reduced backbone — head-to-head는 transparency 목적. UNI-final overall LOTO 0.852 vs ViT-L RAS LOTO 0.308 (artefact) — 우리는 어떤 FM이든 phenotype을 capture할 수 있는지의 frame이지 FM 자체 contest가 아니다."
Evidence ready: image FM head-to-head · paper2_image_dm1.html · UNI-final LOTO 0.852 · ViT-L RAS LOTO 0.308 fail

Pattern 5 — Reviewer changes goalposts

attack vector · post-hoc threshold shift · "이 기준으로는 충분 안 한다"

Pattern 5 · goalpost shift

"AUC 0.852는 clinical decision support로 충분하지 않다 — ≥0.90 필요."

"An AUC of 0.852 is below clinical-grade thresholds in modern AI pathology literature. Acceptance bar should be ≥0.90."
Counter — log original threshold + show registered plan "0.852는 leave-one-TSS-out (LOTO) overall, 즉 가장 엄격한 site-confound-removed 평가다. 같은 dataset에 OOF는 0.874, RAS-like LOTO ceiling은 0.718 (정직). 우리는 paper 2 design 시점에 'clinical-grade'가 아니라 'molecular-phenotype-discovery' 위치를 사전에 등록했다 — 단계는 P2 → external K2 → clinical pilot → CE/FDA. AUC ≥0.90 요구는 4단계 뒤 위치 — 현 paper의 frame을 잘못 적용했다."
Evidence ready: v19_paper2_image_dm1_pass · UNI LOTO 0.852 / OOF 0.874 / RAS-like 0.718 · venue ladder · phase-staged claim ladder
Pattern 5 · goalpost shift (variant)

"Phase C v2 ICI 4 cohort OR=1.53은 약하다 — single-cohort ≥3x effect 필요."

"An odds ratio of 1.53 is modest. Strong predictive biomarkers in ICI literature show ≥3x effects in single cohorts."
Counter — show 4/4 sign consistency + meta-frame "OR=1.53 (p=3.7e-4) IFNG는 4/4 cohorts에서 sign-consistent (Hugo / Riaz / IMvigor / MGH GSE115821). HLA-I OR=1.35 p=0.014, checkpoint OR=1.32, cytolytic OR=1.27 — 모두 동일 방향. 우리 claim은 'thyroid-specific ICI response predictor'가 아니라 'ICI vulnerability axis가 pan-lineage portable'. ≥3x single-cohort effect는 patient-selection biomarker frame이며, 우리는 mechanism frame."
Evidence ready: paper11_pancancer_2026_05_08 · Phase C v2 4 ICI cohorts (n=421) · IFNG OR=1.53 / HLA-I OR=1.35 / checkpoint OR=1.32 / cytolytic OR=1.27 · 4/4 sign-consistent

10 rhetorical moves — toolset

turn-the-flank to yield-the-minor · pick by context, never use defensively

01

Turn the flank

When · reviewer attacks the centerline of your claim

Don't defend the center; reframe the question so the attack misses. "Reviewer asks if X causes Y; the paper actually asks whether X co-occurs with Y in a way that constrains future causal designs."

Use caseP1 — "8-gene panel selection bias"는 'panel이 single-gene driver를 식별한다'가 아니라 'panel silencing pattern이 BRAF/RAS 경계를 자른다'로 reframe.
02

Steelman amplify

When · reviewer's critique has merit but is overgeneralized

Rewrite the reviewer's criticism in the strongest form, cite it, then answer that. This signals confidence and disarms the reviewer.

Use caseP2 cherry-pick — spatial null v15-v18을 우리가 reviewer보다 먼저 더 강하게 disclosure (v17 percentile 0.545, v18 KNN lag fail).
03

Cite the gold standard

When · reviewer doubts panel/method credibility

Anchor to a well-validated standard. TDS-16 (Cancer Genome Atlas), Landa 2016 JCI, AFND South Korea pool — these are not your invention.

Use caseP1 — TDS-16 ρ=0.954 superset capture는 panel selection bias attack 시 즉시 cite. 8-gene ⊂ TDS-16, 90% capture.
04

Disclose the null

When · reviewer is about to find your weakness

Pre-publish every null result before the reviewer does. honest_negatives_wall is a strategic moat, not a confession.

Use caseP2 cherry-pick — spatial v15-v18, Wang metabolomics block, Cell Rep Med 2026 anti-bot block 모두 사전 등록.
05

Show the cost

When · reviewer demands an impossible external

Quantify what the reviewer is actually asking for (time, cost, IRB, sample acquisition window). Reviewers often retreat when faced with $X / 18 months.

Use caseP3 impossible demand — K2 H&E n≥200 access는 분당 SNUH outreach + IRB + scanning + path review = 12-18 months. Methods-route fallback already pre-registered.
06

Declare the limit

When · reviewer pushes past your registered claim scope

"This paper claims X within scope Y. The extension you propose belongs to follow-up paper Z." Pre-registration is a sword.

Use caseP5 goalpost — Paper 2 frame은 'molecular-phenotype-discovery' phase. Clinical-grade AUC ≥0.90은 4단계 뒤 단계.
07

Pivot to mechanism

When · reviewer attacks correlation strength

Move the conversation from "is the effect big" to "what is the mechanism". Effect size shrinks under selection; mechanism survives.

Use caseP5 ICI OR=1.53 — pivot to '4-cohort sign-consistent ICI vulnerability axis' (mechanism frame) not 'patient-selection biomarker' (effect-size frame).
08

Reframe the question

When · reviewer asks the wrong question for your paper

"The reviewer asks A; the paper claims B. We agree A is unresolved, and B is decoupled from A." Don't fight the framing — translate it.

Use caseP4 FM commodity — "어떤 FM이 best인가" → "H&E 단일 modality가 RNA-level DM1 phenotype을 portable하게 capture하는가".
09

Extend the scope

When · reviewer thinks paper is too narrow

"Beyond the immediate findings, this work transfers to pan-cancer Phase E (32 cancers, 10,978 samples) prognostic in 10 lineages." Show the empire.

Use caseP1 Korean overdiagnosis narrowness — pan-cancer Phase E + DepMap MYC d=-0.50 + Phase C v2 ICI 4 cohorts로 confront.
10

Yield the minor

When · reviewer makes a small valid point unrelated to centerline

Concede small points generously. "Excellent point — we have added X to the Limitations." This buys credibility for the major battles.

Use casemethylation polarity flip (Round 4 → H13 corrected) — yield publicly, document in Limitations + Discussion 3.1.

Disagree without offending — phrasebook

Korean + English · use these as language anchors

한국어 — disagree without offending

  • 정중하지만 확인이 필요합니다. — 사실 오류 정정 시 첫 문장.
  • 리뷰어 의견은 정확히 본 논문의 frame을 한 layer 옮긴 것입니다. — reframe 시작 표현.
  • 이 우려는 저희가 사전에 등록한 limitation과 일치합니다. — pre-disclosure 활용.
  • 지적 감사드립니다. 단계적으로 답변드리겠습니다. — 긴 답변의 입문구.
  • 이는 follow-up 논문 범위입니다. — scope declaration.
  • 증거는 다음 페이지에서 사전 공개되어 있습니다. — pre-published null 활용.
  • 본 결과의 효과 크기는 ... 이며, 이는 ... mechanism으로 보강됩니다. — pivot to mechanism.
  • 리뷰어께서 제기하신 더 강한 형태는 ...일 것이며, 이에 대해서도 답변드립니다. — steelman amplify.

English — disagree without offending

  • Respectfully, we believe there may be a factual point to clarify. — fact correction opener.
  • The reviewer is exactly one frame removed from the paper's claim. — reframe opener.
  • This concern aligns with the limitation we registered upfront. — pre-disclosure.
  • We thank the reviewer; we address this in three layers. — long-answer opener.
  • This belongs to the scope of a follow-up paper. — scope declaration.
  • Evidence on this null is pre-published in our honest negatives wall. — pre-disclosure.
  • The effect size here is modest, but it is supported by a 4-cohort sign-consistent mechanism. — pivot to mechanism.
  • A stronger form of the reviewer's concern would be ..., which we address as follows. — steelman amplify.

When to walk away

wrong journal · wrong reviewer · wrong fight · 손절 기준

3가지 walk-away 조건

모든 싸움을 다 이길 필요는 없다. 다음 3 조건 중 하나면 손절하고 다음 venue로 이동한다.

  1. Wrong journal — 두 reviewer 모두 "wrong scope" cite 시 (editor의 wrong-fit signal). Nat Commun 1st pass에서 1명 reviewer가 wrong scope 가르치면 reject 후 즉시 Cell Rep Med / JCI Insight 이동.
  2. Wrong reviewer — reviewer가 paper의 핵심 cite 3개를 misattribute (Landa 2016 case와 유사). Editor에게 conflict request 후, 거부되면 walk away (rebuttal 깊이 들어가지 않는다).
  3. Wrong fight — 두 round 이상 같은 reviewer comment loop. 3번째 round는 거의 항상 venue 잘못. Pre-registered venue ladder의 다음 단으로 즉시 이동.
