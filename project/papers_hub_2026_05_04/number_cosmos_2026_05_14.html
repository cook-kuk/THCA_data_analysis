Single-axis story map — Top 20 → DM1 silencing axis

how 100+ numbers collapse to one story

① Phenomenology — DM1 silencing is real and big

OR=17.9 (DM1=FVPTC) · d=+3.65 (HLA-II in PTC+HT) · ROC AUCs 0.85–0.97 across 4 cohorts. Effect is not subtle — multi-pillar replication.

② Driver split — RAS=98% vs BRAF=1%

BRAF V600E β=0.37 ≈ RET fusion 0.39 vs RAS 0.27 (Deconv v5-v12), MAPK × Panel ρ pooled=-0.327, BRAF-vs-RAS d=±1.615 mirror. Single axis = MAPK output.

③ Specificity — panel honest, not lucky

Thyroid-matched null p=0.007, label-perm p<1e-4, TDS-16 ρ=0.954 (8⊂TDS-16). Captures canonical thyroid de-diff, not outcome-trained pattern.

④ Prognosis — survival rides DM1

TERT⁺ OS HR=7.57, LGG DSS HR=44.7, LUAD DSS HR=19, 11 lineages FDR<0.1, time-dep ROC 1y=0.83. Cross-cancer prognostic.

⑤ Druggability — DM1-high = MAPK-i sensitive

DepMap MYC d=-0.50 p=1e-11, NAMPT d=-0.45, PRISM top 7 ALL MAPK, top 15 = 8 MAPK-i, AZD-0364 MEK FDR=6e-7. Actionable axis.

⑥ Immune coupling — DM1 = inflam co-axis

ICI IFNG OR=1.53, HLA-I OR=1.35, checkpoint OR=1.32, cytolytic OR=1.27, 4/4 cohorts sign-consistent. DM1↔immune axis per Paper 11 Phase C v2.

⑦ Imaging coupling — H&E reads DM1

UNI overall LOTO 0.852 (OOF 0.874), N=59 mean 0.83±0.14, pooled 0.75. Tissue morphology encodes DM1 — Paper 2 main path.

⑧ HLA-overlap mechanism

GSE286332 HLA-II d=+3.65, TLS d=+1.96, BCR clonal d>0.5, AICDA up, IFN-γ FDR=2e-4. Antigen-driven B/T cell response in Hashimoto overlap.

⑨ RAI-refractory bridge

GSE151179 post-RAI thyroid_diff d=-1.01 (p=1e-4) + HLA-II +0.62 + myeloid +0.55. RAI-refractoriness = partial-ATC immunogenomic phenotype on same axis.

Null wall — what does NOT replicate (honest)

cross-link to honest_negatives_wall_2026_05_14.html

HLA-II ICI OR=1.02 (p=0.84)

Paper 11 Phase C v2 4 cohorts: HLA-II does not predict ICI response. HLA-I is load-bearing (OR=1.35 p=0.014) and HLA-II is honest null. Reframe = vulnerability/readiness, not response-predictor.

Phospho null

DM1 Round 3: phospho layer = honest negative. 4-pillar (RNA+protein+phospho+HM450) → phospho 빠짐, 3-pillar로 jacknife.

Spatial MAPK × Panel rescue fail (v15-v18)

Raw GSE250521 tumor MAPK × Panel ρ=+0.208 → residual +0.035; KNN lag, MAPK-high/Panel-low pockets, full+spatial+detection residual all fail (rho +0.007 at depth). Reviewer caveat, not mechanism.

Method-matrix stacker (CROSS-Neo)

Best OOF AUPRC ≈ 0.323 (cancer-vaccine track). Failed; score-booster calibration (stress-only logistic) recovered to OOF AUPRC 0.623 / AUROC 0.909 / top10 precision 0.85.

v5.1 DIAL=0.494 flip

Under proper LODO ComBat all 5 THCA classifiers return DIAL=0.000/true_biology. v5.1 0.494 was leak artifact. v15/v10/v13 downstream halted.

ViT-L RAS LOTO 0.308

Paper 2: ViT-L RAS LOTO artefact judgement; UNI-final overall LOTO 0.852 survives. RAS-like subgroup honest ceiling 0.718.
