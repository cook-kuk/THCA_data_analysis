Para 1 (editor pitch / journal-fit / main framing) — 본인 키보드에서만 작성. 빌더는 placeholder만. 권장 길이 3-5 sentences. NC reach 시 priority claim + cohort scale + biological novelty의 균형. npj base 시 translational relevance 강조.

Para 2 — key findings (draft). We identify and validate a panel of eight thyroid-differentiation genes (TG, TPO, SLC5A5, DUOX2, FOXE1, NKX2-1, PAX8, TSHR; collectively "DM1") whose coordinated silencing defines a high-risk molecular subtype across thyroid cancer. Across 12 contrasts spanning four orthogonal data layers (RNA-seq, mass-spectrometry proteomics, phosphoproteomics, HM450 methylation) in four cohorts (TCGA-THCA n=496, Lee ATC/Normal, Mun 2025 n=336, Landa GSE76039), 10 of 12 contrasts reach FDR < 0.01, no single gene dominates (per-gene mean |d| range 0.72-1.33), and DM1 status reaches AUC 0.90 (TCGA), 0.97 (Lee), and 0.95 (Mun). In TERT-positive patients, DM1 stratifies overall survival with HR = 7.57 [95% CI 2.74-20.91], p = 9.3 × 10⁻⁵. Methylation polarity (DM1 = high-β / silenced) is corrected and locked (H13 correction). Specificity is bulletproofed: thyroid-matched null p = 0.007, panel-permutation null p < 10⁻⁴, cluster stability 97.4 % ≥ 0.9, time-dependent ROC for TERT-or-DM1 at 1 year = 0.83, and TDS-16 correlation ρ = 0.954 with 8 ⊂ TDS-16 (90 % capture by 50 % gene count).

Para 3 — significance + reviewers + COI. The work reframes a long-standing "dark matter" in thyroid cancer as a four-pillar silencing program with mechanistic anchor (MAPK-pathway driven, BRAF V600E β = 0.37 ≈ RET fusion β = 0.39 vs RAS β = 0.27), pan-cancer generalization (Phase E lineage-portable DM1 prognostic in 10 of 32 cancers, including LGG and LUAD with HR ≫ 1), and druggable correlate (DepMap MYC d = -0.50 p = 1 × 10⁻¹¹; NAMPT d = -0.45 p = 1.5 × 10⁻⁹ in DM1-high). We suggest as reviewers three independent experts in molecular thyroid cancer, dark-matter / silenced-gene mechanisms, and TCGA pan-cancer methodology (see file 10_suggested_reviewers.md). No competing interests are declared. Limitations and nine cataloged null observations are addressed transparently in the Discussion (§3.1 with corrected Landa 2016 JCI citation; §3.last Limitations).

Sincerely,
Seungho Cook, M.D.
First author, on behalf of all authors

Para 1 — 본인 voice. histology-to-molecular translation의 framing 결정. computational pathology 분야 editor에게 어떻게 어필할지 본인 키보드.

Para 2 — key findings (draft). Building on our companion molecular work (Paper 1, DM1 dark matter), we ask whether routine H&E histology can predict DM1 status without sequencing. Using a UNI/ViT-L CLAM pipeline on TCGA-THCA whole-slide images (N = 59 leave-one-center-out evaluation), DM1 prediction reaches mean AUC = 0.83 ± 0.14 (pooled = 0.75); UNI LOTO overall AUC = 0.852, with RAS-driven LOTO = 0.718. We additionally provide spatial replication using GSE250521 (16 tumor slides) where LR-diversity peaks at lateral PTC (median 2,512 significant ligand-receptor pairs) with a CXCL13 ↔ CCR6/CCR7 TLS axis, consistent with our prior bulk BCR/TLS finding (HLA #1 companion).

Para 3 — honest disclosure + reviewers. We explicitly disclose a tissue-source-site (TSS) confound: a TSS-only logistic regression reaches 1.000 within-cohort, meaning naive H&E classification is partially driven by batch/site signal. We deconfound by LOTO, by RAS-driven subset analysis, and by external validation against an independent Korean K2 H&E cohort (validation pending gate). We are transparent that this is a pilot histology-to-molecular translation, not a fully deployment-ready clinical model. We suggest three reviewers with expertise in computational pathology, vision-foundation models (UNI / ViT-L), and TCGA H&E benchmarks; we exclude reviewers with whom we have co-authored within the last five years. No competing interests are declared. K2 H&E external validation gate is acknowledged as a prerequisite for any clinical-utility claim.

Sincerely,
Seungho Cook, M.D.
First author

Para 1 — 본인 voice. Hashimoto-overlap PTC framing은 thyroid + autoimmunity 두 editor에게 어필. 본인 키보드.

Para 2 — key findings (draft). In a Korean cohort of pure PTC versus PTC-with-coexistent Hashimoto thyroiditis (GSE286332, n = 18), we identify 10,380 differentially expressed genes and a coherent autoimmune signature: HLA class II Cohen's d = +3.65, interferon-γ pathway FDR = 2 × 10⁻⁴, tertiary lymphoid structure score d = +1.96, and IGHV clonality d > 0.5 with AICDA up-regulation, consistent with an antigen-driven B-cell response. The eight-gene thyroid-differentiation panel (DM1, companion Paper 1) is suppressed d = -1.6 in PTC+HT. Transferring this Hashimoto-like signature to TCGA-THCA stratifies DM2 (BRAF/RAS-negative dark-matter) tumors with odds ratio up to 5×, p = 6 × 10⁻¹⁰, resolving the previously unexplained enrichment of mutation-negative tumors in the dark-matter cohort.

Para 3 — honest disclosure + reviewers. We disclose that HLA class I shows a non-significant association in the independent ICI cohort series (Phase C v2, n = 421; OR = 1.02 p = 0.84) — the HLA-II signal is the load-bearing component, not pan-HLA. We frame the work as a Hashimoto-PTC immunogenomic axis with TCGA transfer, not as an ICI response predictor. We suggest three reviewers in (i) Hashimoto-PTC molecular pathology, (ii) thyroid autoimmunity / HLA-II biology, and (iii) tertiary lymphoid structure / B-cell mechanism. No competing interests are declared. The work is co-developed with Paper 1 (DM1 dark matter) and is submission-ready independent of any Bundang or unreleased Korean cohort.

Sincerely,
Seungho Cook, M.D.
First author
