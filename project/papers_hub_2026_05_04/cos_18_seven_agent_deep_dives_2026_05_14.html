Agent 1 · Richard P. Feynman (1918–1988) · Path Integral · QED · "What I cannot create, I do not understand"

  • quality bucket: basic / advanced / creative / visionary
  • SPARK Generative pipeline의 LLM_IGA + LLM_IRA
