02Core thesis

Thesis. BioDarwin is a regime-aware routing system that chooses between internal-feature, public-anchor, and BigMHC-style experts. It improves over lookup and over the best single expert on the frozen public bundles, and it wins the locked industrial mini-set in diagnostic mode.

08Next unlocks

Priority. Add a new expert pool instead of squeezing the same pool, keep the reviewer-safe table separate from the performance-max table, and re-run the 96-well search only after the next locked batch.
  1. Freeze the claim boundary around router improvement, not universal dominance.
  2. Move external winners into a separate comparator landscape.
  3. Use the 96-well interpreter as the prospective execution scaffold.
  4. Only then decide whether the paper becomes platform-only, application-only, or a split platform/application pair.

09Paper split

Rule. If the manuscript needs too many caveats to hold one claim, split it. A stronger paper is usually narrower and more falsifiable.
