평결 — Choices justified across 12 pipelines; 1 deferred (cookHLA)

verdict · 2026-05-14 · method choice audit

GREEN — 12 pipelines × 도구 선택 모두 정당화 완료

Image FM = ViT-L+CLAM PASS_LAUNCH_PAPER2 (UNI 0.852 / ViT-L RAS LOTO 0.308 보고), HLA = arcasHLA n=260 (DPB1*05:01 56% Kim 2014 replication), RNA quant = Kallisto reference + STAR for re-alignment (Pod D /tmp lesson 반영), Batch = LODO ComBat (v5.2 fix), Causal = DoWhy + EconML + DR learner + target trial. 모든 선택은 reviewer 1줄 답안이 준비되어 있다.
