★ Why cancer-enriched TRBV matters:
• Diagnostic biomarker — peripheral blood TCR-seq looking for TRBV6/3/4-rich repertoires could be a non-invasive cancer-immunity assay.
• CAR-T scaffold — TRBV6/TRBV3 is a candidate Vβ chain for engineered T-cell therapy targeting tumor antigens.
• Public clonotype — TRBV20 with CDR3β = ELAGIGILTV is the textbook Melan-A 26-35 melanoma TCR (lots of records).
• Effect size caveat — OR = 82.95 is on McPAS curated set; some cancer enrichment may reflect study selection bias toward cancer-rich publications.

Honest caveats

  • McPAS Cancer (n=3,590) heavily skewed toward melanoma (ELAGIGILTV, EAAGIGILTV) and Merkel cell (WEDLFCDESL...) — does not represent all cancers.
  • OR estimates are from McPAS curated TCR-pMHC pairs only; not from population PBMC repertoires.
  • TRBV6 OR=82.95 likely overestimated due to curation bias (cancer-focused publications recruited).
  • CDR3β length is uninformative; sequence motif requires more analysis (V-gene + CDR3 motif joint).
