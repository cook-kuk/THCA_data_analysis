★ Korean cohort necessity: The Korean SK population's dominant HLA-A*24:02 (18.5% allele freq) is 10× under-represented in the master corpus. Per-allele AUROC for A*24:02 = 0.74 (best), but the model is trained on only 115 records. K2/Bundang FFPE cohort acquisition would directly address this gap and is gated as Paper 4 backlog.

Implications for vaccine design

  • Vaccine peptides need HLA presentation testing across multiple alleles, not just A*02:01.
  • For Korean PAAD: A*11:01 (9%) + A*24:02 (18.5%) coverage = ~26% (independent assumption).
  • For Korean THCA: A*24:02 (18.5%) + A*33:03 (10.8%) coverage = ~27%.
  • Class II (DR/DP/DQ) is heavily under-represented across all populations — limits CD4 helper-arm vaccine design.
