The opportunity — a self-limiting natural experiment

Korean PTC overdiagnosis · 2014-2030 window · causal evidence that cannot be repeated

Korea conducted, between roughly 2000 and the present, the largest natural experiment in cancer overdiagnosis on record. National screening intensity for papillary thyroid cancer (PTC) pushed incidence into the world's highest band — without altering mortality — and produced cohort phenotypes that no other healthcare system can reproduce: tens of thousands of indolent, surveillance-eligible PTCs treated by world-class robotic thyroidectomy, with intact molecular, imaging, pathology, and longitudinal-outcome ledger.

That window is closing. Korean clinical practice and US/JP guidelines are converging on active surveillance (AS) for low-risk PTC. From 2030 onward, the cohort signal that drives this portfolio — the contrast between actually-operated indolent PTC and actually-monitored indolent PTC — will no longer be observable at scale.

Yu (Bundang SNUH endocrine surgery, 5,000+ BABA robotic cases) and Cook (Co-PI, agentic AI + multi-omics + QML stack) sit on the only collaboration that combines surgical volume, biobank access, and computational infrastructure to harvest the window before it closes. The portfolio is a wager that causal counterfactual patient trajectories, locked to a vanishing natural experiment, will outperform any non-causal foundation model trained on routine retrospective cohorts.

Why now — three converging forces

public data tipping point · Korean AS window · regulatory convergence

Force 1 · Public reuse data tipping point

TCGA pan-cancer (11,069 × 33 lineages), CELLxGENE (33 million cells), DepMap (1,141 cell lines), and ICI cohorts (4 datasets, n=421) all now reach a critical mass that supports a pretrain-then-fine-tune foundation-model architecture. This was not possible in 2020 or 2022.

Force 2 · Korean AS window before 2030

MAeSTro / MASTER / KoMPASS registries are capturing the surgical-vs-monitoring contrast at scale right now. Once Korean practice converges fully on AS for low-risk PTC (projected 2028-2030), the counterfactual structure that anchors this portfolio's causal claims will close. This is a vanishing natural experiment.

Force 3 · Regulatory convergence

US (NCCN), Korean (KTA), and Japanese guidelines are all migrating toward AS-first low-risk PTC. Translational evidence anchored to the surgery-era cohort will be needed to interpret the AS-era data — and the only way to build that bridge is to lock the evidence now, before the bridge becomes ahistorical.

Honest risks

claim discipline · no 70%+ probability claims · no hype

R1 · Small-N causal events

AS-vs-surgery event rates are ~5%. Doubly robust / causal forest estimators have variance issues at this N. Mitigation: target trial emulation framing + Bayesian shrinkage + sensitivity analysis. Cannot be eliminated; must be disclosed in grant methods.

R2 · Surveillance / monitoring schedule bias

AS patients are imaged more frequently than surgical patients post-op; detection bias confounds outcomes. Mitigation: IPTW + protocol emulation explicitly modeled. Reviewer Q2 will press here.

R3 · Quantum-inspired sidecar over-claim

NISQ-era quantum-inspired methods cannot yet beat classical pipelines on this size of problem. Mitigation: framed as 3-billion-KRW future-proofing pilot inside the 30-billion-KRW grant, claim-limited, decoupled work package. No headline quantum claim.

R4 · Self-extinguishing data structure

The Korean PTC overdiagnosis natural experiment is closing. Mitigation: explicit transfer framing to other overtreated cancers (low-risk prostate, DCIS, indolent lymphoma) — the methods are portable even when the cohort is not.

R5 · "Foundation model" naming mismatch

TCGA-THCA n=500 is too small to call a "foundation model" on its own. Mitigation: framing is pan-cancer pretrain → thyroid downstream — pretrain on TCGA pan-cancer + CELLxGENE + DepMap. Reviewer Q1 will probe this.

R6 · K2 access path failure

If K2 H&E external validation cannot close in 4 weeks, Paper 2 absorbs into Paper 1 Supplementary Text 1 and the Samsung Yu-validation pillar weakens. Mitigation: absorb path is rehearsed; LUAD/BRCA methods route is on standby.
