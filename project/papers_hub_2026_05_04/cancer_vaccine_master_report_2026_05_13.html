01Thesis

One sentence, but enough context to be useful.

The platform is a decision system, not a single model.

It ingests public neoantigen data, audits leakage, ranks candidates with simple and LM-based models, adds a router / ensemble layer, and turns the shortlist into executable outputs plus a 96-well validation path.

07Data spine

What was used and why it matters.
Core corpus

Primary cancer vaccine data

13 sources, 119,237 cleaned records, and 19,817 leakage-free benchmark rows form the main evaluation base. The structural layer adds 115 ESMFold structures and the executable shortlist adds the 7-peptide set cover plus the KRAS lead GADGVGKSAL.

Public comparator layer

External and industrial context

59 public industrial sources, 54 fetch successes, 3,668 extracted entities, 26,323 candidate pairs, 11,511 Class I score-ready rows, and 666 Class II rows were used to build the external comparison map and the customer-facing boundary.

What the comparison says

  • RF is the anchor.
  • ESM2 is the rescue branch.
  • XGBoost is the challenger.
  • Public comparators are the context, not the whole story.
  • BioDarwin + NeoQ is the only stack that combines ranking, audit, structure, and a deliverable in one front door.

Implication

The project should not claim universal SOTA. The stronger claim is that the router learns which expert family to use, and that the system is honest about when a public comparator is better.

1296-well scaffold

The bridge from retrospective ranking to prospective validation.
Lane

Hit / fail / helper / probe

High-confidence candidates go to hit, low-confidence rows go to fail, ambiguous rows to helper, and exploratory rows to probe. That is the decision surface the wetlab can actually use.

Why it matters

It is a readout schema

The plate is not just a list of wells. It is a schema that lets the interpreter update without redesigning the entire pipeline.
