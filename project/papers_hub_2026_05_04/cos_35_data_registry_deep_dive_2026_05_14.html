AllowedForbidden

AllowedForbidden

8.1 What is verifiable vs not

Programmatic verification possible: GEO accession (GSE/GDS/GSM) + PubMed PMID + DOI + ArrayExpress E-MTAB. Programmatic verification not possible: AFND (web-only), KMDP/KCDC (registry no-API), MSigDB/KEGG/Reactome (concept DBs not datasets), internal cohorts. label: DATA_EXPANSION §1
