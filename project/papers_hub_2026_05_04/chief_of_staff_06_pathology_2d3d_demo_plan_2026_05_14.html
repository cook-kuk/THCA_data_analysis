7. ROI strategy — full-WSI infeasible

100k×100k pixels → tile → register → ROI selection per stage

7.1 Why ROI

  • LITERATURE Typical thyroid WSI at 0.25 μm/pixel ≈ 100,000 × 100,000 pixels per slice. 30 slices = 9 × 10¹¹ pixels = 900GB raw uint8.
  • FILE-BACKED /data 가용 디스크 454GB (PATHOLOGY_DM1_FEASIBILITY §2.3) — full WSI 30 slice 도 디스크 fit 못 함.
  • INTERNAL VERIFY 따라서 stage-별 ROI 100-500 patches (224 px each) 로 좁힌다. ROI 당 patch 수 100 × 224² × 3 = 약 15MB → 30 slice × 4 stage × 100 patch = 4.5GB (manageable).

7.2 ROI selection rule per stage

StageROI targetPatches per ROIAnatomical landmark
Normal-adjacent2 ROIs100 patchesfollicle-rich region (uniform thyrocyte epithelium)
PTC3 ROIs200 patchestumor center + invasive margin + adjacent normal
LPTC3 ROIs300 patchestumor center + lymphocytic infiltrate (Hashimoto-overlap) + margin
ATC3 ROIs500 patchesanaplastic center + necrosis edge + invasion front

7.3 Cross-slice ROI consistency

  • ROI 위치는 first slice 에서 결정 후 인접 slice 에 propagate (affine + landmark refinement).
  • ROI tracking 실패 (예: tumor boundary drift >200 μm) 시 manual reanchor 필요.
  • ATC 의 500 patches 는 anaplastic morphology heterogeneity 때문 (closure battery §1: ATC AUROC 0.689 — gross histology trivial).

9.2 Reference tools (pathology-specific)

  • LITERATURE BigStitcher (Hörl et al. Nat Methods 2019) — multi-tile + multi-slice z-stack, optimized for fluorescence but generalizes to H&E.
  • LITERATURE TeraStitcher (Bria & Iannello BMC Bioinformatics 2012) — TB-scale 3D microscopy stitching.
  • LITERATURE ANHIR challenge winners (Borovec et al. IEEE TMI 2020) — histology-specific 2D registration benchmark; ANTsPy + elastix dominant.
  • LITERATURE VALIS (Gatenbee et al. Nat Methods 2023) — virtual alignment of pathology image series; designed for serial-section IHC alignment.
  • LITERATURE PALOMA, PASTE — spatial transcriptomics 3D alignment (for Phase C if Visium anchors added).

10.2 Dominant failure cause (closure battery)

"Spot-level ST molecular label 과 H&E tile content fundamentally mismatched" — 즉, spot 마다의 분자 label 과 그 spot 의 H&E morphology 사이에 학습 가능한 신호가 없음 (closure battery §5 cause 4).

10.3 Why 2D→3D track is DIFFERENT objective

Image-DM1 prediction (DROPPED)

  • Objective: predict molecular score from H&E pixels.
  • Gate: Spearman ≥ 0.30 or AUROC ≥ 0.65.
  • Verdict: D 폐기.
  • Why fail: spot-level mismatch (cause 4).
  • Foundation gain insufficient (+0.05-0.15 vs +0.28 gap).

2D→3D reconstruction (본 트랙)

  • Objective: align serial slices → 3D atlas.
  • Gate: visual coherence + landmark RMSE.
  • Verdict: Phase A gated by sectioning cohort.
  • Why expected to work: geometric similarity 만 필요 — adjacent slice 는 본질적으로 비슷한 tissue (z step 4-5 μm).
  • Foundation model 효과 충분 — UNI/CONCH 가 patch similarity 잘 잡음 (closure battery §2.5 의 published gain 은 prediction task 에 대한 것; similarity task 는 더 안정).

10.4 Transferred lessons

  • L1. ResNet50 ImageNet 으로는 fine molecular-level signal 안 잡힘 — UNI/CONCH 권장 (Phase A 부터 token 신청).
  • L2. Depth-residualization 정당 (closure §5 cause 3) — depth/stain artifact 와 진짜 morphology 를 분리.
  • L3. 224 vs 448 vs 672 px tile size effect 작음 — 본 트랙 244 px baseline + 448 멀티스케일 fusion 만.
  • L4. Single cohort 16-slide POC 는 진단 task 에 underpowered (closure §5 cause 5) — 본 트랙은 동일 환자 serial 사용으로 cohort 작아도 가능 (z-axis = independent variable).
  • L5. Strict re-entry condition (closure §5): image-DM1 PREDICTION 으로의 재진입은 5개 조건 모두 충족 시만. 본 트랙은 prediction 이 아니라 reconstruction 이라 별도 게이트.
