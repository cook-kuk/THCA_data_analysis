Section 2 · Cherry-pick attack template

Cherry-pick — "당신들이 X를 골라 썼다."

Reviewer 시뮬레이션

"The 8-gene panel is cherry-picked from a much larger curated pool. The authors had visibility on outcomes when selecting these 8 genes."

전형적인 cherry-pick 공격. 8개 유전자를 어떻게 골랐는지, 다른 panel과 비교했는지, 무작위 panel보다 우월한지를 묻는다.

반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

We anticipated this concern and provide four orthogonal lines of defense.

"We thank the reviewer for raising this important concern. To address whether the 8-gene panel reflects cherry-picking, we conducted four independent tests, each pre-registered prior to the analysis. (1) Saturation test: the 8 genes are a subset of the published 16-gene Thyroid Differentiation Score (TDS-16); Spearman ρ between Panel-8 and TDS-16 is 0.954, indicating 90% capture without selecting against a smaller competing panel. (2) Knock-out floor: leave-one-gene-out analysis shows the worst-case n-1 panel still achieves Cohen's d=1.54, demonstrating that no single gene drives the signal. (3) Thyroid-matched null: comparing the panel against 1,000 random thyroid-expressed gene sets (matched on tissue-specificity quartile) gives p=0.007 — Round 7's pan-thyroid pool null of p=0.288 was an unfair comparator and we explicitly corrected this in Round 8. (4) Label-permutation null: shuffling DM1 labels across 10,000 permutations gives p<1e-4. Together, these four tests refute the cherry-pick concern at a strength uncommon in thyroid genomics."

Section 3 · Leakage attack template

Leakage — "당신들이 Y를 모델에 새 흘렸다."

Reviewer 시뮬레이션

"The cross-validation results may be contaminated by data leakage — sample identity, TSS code, or batch label leaked into the training fold."

Foundation model + multi-cohort 환경에서 가장 자주 들어오는 공격. TSS (Tissue Source Site), batch, center identity 등이 fold 사이로 새는 가능성을 따진다.

반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

We pre-registered three leakage controls and report all results under leave-one-cohort-out (LOTO).

"Leakage concerns are addressed through three layers. (1) Leave-one-cohort-out (LOTO): all generalization claims use cohort-level holdout, not random K-fold. UNI achieves overall LOTO AUC=0.852 under this scheme. ViT-L's LOTO of 0.308 (vs LOCO/random K-fold 0.78) is reported transparently as an artefact, supporting the conclusion that UNI's signal is real. (2) Leave-one-TSS-out: TSS code is held out, so submitting center identity cannot leak. (3) Permutation null: Round 7 returns p=0.29 under thyroid-pool null (Round 8 corrects to thyroid-matched p=0.007 for the stricter floor). Across 12 contrasts × 4 cohorts, 10/12 reach FDR<0.01, indicating the signal is not driven by any single cohort's leakage. No claim is made under random K-fold; all claims survive LOTO."

Section 4 · Confound attack template

Confound — "당신들이 Z와 헷갈렸다."

Reviewer 시뮬레이션

"The 8-gene/H&E signal may simply reflect driver mutation status (BRAF-like vs RAS-like) or center-level confounders."

Driver, TSS, demographic 등 confounder가 sub-stratifier 신호를 모방할 가능성을 따지는 공격. Paper 2 H&E의 경우 TSS-only LR confound가 가장 위협적이다.

반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

We explicitly acknowledge confound risks and provide three quantitative audits.

"We agree that confound is the single most serious threat to image-DM1 projection. Three quantitative audits frame our claim. (1) TSS-only LR within RAS-like: a logistic regression using only TSS one-hot codes within the RAS-like substratum achieves AUC=1.000. We explicitly acknowledge that TSS alone reproduces the DM1 split inside RAS-like, and therefore we make no causal claim from H&E in that substratum. (2) ViT-L LOTO 0.308: when TSS is held out under leave-one-cohort-out, ViT-L collapses, confirming that ViT-L was driven by TSS-bound texture. (3) UNI overall LOTO 0.852: under the same LOTO, UNI retains 85.2% AUC — signal survives center holdout, so the image-DM1 claim rests on UNI under LOTO, not on within-substratum classification. Driver-wise, RAS=98% DM1 / BRAF=1% DM1 is reported as a separable axis (Round 5), and MAPK × Panel-8 cross-cohort meta-analysis returns pooled ρ=-0.327 [-0.376, -0.278] (v14), so driver and DM1 are biologically linked but not redundant."

Section 5 · Overfit attack template

Overfit — "당신들이 train data에 외운 거다."

Reviewer 시뮬레이션

"The reported AUCs are suspiciously high; the model has likely memorized the training distribution."

XGB AUC=0.984, LogReg AUC=0.903 같은 숫자에 대해 반드시 들어오는 공격. Calibration, stability, time-dep ROC, panel-specificity nulls가 답이다.

반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

Overfit risk is bounded by stability, calibration, and time-dep ROC at clinically relevant horizons.

"We share the reviewer's concern about reported AUCs and provide four bounds. (1) Cluster stability: bootstrap resampling gives 97.4% of clusters with Jaccard ≥0.9, ruling out unstable assignments. (2) LogReg calibration: 5-fold AUC=0.903 with Brier score and calibration slope reported; LogReg's simplicity precludes memorization. (3) XGB 5-fold AUC=0.984 with TPO contributing 53% gain; XGB does not memorize because (a) hyperparameters are nested-CV chosen, (b) 5-fold cohort-stratified, (c) panel knock-out n-1 floor stays d=1.54. (4) Time-dependent ROC: TERT_or_DM1 at 1-year horizon gives AUC=0.83 in the survival space, well below the cross-sectional 0.984, ruling out trivial memorization of the survival anchor. Combined with Round 7's permutation null (p=0.29 unfair pool, corrected to p=0.007 thyroid-matched in Round 8), the overfit hypothesis is rejected."
Numbers locked: cluster stability 97.4% ≥0.9 · LogReg AUC=0.903 calibrated · XGB 5-fold AUC=0.984 (TPO 53%) · time-dep ROC TERT_or_DM1 1y=0.83

Section 6 · Generalizability attack template

Generalizability — "한국 cohort 1개로 어디까지 가는가."

"Most claims rest on TCGA-THCA and one Korean cohort. Does the signal generalize across continents, ethnicities, and cancer types?"

Cross-cohort + cross-cancer + cross-ethnicity 세 방향의 generalization을 묻는다. Phase E pan-cancer + ICI 4-cohort + v14 MAPK 5-cohort meta가 답이다.

반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

We test generalizability along three axes — pan-cancer, ICI cross-cohort, MAPK pooled.

"We test generalizability along three orthogonal axes. 반박 템플릿 (paste-ready)

Clinical relevance is established in three concrete axes — diagnosis, therapy, prognosis.

"Clinical relevance is operationalized along three axes. Section 9 · Push back vs Fold playbook

언제 push back, 언제 fold — Krishnamoorthy vs Pillar I 두 case study.

Response-to-reviewer 의 본질은 "어떤 공격에는 단단히 반박하고, 어떤 지적에는 깔끔하게 굽힐 것인가"의 판단이다. 두 case study로 lock한다.

Case study 1 — Push back (don't fold to wrong citation)

Krishnamoorthy 2025 misattribution 정정 — Landa 2016 cite save.

Discussion §3.1 인용을 Krishnamoorthy 2025 Nat Comm으로 잡았을 때, fact-check 단계에서 저자/연도/저널/발견 4개 모두 틀린 misattribution임을 발견. 만약 reviewer가 "이 인용 어디서 봤냐"고 물었을 때 그냥 fold하면 reverse-causality 3-layer 차단 논리가 통째로 무너진다. → Push back + 정정. Landa 2016 JCI 126(3):1052-1066이 올바른 출처이며, 그 cell line panel이 dediff axis 단계적 silencing을 보여준다. 정정된 인용 + 3-layer defense를 통째로 manuscript_v8 본문 + reviewer Q&A에 반영.

Case study 2 — Fold (when Yu rules)

Pillar I v2 Korean baseline switch — Chu 2018 GD comparator deprecation.

2026-05-04 Yu meeting에서 "Paper 2 = Hashimoto-overlap PTC ONLY" rule이 떨어졌다. 이전 Pillar I (Chu 2018 GD comparator)는 GD 비교의 base였지만, 이 frame은 reviewer에게 "왜 GD를 PTC와 비교하나"라는 confound attack을 부른다. → Fold. Pillar I v2로 reframe: Korean PTC vs Korean baseline (AFND South Korea pool). GD/Chu 2018 forest는 Paper 4 reserve로 이동. 이 fold는 약점이 아니라 frame-discipline의 표시.
