Rule 1 · TCGA ≠ CTC

TCGA is tissue bulk RNA-seq. CTC is peripheral blood circulating tumor cell. They cannot substitute for each other.

TCGA-THCA primary tumor n=505는 surgical resection의 tissue bulk transcriptome이다. CTC count, CTC EMT phenotype, ctDNA fraction은 TCGA에 존재하지 않는다. 본 repo가 가장 자주 마주치는 hallucination 위험.

TCGA can supportTCGA CANNOT support
  • tissue-level differential expression (PTC vs ATC)
  • driver mutation × expression label association
  • HLA module signature (transcriptomic, not allele)
  • OS/DSS/PFI/DFI Cox survival
  • RAI-low dedifferentiated proportion in cohort
  • Methylation 450K + miRNA + WXS integration
  • CTC count or CTC EMT phenotype
  • ctDNA detection rate
  • postoperative blood timepoint
  • Korean-specific allele frequency
  • spatial heterogeneity within a slide
  • antigen presentation at peptide level
  • iodine uptake direct measurement
  • drug penetration / nanoparticle distribution

Allowed: TCGA-THCA 505명에서 7개 vulnerability label이 환자별로 분리된다. Driver mutation은 label과 관련되지만 결정하지 않는다.

Forbidden: TCGA에서 CTC count X였다 · TCGA score가 RAI 반응을 예측한다 · TCGA n=505 결과를 본 환자 cohort에 직접 적용한다.
