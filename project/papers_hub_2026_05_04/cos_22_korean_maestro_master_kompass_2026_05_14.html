2 · MAeSTro — Multicenter Active Surveillance prospective

literature + Yu meeting context · requires verification

7 · Treatment arms — Arm A AS · Arm B immediate surgery

target trial emulation arms · cos_15 protocol

AS-vs-Surgery target trial emulation의 두 arm 정의다 (cos_14 Hernán/Robins primer + cos_15 protocol 기반).

Arm A · Active Surveillance

Active Surveillance (AS)

Treatment: no immediate surgery. Surveillance schedule with imaging + lab + clinical assessment.

Schedule: 6-month US + Tg + clinical evaluation; annual after 2y if stable.

Crossover trigger: tumor size growth ≥3mm in any dimension; new LN appearance; ETE development; patient preference change.

Endpoint: PFS (no crossover) at 2y/5y/10y · size trajectory · LN appearance.

Crossover policy: censored at crossover OR (in causal analysis) inverse probability of crossover weighting (IPW · cos_14).

Arm B · Immediate Surgery

Immediate Thyroidectomy

Treatment: lobectomy or total thyroidectomy + (선택적) LN dissection, within 8-12 weeks of diagnosis.

Approach: BABA (분당 SNUH 표준) · open · endoscopic — center 정책별. cos_20 BABA archive와 매핑.

Adjuvant: RAI consider for intermediate risk (post-pathology stratification).

Endpoint: pathology-confirmed residual disease · post-op Tg trajectory · recurrence at 2y/5y/10y · DSS.

Subgroups: lobectomy-only vs total thyroidectomy · LN dissection extent · driver-stratified outcomes.
