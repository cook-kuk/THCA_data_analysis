Reviewer Q&A — anti-collision pre-staged

"isn't this just extension?" / "what about X?" / "you cite Z but..."

Q1. "Isn't this just an extension of TCGA-THCA 2014?"

A: TCGA-THCA 2014 (Cell) established BRAF/RAS dichotomy with RNA + miRNA + methylation + DNA. We identify a third axis (DM1) orthogonal to dichotomy, replicated across 4 pillars including protein (Mun 2025), image, methylation (H13-corrected). RAS=98% DM1 + BRAF=1% DM1 explicitly maps DM1 onto the BRAF/RAS framework.

Q2. "Mun 2025 already shows protein evidence — what's new?"

A: We use Mun 2025 as Pillar 2 replication (sign-match 7/7, 5/7 Spearman r<-0.5). New contributions: (a) Pillar 3 methylation H13-corrected with Landa 2016 cross-cohort, (b) Pillar 4 image-DM1 (UNI LOTO 0.852), (c) pan-cancer 11 lineages, (d) DepMap MYC d=-0.50 druggable axis.

Q3. "You cite Krishnamoorthy 2025 but the facts are from Landa 2016."

A: Correct. We have already corrected this via the cite_save_register: Landa 2016 JCI 126(3):1052-1066 is the original source for the 4 facts (PDTC/ATC continuum, 5/8 ATC overlap, etc.). All PDTC/ATC sections now cite Landa 2016 as anchor. Krishnamoorthy 2025 is no longer cited for these claims.

Q4. "UNI 2024 is the foundation model — what is your contribution?"

A: We use UNI as backbone feature extractor (not compete). Our contribution: CLAM-UNI framework for cross-modality H&E → DM1 prediction (a new task UNI was not trained for), with mean AUC 0.83±0.14 (N=59), overall LOTO 0.852, ViT-L LOTO 0.308 artifact disclosed. UNI is methods anchor in cover letter.

Q5. "Caturegli 2014 already reviewed HT immune pathology."

A: Caturegli 2014 reviews HT immune pathology in general; we extend to PTC+HT cancer interface with spatial replication (GSE250521 LPTC TLS axis, TLS d=+1.96, IGHV clonality, AICDA up) + TCGA generalization (DM2 OR up to 5×, p=6e-10). Caturegli is cited as background anchor + HLA class II / DPB1 link.

Q6. "Kim 2014 already showed DPB1*05:01 in Korean GD."

A: Kim 2014 used HLA typing; we replicate via RNA-seq arcasHLA imputation on PRJEB11591 n=260, achieving 56% allele frequency match. New contribution: methods feasibility (RNA-seq imputation without SNP), AFND South Korea pool anchoring, Pan-Asian comparator extension.

Q7. "Kim Kwang-Ho 2025 (Samsung 인과 파운데이션) already proposed causal foundation."

A: Kim Kwang-Ho 2025 is a general causal foundation framework; we apply to thyroid-specific disease anchor with unique cohort access: Yu MAeSTro/MASTER/KoMPASS (5,000 BABA + 62 CTC/EMT). The cohort is the moat — generic causal foundation cannot be tested against without the cohort.

Q8. "Why not Bulten 2022 / Chen 2024 framework?"

A: Bulten 2022 is Gleason-grade (different framework); Chen 2024 Nat Med is different cancer. Our task is cross-modality molecular axis prediction from H&E, validated with multi-center LOTO. Different problem, different framework.

Q9. "Methylation polarity flip suggests artifact."

A: The H13 polarity correction is registered; r9_1 master is locked with DM1=HIGH-β/silenced (cPTC d=+1.50). Round 4 d=-1.75 was a label-flip artifact, explicitly disclosed in supplementary. Landa 2016 GSE76039 PDTC vs ATC d=+3.47 cross-cohort confirms.
