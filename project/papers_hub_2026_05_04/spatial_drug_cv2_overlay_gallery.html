02Visual Takeaway

The strongest visual story is not a single measured drug response. It is the spatial co-localization question: do high predicted vulnerability pockets overlap tumor-state programs, target programs, and H&E regions that look biologically coherent?
Visual questionBest figureHow to use it
Which drugs create visible spatial pockets?Fig 24Scan the six-drug atlas and compare hotspot contours across the same H&E slide.
Does vulnerability overlap target/state biology?Fig 25Compare vulnerability, target program, and DM1/TDS/RAI-like state in matched coordinates.
Are tumor-state fields spatially structured?Fig 26Inspect DM1-like, TDS-like, and RAI-readable fields directly on tissue.
Are hotspots localized or diffuse?Fig 27Use contour zooms to choose regions for IHC, RNAscope, or ex vivo perturbation.

08Claim Boundary

Allowed: hypothesis generation, target-aware visual prioritization, spatial heterogeneity analysis, region selection for validation, and wet-lab validation roadmap design.
Not allowed: clinical treatment recommendation, true measured IC50 per spot, patient selection, or causal drug response claim without perturbation validation.
