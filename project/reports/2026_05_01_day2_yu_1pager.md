## 4. P4 — Pan-genome top-5000 MAD vs TIERA67 (reviewer Q3 robustness)

| Cluster panel | n_genes | ARI vs DM1/DM2 |
|---|---|---|
| 8-gene panel | 8 | **0.489** |
| TDS_core 16 | 16 | 0.467 |
| TIERA67 (full) | 67 | **0.903** |
| Pan-genome top 200 MAD | 200 | 0.864 |
| Pan-genome top 1000 MAD | 1000 | 0.902 |
| **Pan-genome top 5000 MAD** | 5000 | **0.918** |
| TIERA67 minus 8-gene | 58 | 0.951 |
| **Driver_anchor 12 only** | 12 | **−0.007 (NULL)** |

**해석:**
- DM1/DM2 cluster 는 **global transcriptomic axis** 임 (pan-genome top-5000 ARI=0.92 vs TIERA67 ARI=0.90 ≈ 일치)
- **8-gene panel 단독 ARI=0.49 — the panel is "biologically interpretable subset, not maximum statistical signal"** (paper Discussion 정직 기재)
- **Driver-only cluster 는 random (ARI=0)** — "BRAF/RAS/TERT alone cannot define DM" — P1 결과와 정합
- TIERA67 enrichment in pan-genome top 100: hypergeometric p = **3e-4** (biological prior justified)

**Methods phrasing 권장:** "We deliberately selected the 8-gene panel based on canonical RAI-uptake biology rather than maximum univariate discriminative power. This enables clinical interpretability at modest cost in cluster ARI (0.49 for 8-gene alone vs 0.92 for unrestricted pan-genome top-5000), while the curated TIERA67 candidate pool reproduces the unbiased pan-genome cluster (ARI 0.90 vs 0.92)."

## 5. P5 — 8-gene vs HLA-II autocorrelation + 3-cohort meta

### 5.1 TCGA (n=500) Spearman ρ matrix
| | g8 RAI | HLA-I | HLA-II | immune |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| g8 RAI | 1.00 | -0.52 | **-0.51** | -0.32 |
| HLA-II | -0.51 | 0.89 | 1.00 | 0.86 |

→ Moderate negative correlation; partially independent axes.

### 5.2 Residualization (TCGA, DM1 vs DM2)
| Model | d | MW p |
|---|---|---|
| raw 8-gene RAI | +1.78 | 1.6e-44 |
| **8-gene \| HLA-II residualized** | **+1.00** | **4.4e-24** |
| 8-gene \| immune-proxy residualized | +1.50 | 5.5e-38 |
| 8-gene \| HLA-II + immune residualized | +0.87 | 2.0e-19 |
| HLA-II \| 8-gene residualized | -0.58 | 1.4e-9 |

→ **8-gene 의 DM 예측 signal 의 약 56% 는 HLA-II 와 독립** (d 1.78 → 1.00). 두 모듈 모두 잔존 잔여 signal 보유 → 진정 partial-orthogonal axes.

### 5.3 GSE286332 (n=18, autoimmune-PTC context)
- ρ(8-gene, HLA-II) = -0.83 (small n에서 더 entangled)
- raw 8-gene d=-1.60 → HLA-II residualized d=+0.43 (NS)
- raw HLA-II d=+3.65 → 8-gene residualized d=+1.60 (still strong)
- → 작은 cohort 에서는 두 axis 가 같은 변동성을 share

### 5.4 2-cohort 8-gene RAI meta (random-effects)
| Cohort | n_case | n_ctrl | Cohen d |
|---|---|---|---|
| TCGA-THCA (DM2 vs DM1, n=500) | 360 | 140 | −1.78 |
| GSE286332 (PTC+HT vs PTC, n=18) | 9 | 9 | −1.60 |

**Pooled d = −1.78 [95% CI −2.00, −1.56]**, τ²=0, Cochran's Q=0.11 (df=1) — **no heterogeneity, perfect direction concordance**.

K2 cohort calibration mismatch (raw-TPM mini-index ≠ classifier centered profile, ρ=+0.33 wrong direction) — excluded from raw-score meta but contributes via DM call distribution (246/260 = 94.6% DM2; vs TCGA 72%; consistent with Korean elevated Hashimoto background).
