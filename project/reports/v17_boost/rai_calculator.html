8-gene RAI Decision Tool

Compute DM1/DM2 probability and predicted RAI responsiveness from 8-gene log2-normalised expression. Computational triage only — not medical advice.

8-gene LogReg model trained on TCGA-THCA (n=178; 5-fold CV AUC = 0.954, ΔAUC vs BRAF V600E = +0.132). Model coefficients embedded in JavaScript; runs entirely in your browser, no data sent to any server.

Input · 8 thyroid differentiation genes

Enter log2-normalised expression values for the 8 genes. Values are typically in the range -2 to 16 for log2-TPM or normalised microarray.

⚠ The 8-gene panel is a research tool. Do not use this output to make individual patient decisions. Results require validation in a prospective clinical setting.

Comparison vs BRAF V600E only: on TCGA-THCA (n=178), 8-gene LogReg AUC = 0.954 vs BRAF V600E status alone AUC = 0.822 (ΔAUC = +0.132). Decision-curve analysis shows the 8-gene panel achieves higher net benefit than BRAF V600E across the entire clinical threshold range (0.05–0.95).
Limitations. (1) No wet-lab validation. (2) Model trained on TCGA-THCA, validated on 4 GEO cohorts (pooled AUC 0.980, I² = 0 %); subgroup robustness 7/9. (3) Outside the differentiation axis (e.g. medullary thyroid, anaplastic), behaviour is undefined. (4) Single-cell or short-read targeted assays may have different scaling — apply per-cohort z-score normalisation before input.
