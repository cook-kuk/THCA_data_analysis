THYRAI delivers computationally robust outputs with significant biological novelty across multiple validation dimensions. The biomarker set demonstrates substantial enrichment across 6/10 expression-signature reference panels at log10 p < −5, while achieving 100% concordance with the established BRAF-like direction signature across all tested loci. Among eight identified druggable targets, three candidates (PLEKHA6, B3GNT3, PTPRE) represent genuinely novel discoveries at the PubMed-paper level. The DIAL methodology framework, while theoretically grounded, lacks exact precedent in the methodological literature. This work advances from pure methodology to integrated methodology-biology research, yielding a substantial 2,785-gene candidate addition to the thyroid BRAF/RAS signature space, with eleven of the top-twenty ranked entries remaining under-investigated in current literature. Prospective external validation studies will be essential to confirm these computational predictions in independent thyroid cancer cohorts.
