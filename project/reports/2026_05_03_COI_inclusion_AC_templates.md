## 2. Inclusion + Diversity Statement (Cell Press mandatory)

```
Inclusion and Diversity:

We support inclusive, diverse, and equitable conduct of research.

While citing, the authors made an effort to ensure citation diversity 
across geographic regions, gender of authors, and career stages. The 
study integrates East Asian (Korean and Han Chinese) cohorts which are 
historically under-represented in thyroid cancer genomic literature 
(predominantly EUR-dominant TCGA cohorts), with explicit population 
stratification analyses to avoid generalizing findings beyond the 
sampled populations. We acknowledge the patients and clinical 
investigators across all five contributing cohorts whose generosity 
and collaborative spirit enabled this work.

The authors acknowledge that this work does not include direct 
under-represented minority co-authorship beyond the East Asian 
context, and we welcome collaborators from diverse backgrounds for 
follow-up phases of this research program.
```

## 9. Reproducibility statement (Cell Press encouraged)

```
Reproducibility:

All analyses are fully reproducible via the public code repository 
(https://github.com/seunghocook/thyca-paper-2026, Zenodo doi: TBD). 
Random seeds are fixed throughout (seed=42 for KMeans, GMM, bootstrap, 
GSEA permutations). Software versions are pinned in requirements.txt 
(numpy 1.26+, pandas 2.0+, scipy 1.11+, statsmodels 0.14+, sklearn 
1.3+, pydeseq2==0.5.4, gseapy==1.1.13). Reference databases are 
version-locked: IPD-IMGT/HLA v3.44.0 for arcasHLA, GENCODE v44 for 
gene-symbol mapping. Smoke tests are provided in tests/ directory and 
verified on the submission environment via pytest.
```
