## Cover letter framing

### Cell Rep Med cover letter draft

```
Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript "[Working title]" for consideration in Cell 
Reports Medicine. Our work establishes a transcriptional differentiation 
axis (DM1/DM2) that stratifies papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) by 
autoimmune-driven dedifferentiation, complementing rather than replacing 
the canonical BRAF/RAS/TERT mutation framework.

Our findings are particularly suited to Cell Rep Med given:

(1) **Direct translational implication**: the 8-gene panel (anchored in 
canonical RAI-uptake biology) is implementable as a NanoString/clinical 
assay for sub-stratifying ATA 2015 intermediate-risk patients.

(2) **Multi-modal evidence integration**: transcriptomic (TCGA n=500 + 
GSE286332 n=18 + GSE213647 n=632) + HLA imputation (Korean Pan-Asian 
n=874 + Chu 2018 Han Chinese GD n=2,958) + Asian autoimmune-overlap 
mechanism (HLA-II 140% mediation, antigen-driven B cell clonal expansion).

(3) **Mechanistic deep dive**: PTC + Hashimoto's overlap as a distinct 
molecular sub-population with quantifiable autoimmune-driven 
dedifferentiation, generalizable across two Korean cohorts.

(4) **Clinical relevance**: identifies the BRAF-/RAS- NBNR (mutation-
negative) cluster as a previously underexamined sub-population with 
elevated Hashimoto-overlap rate, suggesting Korean Asian-specific 
risk factor architecture.

This work also demonstrates cross-disease applicability of cookHLA 
(Cook et al. 2021 Nat Commun) HLA imputation pipeline to thyroid 
oncology, extending our prior work in rheumatoid arthritis, T1D, and 
Crohn's disease.

We thank the editor for considering our submission.

Sincerely,
Seungho Cook (corresponding author)
[Yu professor]
```
