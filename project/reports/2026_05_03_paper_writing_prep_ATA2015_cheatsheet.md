## 1. Three-Tier System Overview

ATA 2015 modified the 2009 framework with **expanded low-risk** + **expanded high-risk** categories. Risk stratification is **dynamic** — recomputed at follow-up based on response to therapy.

### Tier definitions

| Tier | Structural recurrence | Excellent response (NED) |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** | 3-5% | 80-90% |
| **Intermediate** | ~20% | ~60% |
| **High** | 50-75% | <30% |

(Source: Tuttle/Alzahrani JCEM 2019 review, dynamic risk stratification framework)

## 2. Detailed Criteria per Tier

### LOW RISK — meets ALL of:
- No local or distant metastases
- No tumor invasion of locoregional tissues
- Complete macroscopic resection
- No aggressive histology (tall cell, hobnail, columnar variants)
- No vascular invasion
- Clinical N0 OR ≤5 pathologic N1 micrometastases (**<0.2 cm** largest dimension)
- Intrathyroidal encapsulated follicular variant PTC
- **Intrathyroidal papillary microcarcinoma** (unifocal/multifocal, including **BRAF V600E mutated**)
- Intrathyroidal well-differentiated FTC with capsular invasion and **<4 foci vascular invasion**

**Histology subtypes typically low-risk:**
- Standard PTC (intrathyroidal, ≤4 cm)
- Encapsulated follicular variant PTC (no vascular invasion)
- Papillary microcarcinoma (≤1 cm)

### INTERMEDIATE RISK — any of:
- Aggressive histology variants (tall cell, hobnail, columnar — present but not dominant)
- Vascular invasion present
- **Microscopic perithyroidal soft tissue invasion** (microscopic ETE)
- Clinical N1 OR **>5 pathologic N1** with involved nodes <3 cm
- Multifocal papillary microcarcinoma with extrathyroidal extension and BRAF V600E mutation
- RAI-avid metastatic foci in neck on first post-treatment scan

**Histology subtypes typically intermediate-risk:**
- Tall cell variant PTC
- Hobnail variant PTC
- Columnar cell variant PTC

### HIGH RISK — any of:
- **Macroscopic invasion of perithyroidal soft tissues (gross ETE)**
- Distant metastases
- Incomplete tumor resection
- Postoperative serum thyroglobulin suggestive of distant metastases
- Pathologic N1 with **any metastatic node ≥3 cm**
- Follicular thyroid cancer with **>4 foci vascular invasion**

**Histology subtypes typically high-risk:**
- FTC with extensive vascular invasion (>4 foci)
- PDTC (poorly differentiated)
- ATC (anaplastic) — generally outside ATA risk tier system, treated separately

## 3. Cutoff Numbers Summary Table

| Factor | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Lymph node size (low → intermediate boundary)** | **<0.2 cm = low risk** |
| **Lymph node size (intermediate → high boundary)** | **≥3 cm = high risk** |
| **Lymph node count (low → intermediate)** | **>5 N1 = intermediate** |
| **Vascular invasion foci (low → high)** | **<4 = low; >4 = high** |
| **Microcarcinoma definition** | **≤1 cm diameter** |
| **Active surveillance growth trigger** | **3-mm diameter increase** |
| **ETE microscopic vs gross** | Microscopic = intermediate; Gross = high |

## 4. Molecular Markers (BRAF / TERT) per ATA 2015

> "BRAF V600E and TERT promoter mutations appeared to add only marginal benefit to traditional anatomic staging factors for AJCC staging, but **can be used to refine further and individualize risk within risk categories**."

### Specific mentions
- **BRAF V600E alone** in intrathyroidal microcarcinoma → **still LOW risk** (no upgrade)
- **BRAF V600E + multifocal microcarcinoma + ETE** → INTERMEDIATE
- **TERT promoter** → marker for poor outcome (within risk tier individualization)
- **BRAF V600E + TERT promoter co-occurrence** → strongest risk signal (paper opportunity)

→ ATA 2015 stops short of formally upgrading risk based on BRAF/TERT alone — leaves room for our 8-gene panel as **transcriptional axis complement**.

## 5. AJCC TNM 8th Edition (2018) reference

ATA 2015 risk tier ≠ AJCC stage. AJCC stages I-IV based on:
- T: tumor size + ETE
- N: regional lymph nodes
- M: distant metastases
- **Age cutoff <55 vs ≥55 yr** (stage I young, can be stage II if M+)

ATA 2015 risk = recurrence prediction (dynamic, response-driven)
AJCC = mortality prediction (static, anatomic)

→ Both used together in clinical practice. Paper Discussion 3.2 should distinguish: "ATA risk tier predicts recurrence; AJCC stage predicts mortality."

## 6. Korean Thyroid Association (KTA) 2016 Modifications

Korean cohort considerations (Endocrinol Metab 2016;31:373):
- **Indication for total thyroidectomy more conservative** than ATA 2015
- Similar three-tier system but with adjustments for Korean PTC presentation patterns
- Active surveillance for microcarcinoma less commonly applied in Korea
- BRAF V600E testing more routine in Korean clinical practice

→ Paper Methods/Discussion: "Korean clinical practice follows KTA 2016 guidelines, modified from ATA 2015 with attention to Korean PTC presentation patterns."

## 7. Paper-Ready Discussion §3.2 Phrasing (Cell Press paste-ready)

> "The American Thyroid Association 2015 Guidelines¹ stratify differentiated thyroid carcinoma into three risk tiers based on anatomic and pathologic criteria — low (<0.2 cm pathologic lymph node micrometastases, no extrathyroidal extension, ≤5 nodal foci, <4 foci vascular invasion in follicular variants), intermediate (microscopic perithyroidal soft tissue invasion, aggressive histologic variants, >5 pathologic nodes with foci <3 cm, vascular invasion), and high (macroscopic gross extrathyroidal extension, distant metastases, ≥3 cm nodal disease, >4 foci vascular invasion in follicular cancer). Structural recurrence rates range from 3-5% in low-risk to 50-75% in high-risk patients², yet within each tier substantial heterogeneity persists. BRAF V600E and TERT promoter mutations are acknowledged in ATA 2015 as adding 'marginal benefit' to traditional anatomic staging³, indicating an opportunity for transcriptional-axis complementation. Our DM1/DM2 cluster system, anchored in eight differentiation-axis transcripts, provides this orthogonal layer: DM1 (mutation-permissive, RAI-uptake-preserved) and DM2 (Hashimoto-overlap-enriched, dedifferentiation-prone) sub-stratify the same anatomic risk tier."

## 8. Discussion §3.2 supporting bullet points

For paper Discussion §3.2 "ATA 2015 + 8-gene panel complementarity":

1. **Anatomic vs molecular complementarity:** ATA tier predicts recurrence by tumor anatomy; 8-gene panel predicts dedifferentiation by transcriptional state.
2. **Within-tier heterogeneity:** intermediate-risk patients (~20% structural recurrence) show greatest within-tier variability — ideal target for molecular sub-stratification.
3. **BRAF V600E "marginal benefit" gap:** ATA 2015 acknowledges molecular markers are not anatomically dominant, but does not specify a transcriptional differentiation panel.
4. **8-gene NIS-axis:** captures RAI-uptake biology directly, predicting RAI-refractoriness which is the clinical decision point at intermediate-high risk.
5. **DM2-Hashimoto-overlap signal:** PTC+HT (Pillar 5 finding) shows strongest 8-gene RAI score reduction, identifying a sub-population with autoimmune-driven dedifferentiation independent of ATA criteria.
6. **NBNR (BRAF-/RAS-) cluster:** DM1 sub-B = 96% mutation-negative + 4× Hashimoto overlap rate → ATA 2015 anatomic tier may underestimate risk in this population.
