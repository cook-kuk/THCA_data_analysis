# ICI vs Synthetic Lethality Boundary Map

Date: 2026-05-06  
Scope: design-only boundary document; no execution; no new data acquisition

## 1. Shared Foundation

The shared foundation is Paper 1:

- driver-orthogonal thyroid-lineage differentiation axis
- compact 8-gene RAI-lineage readout
- external expression validation as current HIT
- TROP2 demoted from title/main claim

Paper 1 defines the lineage-silenced thyroid cancer state. Paper 3 and Paper 9 use that state for different downstream questions.

## 2. Paper 3: ICI Readiness

Paper 3 focuses on immune vulnerability and ICI readiness.

Core domain:

- immune ecotypes
- HLA intactness
- neoantigen presentability
- antigen-presentation architecture
- TLS/CXCL13 biology
- IFNG/TIS and cytolytic immune activity
- suppressive myeloid and Treg context
- integrated ICI-readiness atlas

Permitted claim space:

- ICI vulnerability
- ICI readiness
- immunogenomic prioritization
- immune-state atlas

Not permitted:

- ICI response predictor
- patient treatment recommendation
- thyroid ICI response validation

## 3. Paper 9: Synthetic Lethality

Paper 9 focuses on cell-intrinsic therapeutic dependency.

Core domain:

- synthetic-lethal and synthetic-rescue hypotheses
- targetable dependencies
- drug-response integration
- lineage-silenced vulnerability discovery
- cell-line and organoid validation
- research-use vulnerability report

Permitted claim space:

- candidate synthetic-lethal dependencies
- state-guided therapeutic vulnerability
- research-use target prioritization
- future validation framework

Not permitted:

- ICI response claim
- patient treatment selection
- proven clinical utility
- TROP2 synthetic-lethality claim without direct evidence

## 4. Overlap

The papers overlap biologically but not in claim space.

Shared or bridging biology:

- STAT3/JAK
- IFNG/antigen presentation
- dedifferentiation context

Interpretation of overlap:

- STAT3/JAK may contribute to immune exclusion in Paper 3 and therapeutic vulnerability in Paper 9.
- IFNG/antigen presentation belongs primarily to Paper 3, but may modify vulnerability states in Paper 9.
- Dedifferentiation context is inherited from Paper 1 and should be treated as the state variable that both papers condition on.

## 5. Boundary

Paper 3 boundary:

- does not rank small-molecule dependencies
- does not claim synthetic lethality
- does not perform drug-response prioritization
- does not recommend therapy

Paper 9 boundary:

- does not claim ICI response
- does not validate immunotherapy benefit
- does not use immune readiness as a treatment-selection claim
- does not replace Paper 3's antigen-presentation and TLS framework

Practical separation:

- Paper 3 answers: "Which lineage-silenced thyroid tumors are immunogenomically ICI-ready?"
- Paper 9 answers: "Which lineage-silenced thyroid tumors may have targetable cell-intrinsic dependencies?"

## 6. Future Integrated Business Vision

Long-term integrated product direction:

- molecular triage
- ICI readiness
- synthetic-lethality vulnerability
- trial matching

Potential workflow:

1. Use Paper 1 to assign lineage-silencing state.
2. Use Paper 3 logic to summarize ICI readiness and immune barriers.
3. Use Paper 9 logic to nominate cell-intrinsic therapeutic vulnerabilities.
4. Add trial matching only after sufficient validation and regulatory review.

The integrated vision should remain research-use until clinical validation exists.
