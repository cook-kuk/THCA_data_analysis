## ¶2 — Why Cell Rep Med (fact-only, paste-ready)

```
Our work is particularly suited to Cell Reports Medicine for four reasons:

(1) Direct translational implication — the eight-gene panel (anchored in 
canonical RAI-uptake biology: SLC5A5/NIS, TPO, TG, TSHR, PAX8, NKX2-1, 
FOXE1, DIO1) is implementable as a NanoString or qRT-PCR clinical assay 
for sub-stratifying ATA 2015 intermediate-risk patients, with structural 
recurrence rates ranging from 3-5% in low-risk to 50-75% in high-risk 
tiers — precisely the population where transcriptional axis information 
would alter management.

(2) Multi-modal evidence integration — we combine transcriptomic 
(TCGA-THCA n=500 + GSE286332 n=18 + GSE213647 n=632), HLA imputation 
(Korean Pan-Asian n=874 + Chu et al. 2018 Han Chinese GD n=2,958), and 
mediation analysis to establish a five-pillar evidence structure.

(3) Mechanistic clarity — Baron-Kenny mediation analysis with 5,000 
bootstrap iterations identifies HLA-II module as a 140% mediator 
(over-mediation, full-pathway) of the PTC + Hashimoto's overlap → 
P(DM1) ↓ relationship, establishing antigen-driven autoimmune 
dedifferentiation as a quantifiable mechanism.

(4) Clinical relevance — we identify the BRAF-/RAS- mutation-negative 
"NBNR" cluster (DM1 sub-B, n=56 in TCGA, replicated 47-53% in Korean 
GSE213647) as a previously underexamined sub-population with 4× elevated 
Hashimoto-overlap rate, suggesting a Korean-Asian autoimmune-driven 
differentiated PTC subtype.
```

## ¶3 — Cross-disease and methodology contribution (fact-only)

```
This work also extends our prior cookHLA pipeline (Cook et al. 2021 
Nature Communications) — originally developed for rheumatoid arthritis, 
type 1 diabetes, and Crohn's disease HLA imputation — to thyroid 
oncology, demonstrating cross-disease applicability of HLA imputation 
methods in Asian populations. The DPB1*05:01 53.2% carrier frequency 
in our pooled Korean PTC cohort (n=874) replicates the Asian Graves' 
disease risk allele frequency of 44.0% from Chu et al. 2018 (J Med Genet 
55(10):685-692, doi:10.1136/jmedgenet-2017-105146) and exceeds the 
Han Chinese control frequency of 31.3%, establishing a Pan-Asian 
autoimmune-thyroid susceptibility allele continuum (random-effects 
DerSimonian-Laird pooled OR 2.16, 95% CI 1.65-2.83).

The work directly addresses a stated gap in the 2015 American Thyroid 
Association Guidelines (Haugen et al. 2016, Thyroid 26:1-133), which 
acknowledges that "BRAF V600E and TERT promoter mutations appeared to 
add only marginal benefit to traditional anatomic staging factors" — 
indicating an opportunity for transcriptional-axis complementation that 
our DM1/DM2 framework provides.
```
