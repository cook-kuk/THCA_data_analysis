# v13 Executive Brief

## One-Page Summary

v13 converts the THCA subtype pipeline into a therapeutic decision surface. The practical output is a ranked map of what can be acted on now, what needs biomarker enrichment, and what belongs in a next-wave modality program.

## Headline

**Highest-priority immediate opportunity: `TACSTD2 / TROP2` ADC repurposing in BRAF-like thyroid cancer.**

Why this is the lead:

- Surface antigen biology fits ADC better than small molecules.
- The target is already clinically drugged outside thyroid cancer.
- Two thyroid-relevant TROP2 trials are already visible in the public registry layer.
- A BRAF-like + `TACSTD2`-high enrichment strategy is a plausible correlative add-on rather than a de novo platform build.

## What v13 Delivered

- `8` targets with structure provenance
- `2443` FDA-library compounds screened
- `10` therapeutic modalities compared for each target
- `400` ranked candidate rows
- `59` Tier A candidates
- `60` TROP2/thyroid literature records
- `2` thyroid-relevant TROP2 ADC trials

## Strategic Readout

### Go-now

- `TACSTD2 / TROP2`
  Best immediate translational thesis.
  Recommended framing: biomarker-enriched ADC repurposing.

- `CYP1B1`
  Best small-molecule-native target.
  Recommended framing: compact chemistry follow-up and validation.

### Develop-next

- `TMPRSS4`
  Medium pocketing, biologically plausible, but not yet clean enough for a premium claim.

- `LDLR`
  Strong repurposing signal numerically, but specificity to thyroid oncology remains weaker than the TROP2 story.

### Emerging modality program

- `PLEKHA6`
  Better as `PROTAC / siRNA / ASO` than classic repurposing.

- `B3GNT3`
  Best framed through `siRNA / ASO` and immunotherapy interaction logic.

- `PTPRE`
  Small-molecule fit remains weak; modality innovation is more credible than direct repurposing.

## Business / Translation Angle

There are two distinct value propositions:

1. Near-term translational paper / correlative study
   `TACSTD2`-guided enrichment within ongoing or near-term thyroid ADC trials.

2. Platform thesis
   A single subtype-aware engine that routes targets into different modality classes rather than forcing every hit through a small-molecule repurposing lens.

That second point is important: v13 is stronger as a **decision architecture** than as a list of drug names.

## Do Not Oversell

- The virtual screen is similarity-based, not docking-validated.
- ADMET is proxy-based in this run.
- Tier A does not mean clinically validated in thyroid cancer.
- The strongest numerical hits are not automatically the best thyroid-cancer story.
- `TACSTD2` is the best clinical narrative even though its small-molecule list is not the strongest.

## Recommended Next Moves

1. Build the TROP2 thyroid brief into a standalone translational package.
2. Prioritize a small wet-lab shortlist for `CYP1B1`.
3. Keep `PLEKHA6 / B3GNT3 / PTPRE` as modality-expansion stories, not immediate repurposing headlines.
4. Use the v13 dashboard as the main communication artifact for collaborators, with the TROP2 section as the lead view.
