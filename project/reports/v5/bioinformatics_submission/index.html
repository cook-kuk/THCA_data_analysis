DIAL: a direction-invariant audit metric for batch correction, and what happens when ComBat is fit before the cross-cohort split

⚠ v5.2 SELF-AUDIT (2026-04-25): The v5.1 ``thyroid-specific batch_entanglement (DIAL = 0.494, 4-of-5)'' headline was an artefact of fitting ComBat on the full pooled X before the LODO split. Under proper per-fold ComBat (v5p2_combat_lodo.py) THCA returns DIAL = 0.000 / AUC_post = 0.995 / true_biology for all five classifiers (ΔDIAL = −0.494). Title, abstract, Section 5, Discussion, and Conclusion of the PDFs below have been rewritten to report this honestly. The honest finding is that DIAL diagnosed the leakage as designed; the per-fold ComBat protocol is the constructive fix.

Abstract

Structured abstract — 164 words.

Motivation. Public cancer expression cohorts (TCGA and GEO) are routinely combined via empirical-Bayes batch correction (ComBat) to train molecular subtype classifiers. When label and batch directions share a linear subspace, correction can invert the classifier's decision axis — a failure mode invisible to standard cross-validated AUC.

Results. We introduce DIAL (Direction-Invariant AUC Leakage), a post-hoc metric that flags this inversion under Leave-One-Dataset-Out evaluation. We state a label-flip lemma characterising the geometric conditions under which it fires. Applying DIAL to five cancers (THCA, SKCM, LGG, LUAD, COAD) across five classifier families, thyroid BRAF/RAS classification flips in 4 of 5 classifiers (DIAL up to 0.494, AUCpost as low as 0.006), while 20 of 20 non-thyroid combinations show no flip. Random-effects meta-analysis yields mTHCA = 1.00 with I² > 97 %. FastRNA-style cohort centering eliminates the flip, identifying ComBat over-correction as the mechanism. The flip reproduces under a variational quantum classifier, ruling out a linear-model artefact.

Conclusion. DIAL is a diagnostic, not a correction. It flags batch-entangled gene-level classifiers while pathway-level analyses remain resilient.
