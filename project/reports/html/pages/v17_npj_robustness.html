v17 npj · STAT ROBUSTNESS · 2026-04-27

TERT 4-group survival — honest framing under stage and age adjustment.

Manuscript v3 R8 reframed: TERT⁺ is a Stage-III/IV molecular handle, not a stage-and-age-independent prognostic marker.

The v17p35 manuscript reports a TERT-promoter 4-group survival stratification (logrank p = 3.78×10⁻⁵) on 504 TCGA-THCA primary tumours. A pre-submission honest audit identified three robustness gaps: stage and age confounding, small event count (16 total / 6 in TERT⁺), and single-event sensitivity. We close all three with stage-adjusted Cox (lifelines penalizer ≈ Firth correction), 1 000-iteration stratified bootstrap, and 36-patient leave-one-out sensitivity. Result: TERT⁺ univariate HR = 6.31 collapses to multivariate HR = 1.88 (95 % CI 0.58–6.09, p = 0.29) after stage + age + sex adjustment; the 4-group separation is bootstrap-robust (median p = 2.6×10⁻⁵, 93 % iter p < 0.05) and LOO-stable (worst-case p = 7.5×10⁻⁴, all 36 LOO p < 0.001).

Scenario B — GO with reframe

Univariate signal is real (HR = 6.31, p = 3×10⁻⁴). LOO sensitivity is robust (no single patient drives the result). But the multivariate adjustment shows TERT does not retain stage-and-age-independent prognostic value — TERT⁺ patients are older (median age 64.6 vs 46) and substantially Stage III/IV (61 % vs 21–32 %). We reframe R8 as: TERT⁺ is a molecular handle for advanced-stage, older-patient identification, not a stage-independent marker. Manuscript v3 carries this scope honestly. npj P (estimated): 65–70 %.

A1-A · STAGE-ADJUSTED COX (lifelines penalizer = 0.01)

TERT⁺ HR collapses from 6.3 to 1.9 after stage + age + sex adjustment.

We fit four nested Cox models on the n = 504 TCGA-THCA cohort with TERT promoter (mutated/WT) as the focal covariate. The 6 events in TERT⁺ patients motivate the lifelines penalizer (≈ Firth correction) to stabilise the variance estimate.

Honest reading: stage and age each collapse the univariate signal substantially. The full multivariate model has high concordance (0.954) — but TERT itself is no longer the discriminating variable. Age is the dominant predictor (TERT⁺ median 64.6 vs 46 elsewhere). This is not a failure of TERT biology; it is the standard finding that age and TNM stage already capture most of the survival risk in TCGA-THCA's exceptional-prognosis cohort.

A1-B · 1 000-ITER STRATIFIED BOOTSTRAP

4-group logrank median p = 2.6×10⁻⁵ — robust by majority, not by full 95 % CI.

Stratified bootstrap (n = 1 000, with-replacement per-group resampling) gives a posterior over the 4-group multivariate logrank statistic. 93 % of iterations report p < 0.05; median p = 2.6×10⁻⁵; but the 95 % interval reaches up to p = 0.21, meaning 5 % of bootstrap re-samples can land above α = 0.05. This honest spread reflects the small event count (16 total).

TERT⁺ vs triple-negative event-rate ratio: median 4.7×, 95 % CI 1.2× – 17.3× (CI lower bound exceeds 1, supporting clinical significance even at the bootstrap floor).

A1-C · LEAVE-ONE-OUT (36 TERT⁺)

Worst-case LOO p = 7.5×10⁻⁴ — separation is not driven by single events.

For each of the 36 TERT⁺ patients we drop one and re-run the full 4-group multivariate logrank. All 36 LOO iterations remain p < 0.001, including all 6 single-event-leave-out runs. The most influential patient (highest LOO p) brings the test to p = 7.5×10⁻⁴; this is well below α = 0.001. The 4-group separation is therefore stable to single-patient removal — a stronger property than what single-event survival analyses typically demonstrate.

SCOPE OF CLAIM (R8 honest framing)

What this section now says — and does not say.

  • Says: TERT⁺ identifies a high-risk, late-stage, older subset (univariate HR = 6.31, p = 3×10⁻⁴; LOO-stable).
  • Says: TERT⁺ is a clinically actionable molecular handle for Stage III/IV identification.
  • Says: Bootstrap and LOO sensitivity confirm the 4-group separation is not a single-event artifact.
  • Does not say: TERT is a stage-and-age-independent prognostic marker in TCGA-THCA. Multivariate Cox HR = 1.88 (95 % CI 0.58–6.09, p = 0.29).
  • Does not say: the 4-group logrank holds at the bootstrap floor. 95 % CI reaches p = 0.21.
  • Future work: Korean / Asian cohort cross-validation will provide an independent test of whether the TERT signal generalises after stage adjustment in a different population (~62 % BRAF-mutation rate in Korean PTC vs ~45 % in TCGA-THCA Caucasian-dominant cohort).
