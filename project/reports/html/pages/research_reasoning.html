RESEARCH REASONING · v2 → v5.1 → v8

How we arrived at the THCA BRAF/RAS batch-entanglement finding.

Iteration chain for external clinical review

This page is a reasoning-chain walkthrough of five iterations (v2, v3, v4, v5, v5.1) and two downstream supplements (v7 translational, v8 methodological) that together produced the current THCA-specific DIAL result. It is written for face-to-face review with a clinical collaborator rather than as a dashboard dump: every version records what we believed we would show, what we actually observed, and what that forced us to change next.

Section 2 · Iteration tree

The reasoning chain, version by version.

Seven cards below, in chronological order. Each card is a single claim plus three lines: the hypothesis going in, the observation coming out, and the change it forced on the next version. Reading top to bottom reconstructs how a retraction in v5 became a cleaner, narrower, mechanistically-grounded THCA result by v5.1, v7, and v8.

v2First pan-cancer pilot · late 2025

A single TCGA cohort per cancer was enough to train BRAF/RAS classifiers at AUC > 0.95 — but was it enough to validate them?

What we believed we'd show
That a single TCGA RNA-seq cohort per cancer would be sufficient to train BRAF/RAS-style subtype classifiers at AUC > 0.9, and that the classifier AUC would stand as the headline number of the pilot.
What we actually observed
In-cohort AUC > 0.95 across all five cancers. Zero external validation — we had never shown the classifier worked anywhere other than the cohort it was trained on.
What that forced us to change
Every subsequent version had to include a second cohort per cancer. An in-cohort AUC alone was retired from all headline positions.
v3Two-cohort extension · early 2026

Adding a second cohort and harmonising with pyComBat looked routine — until the AUCs dropped and stayed inconsistent.

What we believed we'd show
That adding a GEO microarray cohort per cancer and harmonising via pyComBat would give us externally validated AUCs at a similar level to v2 in-cohort.
What we actually observed
Cross-cohort AUC collapsed to the 0.6 – 0.8 range, unevenly across cancers. Some pairings still looked clean; others did not. The scatter was not a uniform generalisation gap — it was cohort-specific.
What that forced us to change
We stopped reporting a cross-cohort AUC without an accompanying batch-effect diagnostic. It was no longer acceptable to say "we ComBat'd it"; we had to quantify how much of the signal the correction was creating or destroying.
v4DIAL introduced · the founding metric

A scalar — DIAL — made the THCA polarity inversion legible for the first time.

What we believed we'd show
That a single scalar, DIAL = auc_flip(auc_post) − 0.5 if auc_post < 0.5 else 0, would cleanly flag the THCA cohort pairing (TCGA-THCA × GSE27155) where ComBat inverts the classifier polarity instead of just degrading it.
What we actually observed
On THCA leave-one-dataset-out (LODO), DIAL ≈ 0.314, auc_post ≈ 0.186, with a shared-gene count of 11 731. The classifier was not merely weakened by harmonisation — its direction was flipped. This became the founding result of the project.
What that forced us to change
We had to extend the audit to more cancers. If DIAL was a real diagnostic, it could not be a THCA curiosity — we needed to know whether the flip was a feature of ComBat in general or a property specific to this one tissue.
v5 · retractedAttempted pan-cancer claim · synthetic surrogates

We tried to turn DIAL into a pan-cancer diagnostic. It failed for a reason worth preserving as evidence.

What we believed we'd show
That DIAL would fire on every cancer with a sufficient cohort-subtype confound, and that we could present v5 as a pan-cancer diagnostic for batch-entangled classifier failure.
What we actually observed
Four of the five cancers (SKCM, LGG, LUAD, COAD) had no viable second real cohort once inclusion criteria were applied. v5 fell back to semi-synthetic data anchored to THCA statistics for those four — so the "pan-cancer" result was, in effect, built on surrogates of the one positive case.
What that forced us to change
v5 was retracted. The analysis was archived to results/v5_broken/ as retraction evidence. The successor iteration was required to use 100% real data, with no synthetic surrogate admitted even at the cost of losing the pan-cancer framing.
v5.1 · current primary resultReal-data LODO rerun

On real cohorts only, DIAL fires on THCA alone — a specificity result, not a generalisation failure.

What we believed we'd show
That with real cohorts only and LODO evaluation, DIAL would fire on whichever cancers happened to satisfy the confound conditions — we were prepared for any of the five to light up.
What we actually observed
THCA alone flips: 4 of 5 classifiers, DIAL up to 0.494, auc_post as low as 0.006. SKCM, LGG, LUAD, COAD return 0/5 flips across 20 classifier–cancer combinations. This is specificity, not failure. See v5p1_dial_all_cancers.tsv and v5p1_table1_summary.tsv.
What that forced us to change
We reframed the paper from pan-cancer DIAL diagnostic to subtype-cohort-specific audit with a positive arm (THCA) and a negative arm (SKCM / LGG / LUAD / COAD). The negative arm is load-bearing — it is what converts a single flip into a finding.
v7 · translationalDrug repurposing overlay

With the audit stable, the eight druggable targets became a tractable repurposing question.

What we believed we'd show
That the eight druggable targets carried forward from v4 and v5.1 (TACSTD2, TMPRSS4, PLEKHA6, CYP1B1, LDLR, GABRB2, B3GNT3, PTPRE) would surface actionable FDA-approved repurposing candidates through the Open Targets / DrugCentral / ChEMBL stack.
What we actually observed
83 drug–target interactions returned; top-ranked candidate cannabidiol (score 0.72); two FDA-approved drugs (against CYP1B1 and GABRB2); and three CYP1B1-anchored combination hypotheses. Open Targets knownDrugs degraded due to a schema mismatch, but the pipeline completed via DrugCentral + ChEMBL without losing the primary candidates.
What that forced us to change
v8 had to confirm the eight targets still rank robustly under a more rigorous DE test (DESeq2) and under LMM cohort correction — otherwise the repurposing shortlist inherited whatever gene-ranking fragility a Welch-t-test might carry.
v8 · reviewer-proofingStatistical-genetics rigor + quantum cross-check

Six parallel robustness analyses. The flip survives every axis we can put it on, and one axis names the mechanism.

What we believed we'd show
That six parallel robustness analyses would pre-empt the reviewer's statistical-methodology objections — batch estimator, effect-size scale, feature space, DE test, classifier paradigm, and cohort structure — without moving the headline THCA number.
What we actually observed
  • S1 ComBat-seq. 4/5 THCA classifiers robust (|ΔDIAL| < 0.1).
  • S2 Random-effects meta. m_THCA ≈ 1.00 under 4/5 classifiers; I² > 97%; XGBoost as a negative control with m = 0.50 uniformly. See v8_metasoft_results.tsv.
  • S3 Pathway DIAL. Gene-level 0.49 → pathway-level 0.000 across all 50 MSigDB Hallmark pathways; no pathway flips. Reframed as "gene-level early warning, pathway-level resilience".
  • S4 DESeq2. 8/8 druggable targets retained — the v7 backbone is safe.
  • S5 Quantum. VQC also flips THCA (DIAL 0.386); non-THCA SVC_RBF returns DIAL = 0 — paradigm-invariant specificity. See v8_quantum_comparison.tsv.
  • S6 HanLab. BUHMBOX confirms cohort = PC1 within each subtype (KS p < 1e-40); FastRNA-style cohort centering drops DIAL to 0. Mechanism identified: covariate-preserving ComBat over-corrects when cohort and subtype are non-orthogonal. See v8_buhmbox_concept_check.tsv and v8_fastRNA_style_dial.tsv.
What that forced us to change
We promoted the S6·0 mechanism subsection to main-paper §4.8, and kept S6A / S6B / S6C as appendix material. We also commissioned a v5.2 audit pass against the v5.1 ComBat×LODO implementation — what we expected to be a final reproducibility check became the most important pivot of the project (next card).
v5.2 · honest pivotLeak audit retraction

The v5.1 headline finding does not survive proper LODO. The flip was a covariate-aware ComBat leak fitted on pooled train+test.

What we believed we'd show
That the v5.2 audit pass — ComBat·LODO leakage check, sample-count consistency in Table 1, interpretation-threshold sensitivity at ±0.05, METASOFT bootstrap, VQC five-seed, GSE193581 cellular switch, Dockerfile reproducibility — would tighten the bolts and ship.
What we actually observed
  • F1 (LODO leak fix). v5.1's compute_dial called _combat_preserve(X_raw, Y, B) on the full pooled matrix before the LODO split (v5p1_common.py:167). The empirical-Bayes parameters (γ, δ, α, β) were therefore fit using held-out cohort samples under par_prior=True, covar_mod=Y. Re-running with leak-safe per-fold ComBat collapses all 4 of 4 THCA batch_entangled cells to true_biology — THCA LogReg_l2 goes from auc_post 0.006 / DIAL 0.494 to auc_post 0.995 / DIAL 0.000 (Δ DIAL = −0.494). See v5p1_dial_all_cancers_F1.tsv.
  • F12 (cellular check). Switching v6 from GSE184362 (no BRAF/RAS metadata) to GSE193581 (Lu 2023 JCI, n=8 with explicit BRAF/RAS calls) gives cellular pseudobulk AUC 0.80, DIAL 0.000. Confirms F1: there is no flip to reproduce at the cellular layer because the bulk flip was a cross-cohort evaluation artefact, not a biological signal.
  • What does survive. Independent of leak: S6B BUHMBOX (cohort = PC1 within subtype, KS p < 1e-40 — pre-ComBat structural observation), S4 DESeq2 (8/8 druggable retained), S6C LMM biomarkers (88.6 % survival, 3,000-gene LMM intersect with DESeq2 = 1,786-gene gold slate), Table 1 fix (F2: arithmetically valid cohort schema), Dockerfile reproducibility (F10). Conditional on leak: S1 ComBat-seq robustness, S2 METASOFT m_THCA = 1.00, S3 pathway "gene-level only" claim, S5 quantum paradigm-invariance — all framed around a flip that no longer exists.
  • F4 / F6 corroboration. Threshold sensitivity (±0.05) shows the v5.1 specificity within the leaky regime is robust (0 non-THCA gain entanglement) but the THCA flip count is fragile (4 → 2 if dial_high + 0.05). METASOFT bootstrap weakens m_THCA from 1.00 toward the 0.50 prior. Both are consistent with v5.1 having been at the edge of its threshold band.
What that forced us to change
We pivot the paper from "DIAL detects a THCA-specific ComBat failure" to "covariate-aware ComBat fit on pooled train+test produces classifier-inversion artefacts in LODO when the data have a subtype-cohort confound". The structural prerequisite (S6B BUHMBOX cohort = PC1) is preserved as the necessary condition under which the leak manifests; the v8 supplements are reframed as stress-tests of the leaky evaluation regime rather than confirmations of biology. The retraction header is on the v5.1 LaTeX (reports/v5/v5p1_paper.tex line 2); the v5.2 critical assessment lives at reports/v5p2/v5p2_critical_assessment.md; the v5.2 leak-safe re-run is at v5p1_dial_all_cancers_F1.tsv. The new contribution is methodological and broader than the original positive claim: every cross-cohort ML evaluation that combines LODO with covariate-aware ComBat fit on pooled data inherits the same leak budget.
Section 3 · Four reasoning layers

The meta-level frame the paper ends on.

Four questions the paper answers, each one layer deeper than the previous. These are the frame we want the reader (and reviewer) to walk away with — the technical cards above exist to support them.

L1 · Retraction lesson

"What went wrong with v5?"

v5 attempted a pan-cancer claim by admitting semi-synthetic cohorts for four of the five cancers. The moment one substitutes synthetic surrogates anchored to the positive case, the analysis stops testing the hypothesis and starts confirming it. v5.1 corrects this by restricting to real cohorts only — and learns that the right story was not pan-cancer but THCA-specific.

Ref · results/v5_broken/ retained as retraction evidence.
L2 · Evaluation lesson

"Why does StratifiedKFold mask batch effects?"

Stratified k-fold splits both cohorts' samples into every fold, so every training set already contains the batch signature of every test set. The classifier learns a mixture of biology plus platform, and the test AUC stays inflated because the batch variable is never held out. It is an in-distribution metric marketed as an out-of-distribution one.

Ref · v2 pilot, in-cohort AUC > 0.95, zero external validation.
L3 · Cross-validation lesson

"Why does LODO reveal them?"

Leave-one-dataset-out withholds an entire cohort at test time. The training set no longer contains the test set's platform, so platform-leakage cannot help the classifier. Under LODO the THCA classifier not only loses accuracy, it inverts (auc_post as low as 0.006) — a signature that stratified k-fold would have suppressed and that shared-gene counts alone cannot diagnose.

Ref · v5.1 DIAL up to 0.494 on THCA LODO.
L4 · Mechanism lesson

"Why THCA specifically?"

In THCA, PC1 within each subtype is cohort identity (BUHMBOX KS p < 1e-40) — cohort and subtype are structurally non-orthogonal. Covariate-preserving ComBat tries to remove cohort while protecting subtype, but when the two axes are collinear the correction over-shrinks subtype variance and can invert it. FastRNA-style per-cohort centering avoids the inversion, which is why DIAL drops to zero under it.

Ref · v8 S6 · FastRNA + BUHMBOX convergent evidence.

Section 5 · Future study design

Next steps.

Five concrete items for the next external-validation planning meeting.

  • Prospective external cohort. Independent thyroid surgery archive, targeting n ≥ 100 BRAF/RAS-typed FFPE RNA-seq samples. This is the single most load-bearing item — a prospective cohort is what converts the computational result into a clinically-grounded one.
  • External replication of the 8-gene druggable panel. Run the v7 targets (TACSTD2, TMPRSS4, PLEKHA6, CYP1B1, LDLR, GABRB2, B3GNT3, PTPRE) on the external cohort as a preregistered replication of the v5.1 / v7 ranking.
  • Clinical timeline. First 30 samples Q3 2026; interim read Q4 2026; paper resubmission Q1 2027 with a prospective arm attached.
  • Decision: DE framework. Which of DESeq2 / LMM / hybrid should be the preregistered primary analysis. Lock the choice before unblinding the external cohort.
  • Co-authorship and IRB scope. Agreement that v1 IRB scope is THCA only, BRAF/RAS only. Extension to IDH / EGFR / other subtype axes will be a post-hoc amendment, not embedded in v1.

Caveat

Findings remain computational until external cohort validation. No clinical recommendations implied. DIAL is an audit metric for cohort-harmonised classifiers — it is not a patient- facing score, and the 8-gene druggable panel is a repurposing hypothesis list, not a treatment recommendation.
