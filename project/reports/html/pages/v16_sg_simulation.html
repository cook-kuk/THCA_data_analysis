T1 · SN-38 ↔ TopoI structural analysis

PDB 1K4T Top1-DNA-camptothecin ternary complex.

summary.json contacts.tsv vina_protocol.sh 1K4T.pdb

data + method

PDB: 1K4T (Top1-DNA-camptothecin-class ternary complex, Staker et al. 2002 PNAS). Fetched live from RCSB. Parsed via Biopython 1.87 PDBParser + NeighborSearch.
Ligand identified in 1K4T: resname TTG chain C resnum 991 (32 atoms). TTG = topotecan in this entry — a closely related camptothecin scaffold to SN-38, sharing the same Top1-trapping binding mode. SN-38 redocking was not run (no Vina binary in environment); crystal pose used as reference.
Reproducer: vina_protocol.sh contains full ligand prep + box.conf + Vina run + RMSD calc — runnable in any environment with autodock-vina + openbabel + adfr-suite.

top 10 contacting residues (within 5Å of ligand)

  • DT10 (DNA base, +1 stacking)
  • LYS532
  • ASP533
  • DA113 (DNA base, −1 stacking)
  • ASN722 (lactone H-bond)
  • ARG364 (catalytic, lactone H-bond)
  • THR718
  • DC112 (DNA base)
  • GLU356
  • ASN352

literature reference affinity

camptothecin-class ligands at Top1-DNA interface: ~−9 to −11 kcal/mol (Staker 2002 PNAS). SN-38 specifically: ~−10 kcal/mol from published Vina re-dockings of related Top1-camptothecin entries. The crystal pose recovers the canonical DNA-base-stacking + ARG364/ASN722 H-bond fingerprint; these contacts are the SAR-defining interactions.

Skipped: actual AutoDock Vina re-dock + RMSD vs crystal. To run: install autodock-vina + openbabel + adfr-suite, then bash vina_protocol.sh in the artefact directory.

T4 · BRAF V600E → TF → TACSTD2 mediator inference

Local DE × Claude sonnet literature reasoning.

braf_tacstd2_pathway.json mediator_TFs.md

왜 CellOracle 대신 이렇게 했나

CellOracle/SCENIC requires thyroid-specific scATAC + TF-motif prior, neither of which was available in this environment. 대안: 우리 실데이터(biomarker_validated.tsv)에서 후보 TF들의 BRAF-like 차등 발현을 측정 → Claude sonnet에게 그 stats + 문헌 prior로 mediator 5개를 지명하게 함. 결과는 hypothesis 수준이지 GRN-derived ground truth가 아님.

top 5 inferred mediators

FOSL2 high confidence · log2FC = +1.12, d = +1.38

AP-1 component directly activated by MAPK/ERK. BRAF V600E → ERK → AP-1 axis is canonical. Direction matches TACSTD2's BRAF-like pattern.

Anchor: Murphy et al. Nat Rev Cancer 2013 — AP-1 in cancer progression.

STAT1 high confidence · log2FC = +1.47, d = +1.58

Strongest STAT family upregulation in our DE. STAT1 can be activated downstream of MAPK via interferon-signalling crosstalk, and regulates epithelial differentiation + cell-surface markers in thyroid cancer.

Anchor: Kimura et al. Endocr Relat Cancer 2003.

STAT3 high confidence · log2FC = +0.49, d = +1.22

MAPK ↔ cytokine convergence node; frequently activated in BRAF-mutant thyroid cancers; directly regulates epithelial cell adhesion molecules (TROP2 family inclusive).

Anchor: Borrello et al. Oncogene 2005 — STAT3 in thyroid transformation.

RELA (NF-κB p65) medium · log2FC = +0.34, d = +1.20

Activated downstream of MAPK; regulates epithelial adhesion programs.

MYC medium · log2FC = +0.65, d = +0.66

Generic proliferation TF; supports transcription of cell-surface receptors broadly.

hypothesised path

BRAF V600E → ERK → AP-1 (FOSL2) + STAT1/STAT3 + NF-κB (RELA) → TACSTD2 promoter activation → TROP2 surface expression up. The simultaneous up-regulation of multiple converging regulators rather than a single master TF explains why TACSTD2 induction is robust across BRAF V600E PTC samples.

study design (3 arms × 3 cell lines)

ArmTreatmentModelsHypothesis

BFree topotecan (payload control)B-CPAP · CAL-62BRAF+ ≈ RAS+ (payload alone)
CVehicle모든 linebaseline tumour growth

sample size

Power 80%, α 0.05 (two-sided), Cohen d ≈ 1.4 on log tumour volume → n = 9 per group; with 10% attrition n = 12 per arm. Total ≈ 80 mice.

primary endpoint

Tumour volume at day 21 (caliper, blinded). Linear mixed model: log_volume ~ arm × day + (1|mouse). Pairwise contrasts Bonferroni-corrected.

biomarker readout

  • IHC panel: TROP2 (EPR20043), Top1, Ki67, cleaved-caspase-3, γH2AX, CD8/CD45.
  • Bulk RNA-seq endpoint: 36 samples, GSEA against MAPK output + DNA damage hallmarks.
  • Computational readouts: 54-gene panel score · MAPK output score · TopoI damage score · TROP2 axis score (전부 v3-v15 pipeline에 정의됨).
