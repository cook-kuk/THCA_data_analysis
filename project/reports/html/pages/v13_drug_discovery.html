v13 · INTEGRATED DRUG DISCOVERY · BUILD 2026-04-25

From transcriptomic signature to multi-modality therapeutic landscape.

v13 extends the v7 repurposing atlas into a complete in-silico drug-discovery pipeline over the eight BRAF-like druggable targets identified in v5.1 / v11 / v12. For every target we (i) acquire a 3D structure (PDB or AlphaFold2), (ii) detect candidate ligand pockets and score druggability, (iii) run a 2,443-compound FDA-library virtual screen, (iv) evaluate ten therapeutic modalities — small molecule, mAb, ADC, bispecific, siRNA, PROTAC, CAR-T, radioligand, mRNA, ASO — and (v) filter hits by ADMET red-flags. The strongest finding is an ADC repurposing rationale for TACSTD2 (TROP2) in BRAF-like thyroid cancer, anchored to two active Ph2 trials.

§D · spotlightTROP2 ADC — immediate repurposing opportunity

🔥 FLAGSHIP FINDING

Sacituzumab govitecan & sacituzumab tirumotecan in BRAF-like PTC

Our v13 signature ranks TACSTD2 (TROP2) among the top BRAF-like-up-regulated targets. Two dedicated phase-2 thyroid trials are already recruiting or poised to open, treating unselected TROP2-positive thyroid cancer. Layering the v13 BRAF-like classifier plus TACSTD2 expression as a correlative biomarker could (a) enrich for responders and (b) spin a separate short-form clinical-translation paper.

    §F · tier-2 opportunitiesNovel modality hypotheses

    Targets where no approved or clinical small molecule exists today, but where emerging modalities provide a credible handle. Tier-2 work (v15 preview) will generate novel chemistry via REINVENT4 transfer-learning from known binders.

    PROTAC candidate
    PLEKHA6
    PH-domain scaffold; best in-silico hits (thalidomide / lenalidomide / pomalidomide) all carry CRBN-warhead chemistry. Logical starting point for a bespoke PLEKHA6 degrader.
    PROTAC / siRNA
    PTPRE
    Receptor PTP with shallow pocket (PLB 0.39 → low). PTP-family degraders (cf. SHP2) are a growing field; siRNA preclinical data available in breast/lymphoma.
    siRNA / ASO
    B3GNT3
    Golgi glycosyltransferase. Knockdown rescues anti-PD-1 in Cell 2018. Structural pocket (PLB 0.58) borderline; mRNA-level modulation is the practical handle.
    ADC + radioligand
    TACSTD2 (TROP2)
    Already in the clinic for breast / urothelial / lung. Active thyroid Ph2 (NCT06235216, NCT07521670). Also: [68Ga]Ga-NOTA-T4 ImmunoPET imaging (preclinical).
    siRNA / mAb
    TMPRSS4
    No approved selective small molecule. Nafamostat cross-reactivity is limited; anti-TMPRSS4 mAb (Kim 2020) provides proof-of-concept handle for surface targeting.
    siRNA / mRNA replacement
    LDLR
    Inclisiran (PCSK9 siRNA) already approved for indirect LDLR upregulation. LDLR mRNA replacement under investigation by Moderna for homozygous FH — relevance to THCA metabolic phenotype to explore.

    §H · methodsMethods at a glance

    This page is a synthesis layer over the v13 scripted pipeline, not a hand-written narrative only. The items below state exactly which computational shortcuts were used and where caution is required.

    1. Structure acquisition used RCSB PDB first and AlphaFold DB fallback second; 5 targets used experimental structures and 3 used AF2.
    2. Pocketing used the in-repo alpha-sphere-lite heuristic with HETATM ligand-site rescue, not P2Rank or GNINA.
    3. Virtual screening used Morgan/ECFP4 Tanimoto similarity against curated reference binders because full docking was not executed in this run.
    4. ADMET used the implemented RDKit proxy model and rule-based alerts because `admet-ai` was not available in the active environment.
    5. Composite prioritization combined binding, ADMET, maturity, off-patent status, thyroid PubMed support, modality fit, and repurposing preference.
    6. TROP2 thyroid-trial evidence came from ClinicalTrials.gov v2; literature counts came from NCBI E-utilities.
