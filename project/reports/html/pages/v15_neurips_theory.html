Unified Shift Theory: from bioinformatics to ML theory v15 NeurIPS 2026 / ICML 2027 chapter — Seungho Cook · 2026-04-25

DIAL (Direction-Invariant AUC Leakage) is a single-number post-hoc diagnostic that fires only when a linear batch correction leaves residual class-conditional shift inside the label subspace. Theorem 2 shows DIAL is independent (up to finite-sample noise) of pure covariate, pure label, and orthogonal-conditional shifts. This page summarises the theoretical and empirical artefacts of v15.

D. Foundation-model scaling (negative result)

Encoder parameter count from 2K → 5.4M. No monotonic relationship between scale and DIAL — larger encoders preserve label-aligned shift.

E. Cross-domain reproducibility (4/4 domains)

Same flip mechanism, four domain shapes (vision-PACS, NLP-Amazon, biology-THCA, clinical-MIMIC). Naive ComBat does not fix any of them.

F. Theory tree — connections to existing frameworks

Six adjacent theoretical traditions; each links to v15_theoretical_connections.md.

Covariate shift (Shimodaira, KLIEP)

KLIEP cannot fix DIAL > 0; re-weighting acts on p(x), not p(x|y).

H-divergence (Ben-David 2010)

DIAL is a computable surrogate under linear T and Gaussian conditionals. TODO Seungho: tightness.

Information bottleneck (Tishby)

DIAL > 0 ⇔ correction destroyed Δᶜᵒⁿᵈ_∥ bits of Y while preserving B⊥.

IRM (Arjovsky 2019)

DIAL is the post-hoc auditable form of IRM's invariance gap.

Wasserstein OT (Courty 2017)

10⁴× cheaper than JDOT with the same asymptotic target.

Leakage (Kaufman 2012, v5.2 audit)

Pooled-fit ComBat on class-imbalanced cohorts induces Δᶜᵒⁿᵈ_∥ > 0; DIAL detects it. v5.2 LODO fixes it.
