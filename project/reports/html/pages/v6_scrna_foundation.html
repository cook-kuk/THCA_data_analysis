G · multi-scale DIAL hierarchy

Bulk → cell-type → substate → foundation-model embeddingv5.1 bulk seeded

The hypothesis is monotone: each finer (or each more pre-trained) representation reduces residual cohort signal that ComBat misaligns. Bar 1 (bulk, v5.1 THCA LogReg_l2) is fixed at DIAL 0.494; bars 2-3 (per-cell-type and per-substate pseudobulk) populate after the Wave 1 download lands; bars 4-5 (scGPT and VEGA embeddings) populate after Wave 2.

Critical caveat (audit F11). The Wave 1 cellular bars (per-cell-type pseudobulk DIAL = 0.000 and per-substate pseudobulk DIAL = 0.000) measure a different question than the bulk bar. GSE184362 (Pu 2021), the only thyroid scRNA dataset already on disk, does not publish per-patient BRAF/RAS mutation status; the cellular Y labels are therefore malignant vs non-malignant and sub-cluster identity, not BRAF vs RAS. Furthermore, pyComBat raised single-sample batches under LODO on pseudobulk, so a per-batch z-score was used as the batch-correction surrogate (not ComBat). The two zeros are therefore sanity baselines on a different question, not a cellular reproduction of the bulk THCA flip. A genuine cellular replication is gated on (a) audit-fix F12 (switching to a BRAF/RAS-typed thyroid scRNA dataset such as GSE193581 / GSE154763 / GSE150430 if one provides the metadata) or (b) the prospective SNUBH cohort.
H · BRAF-like-B mechanism story

Tying single-cell substates to the v8 BUHMBOX mechanism and the v11 near-miss modules.

Pu et al. (2021) report a "BRAF-like-B" malignant substate that is transcriptomically intermediate between classical BRAF-V600E papillary tumours and RAS-mutant follicular tumours. Our v8 mechanism (cohort-disjoint PC1, KS p < 1e-40 within each subtype) and v11 near-miss modules (M006 PD-L1/PD-1 checkpoint coherence, M010 wound-healing axis with PTPRF/ACVR1) suggest that a fraction of the THCA cross-cohort flip we observe in bulk is explained by differential admixture of BRAF-like-B cells across TCGA-THCA and GSE27155. The v6 GPU run will test three predictions: (i) scGPT cell-embedding DIAL is at least 50 % lower than bulk DIAL; (ii) GEARS-predicted KO of one of the eight druggable targets normalises BRAF-like-B back toward classical BRAF-A; (iii) CellOracle identifies a TF (likely an ETS-family or AP-1 factor) whose in-silico inhibition flips BRAF-like-B fate probability toward the normal thyrocyte attractor. None of these are claimed yet — they are the falsifiable predictions the GPU run will resolve.
