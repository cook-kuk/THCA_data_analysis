v13 executive brief

TROP2 ADC is the clearest immediate translational story. The rest of v13 is the routing logic.

v13 turns the thyroid subtype pipeline into a therapeutic decision surface. It distinguishes what can be acted on now, what should be biomarker-enriched, and what belongs in an emerging-modality program rather than a classic drug-repurposing pitch.

Why TROP2 leads

  • Surface antigen biology fits ADC better than small molecules.
  • The target is already clinically drugged outside thyroid cancer.
  • Two thyroid-relevant TROP2 trials are already visible in the public registry layer.
  • A BRAF-like plus TACSTD2-high strategy is a realistic biomarker-enrichment overlay rather than a de novo therapeutic build.
Go now

Emerging modalities

Better framed as PROTAC, siRNA, ASO, or modality-expansion programs than classic repurposing wins.

Do not oversell

  • The screen is similarity-based, not docking-validated.
  • ADMET in this run is proxy-based.
  • Tier A does not mean clinically validated in thyroid cancer.
  • The strongest numerical repurposing hits are not automatically the best thyroid-oncology story.
  • TACSTD2 is clinically strongest even though its small-molecule list is not.

Strategic readout

v13 is more valuable as a decision architecture than as a flat list of drug names. It routes each target into the right modality lane instead of forcing everything into small-molecule repurposing.

Next moves

  1. Promote the TROP2 thyroid brief into a standalone translational package.
  2. Build a wet-lab shortlist for CYP1B1.
  3. Keep PLEKHA6, B3GNT3, and PTPRE in modality-expansion framing.
