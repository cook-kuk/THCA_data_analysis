v8 · STAT-GEN RIGOR · REVIEWER SUPPLEMENT (v5.1-leak-conditional)

Methodological robustness supplement.

Six checks around the v5.1 THCA DIAL flip — context after v5.2 leak audit.

We stress-test the v5.1 finding along six axes: batch-correction estimator (ComBat-seq vs ComBat), effect-size scale (random-effects meta-analysis with Han m-values), feature space (gene vs 50-Hallmark pathway aggregation), differential-expression test (DESeq2 vs Welch t-test), classifier paradigm (classical vs quantum kernel/variational), and three Han-lab methods (FastRNA centering, BUHMBOX, LMM). In every direction the THCA-specific flip either reproduces, or dissolves in exactly the way the method predicts, with XGBoost providing a consistent null control. Reviewer objections are anticipated and neutralised; the v5.1 conclusion stands.

S1COMBAT-SEQ vs COMBAT

THCA DIAL survives the count-native estimator.

We re-ran the v5.1 LODO DIAL pipeline on THCA with raw STAR gene-counts and inmoose.pycombat_seq, followed by a cross-platform ComBat bridge to GSE27155 microarray intensities. For 4/5 classifiers |ΔDIAL| < 0.1. Random Forest shrinks (0.324 → 0.129) but still fails to recover above chance (AUC 0.372 < 0.5). The BRAF/RAS signal does not recover above chance under either estimator, so the v5.1 "flip cancer" conclusion survives.

S2META-ANALYSIS

Random-effects m-values isolate THCA as the only effect-cohort.

Treating per-cancer DIAL as the effect size with SE from Hanley-McNeil, we apply METASOFT-style random-effects meta-analysis (Han & Eskin 2011, 2012 AJHG). For 4/5 classifiers, Cochran's Q rejects homogeneity (p < 1e-16, I² > 97%) and the Han m-value concentrates on THCA (m_THCA ≈ 1.00; all other m < 0.3, most < 0.1). XGBoost, which never flipped in v5.1, returns Q=0.12, I²=0, and m ≈ 0.5 for every cohort — a clean null control that rules out a pipeline false-positive.

S3PATHWAY-LEVEL DIAL

Practical mitigation — or gene-level artefact?

The v5.1 gene-level flip vanishes under 50-pathway ssGSEA aggregation: pooled DIAL = 0.000 and AUCpost 0.90–0.98 for every classifier. Two explanations need to be distinguished: (a) aggregation-dilutes — some pathways still flip but pooling cancels; (b) coherent biology — no pathway flips, so the gene-level DIAL was a high-dimensional residual-batch artefact. We ran LODO DIAL once per Hallmark pathway to discriminate.

Figure S3 — DIAL at gene level (3 000 variance-top genes) vs pathway level (50 MSigDB Hallmark). All five classifiers collapse to DIAL=0.000 at pathway level.

No individual pathway is batch_entangled — the pooled DIAL=0.000 is not averaging artefact. The highest-DIAL pathway (Oxidative Phosphorylation, DIAL 0.085, AUC 0.897→0.813) is a real RAS-high vs BRAF-low signature (Landa et al. 2016 Cell), mildly over-corrected by ComBat but not flipped. Recommended paper framing: Table 1 should carry a pathway-level addendum — "gene-level DIAL = 0.494, pathway-level DIAL = 0.000, pathway-level AUC = 0.96 (LogReg_l2)".

S4DESEQ2 RECOMPUTATION

NB-GLM recapitulates all 8 druggable targets.

Under pydeseq2 v0.5.4 with design ~subtype on 351 TCGA-THCA samples (293 BRAF / 58 RAS) we find 6 283 significant genes (BH-FDR<0.05, |log2FC|>1) vs 2 773 from the prior Welch t-test — a 2.3× power gain driven mostly by mid-expression genes that t-test-on-TPM under-powers. All 8 druggable targets (TACSTD2, TMPRSS4, PLEKHA6, CYP1B1, LDLR, GABRB2, B3GNT3, PTPRE) retain padj ≤ 5.22e-83 with log2FC between +2.58 and +5.29.

S5QUANTUM CLASSIFIER DIAL

DIAL is paradigm-invariant on THCA.

To answer "is DIAL a linear-model artefact?" we add a third classifier paradigm — quantum — to the v5.1 tests. Per-cancer, n = 80 downsampled and PCA-reduced to 8 components, three paradigms run in LODO: classical SVC_RBF (baseline), QSVC (Qiskit-ML ZZFeatureMap kernel, reps=2), and VQC (PennyLane strongly-entangling 3-layer ansatz, 60 training iters).

THCA flips under VQC as well. THCA/VQC: AUCpre=0.673 → AUCpost=0.114, DIAL=0.386, batch_entangled. THCA/SVC_RBF reproduces the v5.1 classical flip: AUCpre=1.000 → AUCpost=0.071, DIAL=0.429. Combined with the v5.1 linear-regression and tree-ensemble flips, the direction-inversion is paradigm-invariant. All non-THCA SVC_RBF baselines (SKCM, LGG, LUAD, COAD) return DIAL=0 — specificity preserved.

Two quantum rows are fit pathologies, not flips. THCA/QSVC underfits at n=80 (the fidelity kernel does not induce a THCA-discriminative classifier; AUCpre≈0.55). LGG/QSVC sign-inverts (AUCpre≈AUCpost≈0.18), an SVM label-encoding artefact — VQC and SVC_RBF on the same LGG folds both exceed AUC 0.92.

v8.1 backfill — LUAD/COAD QSVC+VQC complete. All four originally-skipped cells return DIAL = 0: LUAD/QSVC 0.000 (ambiguous), LUAD/VQC 0.000 (true_biology; AUCpre=0.30→AUCpost=0.71 — biology recovered post-ComBat), COAD/QSVC 0.002 (no_signal), COAD/VQC 0.000 (true_biology; AUC 0.52→0.90). Specificity holds across the complete 5 × 3 grid: only THCA flips, and only under SVC_RBF + VQC paradigms. The full table is below.

S6HANLAB METHODS

FastRNA centering, BUHMBOX, LMM — three Han-lab methods converge.

Three independent Han-lab-inspired analyses confirm that the THCA dominant axis is platform, and that simple alternatives to ComBat either recover the BRAF/RAS signal or clarify the biology.

See also: v11 Novel Pathway Modules — WGCNA and Leiden hierarchical decomposition reproduce the cohort-dominates-eigengene pattern below at the modular level (median |r_cohort| = 0.92 across 21 modules), localising the S6B BUHMBOX signal onto specific co-expression programs and flagging 5 near-miss novel markers.

S6A — FastRNA-style per-cohort centering

Subtracting each sample's cohort mean (Lee & Han 2022 AJHG) and running v5.1 LODO without ComBat collapses DIAL from 0.331 to 0.000 across all five classifiers (ΔDIAL ≈ +0.33), with linear-model AUC staying at 0.95–0.99 with correct polarity. ComBat over-shrinks toward a batch prior dominated by the large TCGA arm; centering avoids this.

Figure S6A — Per-classifier DIAL under v5.1 ComBat (purple) vs FastRNA-style cohort-mean centering (green). Centering eliminates the flip.

S6B — BUHMBOX-style PC1 KS test

For each subtype, a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test on PC1 scores between TCGA-THCA and GSE27155 returns KS = 1.00 with p = 1.3 × 10⁻⁴⁰ (BRAF) and 3.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ (RAS). Both flagged hidden_heterogeneity: within a single clinical subtype, PC1 is perfectly disjoint by cohort. This is the platform-axis signature that motivates the flip under linear correction methods.

S6C — LMM with cohort random effect

Per-gene expression ~ subtype + (1|cohort) (statsmodels MixedLM, REML, L-BFGS) vs naive OLS, BH-FDR on 3 000 variance-top genes (Sul et al. 2018). The LMM flags 2 201 genes vs the OLS 2 152: 491 OLS hits are lost_with_LMM and 540 are newly gained_with_LMM. The LMM rescues more genes than it drops — consistent with S6A: once cohort is modelled, BRAF/RAS gets cleaner, not weaker.

Caveat · v8 → v8.1 resolution log

These analyses are reviewer-facing robustness checks, not a replacement for prospective cohort validation. S1's ComBat-seq run uses a hybrid (ComBat-seq on TCGA counts then cross-platform ComBat to microarray) because the THCA public-data landscape has exactly one RNA-seq cohort paired with one microarray cohort. v8.1 resolved the pseudo-count DESeq2 (S4) with raw GDC STAR counts — 95.7 % pseudo-raw concordance, 83.4 % GSE27155 sign agreement; and the 3 000-gene LMM subset (S6C) with a full 11 710-gene LMM — honest biomarker survival 88.6 %. A 4-cohort LODO extension (GSE33630 / GSE29265) was attempted but cannot be run: neither cohort carries the joint BRAF-and-RAS phenotype annotation required for v5.1's binary task, so 2-fold LODO remains the ceiling. S5 quantum ran LUAD/COAD in v8.1 after the original 22-min budget skip. All v5.1 conclusions remain computational; prospective cohort validation remains a future requirement.
