## Required pre-deadline items

1. **OpenReview profile** — author must have an OpenReview profile
   when submitting abstract (May 4). Register at
   https://openreview.net/signup if not already done.
2. **Anonymous code repo URL** — needed before paper deadline (May 6).
   Upload `submission/v15_neurips/supplementary/anonymous_code.zip` to
   https://anonymous.4open.science.
3. **Final compile** — recompile `v15_neurips_SUBMIT.tex` after
   replacing the placeholder URL with the actual anonymous URL.
4. **Submit**: abstract by May 4 AOE, full paper + supplementary by
   May 6 AOE, via OpenReview.

## NeurIPS 2026 abstract+paper sequence

NeurIPS 2026 uses the standard 2-stage submission:
- **Phase 1 (May 4)**: title + abstract + authors + co-authors registered
  on OpenReview. Once submitted, no new abstracts can be added.
- **Phase 2 (May 6)**: full PDF + supplementary uploaded against the
  Phase-1 abstract record.

Authors who miss Phase 1 cannot submit Phase 2.

## Consequence for sprint timing

The 2026 schedule is **tighter than 2025** (May 11 / May 15) by 7 days.
Build buffer accordingly:
- May 1–3: final read-through + anonymous repo upload
- May 4 morning: register abstract
- May 5: final compile pass
- May 6 morning: submit full paper

If author is unavailable on those calendar days, submit earlier; do
not push to deadline-day.
