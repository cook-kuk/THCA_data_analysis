BOOST sprint validation panels (Fig. 6E–G)

Three reviewer-anticipated robustness layers added in v4 manuscript: Decision Curve Analysis (8-gene LogReg dominant at all 91 thresholds 0.05–0.95), 4-cohort random-effects meta-analysis (pooled AUC 0.980, 95% CI 0.869–0.997, I² = 0%, all 4 cohorts AUC > 0.96), and 9-strata subgroup forest (7/9 strata AUC ≥ 0.85; Stage III/IV peaks at 0.996). Underlying tables at results/v17_boost/.

Fig 6E — Decision Curve Analysis

8-gene LogReg vs BRAF V600E vs treat-all vs treat-none. Net benefit (Vickers–Elkin 2006) across thresholds 0.05–0.95 (n = 91). 8-gene strategy is dominant at all 91 evaluated thresholds — the strongest possible DCA result.

Fig 6F — 4-cohort meta forest

Random-effects logit-AUC pooling across GSE27155 (n=99), GSE29265 (n=49), GSE33630 (n=105), GSE76039 (n=37). Pooled AUC = 0.980 (95% CI 0.869–0.997), Cochran's Q p = 1.0, I² = 0%, all 4 cohorts AUC > 0.96.

Fig 6G — 9-strata subgroup forest

Per-stratum AUC + bootstrap 95% CI across Age (<45, 45–65), Sex, Stage (I/II, III/IV), Histology (cPTC, FVPTC), and Overall. 7/9 strata exceed AUC 0.85; Stage III/IV peaks at 0.996 (the most clinically high-risk and the strongest signal).
