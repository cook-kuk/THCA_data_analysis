NEW Section — §R8 TERT 4-group survival

Added in full (~280 words): > The cBioPortal thca_tcga_pub mirror (TCGA Cell 2014 publication MAF) contains Sanger-validated TERT promoter calls (chr5:1295228 C228T, chr5:1295250 C250T) for 36 of 504 TCGA-THCA patients (7.1 %).
