## Slot 2 — BRAF/RAS orthogonality framing (R4 + Discussion)

**Before (v1)**
> The DM1/DM2 axis is **statistically orthogonal** to BRAF/RAS mutation status
> (Result 4 title: "The DM1/DM2 axis is statistically orthogonal to BRAF/RAS mutation")

**After (v2)**
> The DM1/DM2 axis is **statistically distinct from BRAF/RAS mutation status** — it captures sub-axis information that mutation alone cannot (Spearman ρ = 0.49 with the canonical BRAF/RAS dichotomy; substantial overlap but not redundancy).
> (Result 4 title: "The DM1/DM2 axis is statistically distinct from BRAF/RAS mutation")

**Rationale**: Strict orthogonality (ρ = 0) is a strong claim and ρ = 0.49 is moderate, not orthogonal. "Statistically distinct" + explicit ρ value is honest and forecloses reviewer attacks of the form "ρ = 0.49 ≠ orthogonal".

## NEW Section — §R8 TERT 4-group survival

**Added in full** (~280 words):
> The cBioPortal `thca_tcga_pub` mirror (TCGA Cell 2014 publication MAF) contains Sanger-validated TERT promoter calls (chr5:1295228 C228T, chr5:1295250 C250T) for **36 of 504 TCGA-THCA patients (7.1 %)**. A Liu–Xing four-group stratification — BRAF only (n = 250), RAS only (n = 48), TERT⁺ (n = 36), triple-negative (n = 170) — yields **logrank p = 3.78×10⁻⁵** for overall survival. Restricting to the TERT axis alone, TERT⁺ event rate is 16.7 % (6/36) versus 2.1 % (10/468) for wildtype, **logrank p = 4.92×10⁻⁶**. The TERT axis is independent of DM1/DM2 (Fisher exact p = 0.65 within DM-clustered subcohort, n_TERT⁺ overlap = 5, underpowered)…

**Rationale**: Integrates Liu–Xing 4-genotype framework as a separate stratification axis. Makes the paper a four-layer stratification claim rather than a two-layer claim, with stronger survival evidence than DM1/DM2 alone could carry given TCGA-THCA's low event count.
