# Paper quality upgrade pack

## 1. Core thesis

The paper is strongest when it is framed as a **selection-layer paper**, not a platform paper.

- What it does: separate a heterogeneous thyroid cancer population into a compact, decision-useful differentiation axis.
- What it proves well: the axis is reproducible, clinically relevant, cross-cohort portable, and mechanistically coherent.
- What it does not prove: upstream causality for methylation silencing, or a replacement for existing driver biology.

## 2. Claim ladder

1. **Discovery claim:** the 8-gene panel resolves a DM1 / DM2 axis in PTC.
2. **Clinical claim:** the axis aligns with aggressiveness and survival risk.
3. **Portability claim:** the axis holds across TCGA, Korean, and external single-cell cohorts.
4. **Mechanism claim:** fusion-linked and methylation-linked silencing are consistent with the axis.
5. **Boundary claim:** the mechanism is supportive and correlative, not causal proof.

## 3. Figure flow that reads best

### Main text

1. **Figure 1** - define the panel and show that the compact panel is not a cherry-picked artifact.
2. **Figure 2** - show that the axis matters clinically.
3. **Figure 3** - show that the signal is thyrocyte-intrinsic in single-cell data.
4. **Figure 4** - show that the axis is not just BRAF/TERT status.
5. **Figure 5** - show portability in Korean validation and FFPE.
6. **Figure 6** - show pooled survival risk.
7. **Figure 7** - show fusion-driven mechanism support.
8. **Figure 8** - show epigenetic silencing support.

### Supplementary

- move technical stress tests, calibration caveats, and reserve extensions to supplement
- keep the main figures clean and claim-driven

## 4. Reviewer attack map

| attack | best answer location | what to emphasize |
|---|---|---|
| "You excluded drivers" | Q1 / Fig 1 | drivers are included in TIERA67, but the deployable panel comes from the differentiation sub-category |
| "You cherry-picked 8 genes" | Q2 / Q13 | the 8-gene panel behaves like a compact readout of the canonical axis, not a lucky subset |
| "BRAF transcript should explain this" | Q4 / Fig 1 | transcript is not mutation; the panel captures a different axis |
| "Fusion missingness breaks the result" | Q5 / Fig 7 | missingness is MAR and sensitivity analyses retain the OR |
| "Mechanism is not proven" | Q9 / Fig 8 | explicitly correlative, with a clear boundary |
| "Immune artifact" | Q10 / supp deconvolution | residualization retains effect; single-cell confirms thyrocyte intrinsic signal |
| "External Korean cohort breaks the classifier" | Q8 / Q11 | calibration mismatch is real and documented; portable score form fixes the direction |
| "Sub-B is just clustering noise" | Q12 / Q14 | silhouette support, reproducibility, and two-axis convergence explain it |

## 5. What to move out of main text

Keep the main text clean by moving the following to supplement or reviewer reserve:

- method-comparison details
- calibration mismatch diagnostics
- spatial caveats and counterexamples
- reserve mechanism extensions
- any figure that answers a niche reviewer question rather than advancing the main claim

## 6. What makes the paper better

The paper gets stronger when it does less:

- fewer claims
- tighter figure sequence
- more explicit boundary language
- more visible cross-cohort validation
- clearer separation of discovery, validation, and mechanism
