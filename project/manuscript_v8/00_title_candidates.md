# Title Candidates

## Reference (v4 baseline, NOT recommended)

> "An 8-gene RAI-responsiveness biomarker reveals a young-onset fusion-driven epigenetically-silenced actionable subtype within BRAF/RAS-negative papillary thyroid carcinoma"

- **Char count:** 174 (over by ~50)
- **Issues:** four adjectives stacked ("young-onset fusion-driven epigenetically-silenced actionable") = title overload; readers skim past; Cell Press reviewers will flag.

## ★ Candidate 1 — Mechanism-forward

> **"Fusion-driven, epigenetically silenced dark matter in BRAF/RAS-negative papillary thyroid carcinoma"**

| Aspect | Note |
|---|---|
| **Char count** | 99 |
| **Hook** | Dual-mechanism (fusion + methylation) in one phrase; "dark matter" cites Xing 2014 NEJM |
| **Foregrounds** | R3-F4 (76.8% fusion+) + R5-2 (TPO d=2.30 hypermethylation) — Tier-1 paper-defining findings |
| **Misses** | No panel mention; no actionability signal upfront |
| **Venue fit** | Cell Reports Medicine ★★★ (Cell Press loves mechanism-forward); Nat Commun ★★★ |
| **Pros** | • Cell-Press style brevity • Both mechanism layers in title • "Dark matter" is the Xing 2014 framing the reviewers will know • Easiest abstract entry |
| **Cons** | • "Dark matter" requires 1-line abstract definition • Translational reader may not see clinical signal in title • "Epigenetically silenced" depends on reader knowing differentiation context |

## Candidate 2 — Translational-forward

> **"An 8-gene RAI panel enables reflex fusion testing and re-induction therapy in BRAF/RAS-negative thyroid cancer"**

| Aspect | Note |
|---|---|
| **Char count** | 110 |
| **Hook** | Clinical algorithm (DM1+ → fusion NGS + HMA/RAI re-induction); selpercatinib-eligible 48/1000 PTC implied |
| **Foregrounds** | R4-3 (DM1 captures 81.8% RET+) + R5-2 → HMA rationale |
| **Misses** | Dark-matter framing dropped; epigenetic mechanism not visible |
| **Venue fit** | Cell Reports Medicine ★★★ (matches "actionable" emphasis); JCO Precision Oncology ★★★; npj Precision Oncology ★★ |
| **Pros** | • Most actionable title — clinician-friendly • Panel + therapy stated upfront • Matches Cell Rep Med "Brief Report" / "Article" tone of clinical translation |
| **Cons** | • "Re-induction therapy" is forward-looking (rationale, not trial) — careful editorial framing needed in abstract • Reads more like biomarker paper than mechanism paper • Loses the discovery / paradigm-shift signal |

## Candidate 3 — Discovery-forward

> **"An 8-gene panel sub-stratifies BRAF/RAS-negative thyroid cancer into fusion-driven and immune-overlap subtypes"**

| Aspect | Note |
|---|---|
| **Char count** | 109 |
| **Hook** | DM1 vs DM2 + DM1 sub-A vs sub-B — heterogeneity discovery foregrounded |
| **Foregrounds** | DM1/DM2 split + R4-2 fusion+/fusion- heterogeneity + Pillar 5 autoimmune-PTC layer |
| **Misses** | Epigenetic layer not visible; HR/clinical aggressiveness not signaled |
| **Venue fit** | Cell Reports Medicine ★★; Genome Medicine ★★★; npj Precision Oncology ★★★ |
| **Pros** | • Honest about R4-2 fusion+/− heterogeneity finding (not all DM1 is the same) • "Immune-overlap" foreshadows Pillar 5 (Hashimoto-DM2 axis + GSE286332) • Discovery-style flat tone |
| **Cons** | • "Sub-stratifies" reads as biomarker, not mechanism • Doesn't capture the meta HR 2.53 clinical aggressiveness signal • Two-axis discovery may dilute the headline DM1 fusion+epigenetic story |

## ★★★ Hybrid Candidate (web Claude 권장, FINAL)

> **"An 8-gene panel reveals fusion-driven, epigenetically silenced dark matter in BRAF/RAS-negative thyroid cancer"**

| Aspect | Note |
|---|---|
| **Char count** | 108 |
| **Hook** | Cand 1 mechanism layer + Cand 2 panel mention 합성. "Reveals" = Cell Press discovery verb |
| **Foregrounds** | 8-gene panel (clinically interpretable) + dual-mechanism (fusion + epigenetic) + "dark matter" Xing 2014 cite |
| **Venue ladder cover** | Cell Rep Med ★★★ + Nat Comm stretch ★★★ + JCI Insight ★★★ + npj Prec Onco ★★★ — **모든 ladder 단계 fit** |
| **Pros** | • Cand 1 의 약점 (no panel mention) 보완 • Cand 2 의 약점 (no mechanism) 보완 • 108 chars Cell Rep Med 표준 • discovery verb 로 paradigm-shift 신호 |
| **Cons** | • "Reveals" 가 약간 generic — alternative: **"unmasks", "resolves", "defines"** (본인 voice 선택) • 길이 108 vs Cand 1 의 99 — 미세하게 길지만 panel 명시 가치가 더 큼 |

**Verb 대안 (본인 선택):**
- "**reveals**" — discovery, 가장 안전 (default)
- "**unmasks**" — paradigm-shift 강조 (Nat Comm reach 시 추천)
- "**resolves**" — heterogeneity sub-stratification 강조 (JCI Insight 시 추천)
- "**defines**" — flat statement 톤 (npj 시 추천)
