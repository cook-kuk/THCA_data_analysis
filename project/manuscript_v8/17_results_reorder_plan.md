# Results reorder plan

## Preferred reading order

1. **2.1 Discovery** - the 8-gene panel defines the DM1/DM2 axis.
2. **2.2 Clinical relevance** - the axis predicts aggressiveness and survival.
3. **2.5 Validation / portability** - the axis is reproducible in Korean bulk, single-cell, and FFPE settings.
4. **2.3 Mechanism I** - fusion enrichment and reflex testing.
5. **2.4a Mechanism II** - promoter hypermethylation and epigenetic silencing.
6. **2.4b Boundary case** - sub-B immune-overlap state, explicitly reserved for Paper 2.

## Why this is stronger

- The reader sees the core discovery before the mechanism layers.
- Clinical utility appears before mechanistic detail, which is easier for broad review panels.
- Validation is placed before the deeper mechanism, so the paper feels transportable rather than dataset-bound.
- The Paper 2 boundary is preserved at the end instead of interrupting the core story.

## What should be trimmed from the main flow

- overlong calibration caveats
- niche stress tests
- reserve-only spatial diagnostics
- method-comparison detail

## What should remain in the main flow

- the compact selection-layer claim
- survival / clinical relevance
- external validation
- fusion mechanism
- epigenetic mechanism
- explicit limitation boundary
